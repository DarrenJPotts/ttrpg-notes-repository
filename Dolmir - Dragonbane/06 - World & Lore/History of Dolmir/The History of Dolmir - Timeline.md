## Timeline

# Age of Dreams
*Duration: 1,627 years*
## Year 0
**O Birth of the Eldar**
The Eldar emerge from star, root, and stone.
## Year 590
**O Rise of the Jotun-born**
The Jotun-born rise to power across the Stormlands.

**O The First Long Night begins**
Darkness descends upon the known world.

**O Rise of the Beast-kin**
The beast-kin emerge amid the chaos of the Long Night.

## Year 590 – 1,627
*Over 1,037 years*

**O Ifund of Rinan Blood**
Ifund rises from the Titans.

**O Battle of the First Eldar Alliance**
The Eldar Alliance meets the forces of darkness in open war.
Ifund is slain. Glumthang, Wyrm of the Stormlands, falls.

------
# Age of Separation
*Duration 522 years*

## Year 1628
**O The Arrival of Man**
Man arrives on the Eastern shores of Dolmir

**O Formation of the Tribes of Man**
The kin of Man spread throughout the Eastern lands, forming tribes and eventually kingdoms.

## Year 1754 
**O The Second Long Night begins**
Once more the Jotun-born rose from the West, claiming ruin the Mittlands and Heartlands

**O The kin of Man joins the Alliance of Eldar**
The Second Long Night brought the near extinction of Man and Eldar, prompting them to join the Alliance.

## Year 1763
**O The Faith of the One God forms**
With the raging war of the Second Long Night, Man turned to a new god as their losses grew.

## Year 1884 - 2133
**O The Umbarian Line begins**
Ordained by the First Father of the One Faith, the Umbarian throne units the Kingdoms of Man under one rule of King Aldric I Umbar The Unifier. 

**O The death of the First King**
The Second Long Night took many lives, of Eldar and man alike, including the life of King Aldric I Umbar at the age of 67.

**O The second king of Umbar**
King Alric I Umbar's son, Othric I, sits upon the throne of the Heartlands.

**O The Kin of Man withdraw from the Alliance**
Man's lives are short-lived compared to the Eldar, a driving point of the Faith of the One God as the short lives of man were cut shorter and their losses greater. Under their guidance, King Othric I Umbar withdraws from the Alliance and unknowingly begins the steps towards the Rite of Purification.

**O The Kingswood is claimed**
King Othric I Umbar claims the land of the Kingswood, the home of the Great Wood of the Heartlands, expelling the Eldar within it back to the Mittlands.

**O The First Blight begins**
A sickness spreads throughout the Heartlands, causing famine and plague, devastating the lands of man. The Kingswood, wilting with it.

**O The Death of the Second King**
The second Umbarian King dies, the blight taking him at the age of 60 and Corvin The Faithful takes the throne.

**O The Silence of the Eldar**
The Eldar's long life begins to fade becoming mortal and magic begins to fade.

**O The Rite of Purification**
Under King Corvin II Umbar, the Faith of the One God begins the **Rite of Purification**. A religious war and persecution of those deemed heretical and the cause of the Blight, The Eldar and men of the Ordo Magi, arcanists taught the ways of the Eldar. 

**O The Eldar withdraw from the Heartlands**
The Eldar, persecuted and mortal, flee from the Heartlands to their Great Woods in the Mittlands. Some kin of Man follow and take refuge in the Stormlands, eventually becoming known as the Nomad Tribes of the Stormlands. 

**O Records of the first blight disappear**
The Faith of the One God, scrub the lands of records of the first blight, its start and its end.
## Year 2134 
**O The Third King dies**
King Corvin I Umbar, The Faithful, passes in his sleep at the age of 53. The rumor of the curse of the Umbar line begins. King Haldric Umbar, The Cursed, beings his reign.

## Year 2140 - 2143
**O Haldar foresees the future**
King Haldric's son, Haldar Umbar spends his childhood with night terrors of a woman saint, who will shatter the current line of Umbar. His father becoming increasingly paranoid.

**O The Story of the Lady Saint**
Tales of the 'divine will' of the One God spread through the land. Lady Odellia, shows a new way for Eldar and the kin of Man to unite. 

## Year 2144 - 2149
**O Death of the Paranoid King**
King Haldirc Umbar, The Cursed, dies. His paranoia and isolationism led to the early loss of his life at 46 years of age. The child King rises in his place, King Haldar the Dreamer. 

**O King Haldar the Dreamer chooses a new path**
The child king chooses a different path to that of his forbearers, believing in the tales of the Lady Saint.  

**O Capture and Execution of the Lady Saint**
Despite the King's belief, the current leaders of the Faith of the One God, capture and execute by fire and stake Lady Odellia The Saint in the depths of the Vale. 

## Year 2150
**O King Haldar reforms the Faith**
King Haldar the Dreamer upon learning of the death of Lady Odellia, excommunicates the current leaders of the faith and naming Lady Odellia an official Saint of Dolmir. She becomes the forefront of the new reformation of the faith. 

# The Third Age 
*Duration unknown*

## Year 2151 - 2168
**O King Haldar begins the new age**
Under the rule of King Haldar the Dreamer, a new age begins. One of unity and the repairing of relations between Eldar and Man. 

**O King Haldar bears two sons**
The new line of Umbar is blessed with Twin sons, Vaelor and Halthor, at the Age of 21. 

## Year 2169 
**O The death of the Dreamer**
King Haldar the Dreamer dies at the age of 39 under mysterious circumstances.

**O The story begins**
The current campaign begins during the start of Spring, in the town of Ashveil.
