During the First Age of Dolmir, [The Age of Dreams] The [Eldari] walked the lands under the domain of the Old Gods, spirits and titans whom formed Dolmir. The spirits begot the Great Woods and roots that flow through the living form of Dolmir while the Titans begot the mountains, the stone and those that dwell beneath. 

# The Old Gods
## The Eldar Spirits
The Old Gods are said to he the creators of the world of Dolmir. Their forms appearing as the Great Woods, large trees carved with their likeness. According to the ballads and tales of the [Elveth'an], they are beings of sentience, of the natural order and laws of the world who breathe life and 'sentience' into new beings. 

## The Titans
[Titans], also known as "the pillars of Dolmir", the Ancients, or The Stone are powerful primordial entities that dwell deep beneath the earth. They are massive creatures of stone and metal who sing in the Stone, shaping it. The Titans begot the mountains with their own forms, creating living mountains and earth that would form [those whom dwell beneath], the [Dwarves] and eventually [Jotun-born].
