Dolmir has seen only one Blight, set in the year 1884 - 2133. It is unsure of the exact date or origins of the Blight. The Order of the One God teaches it's existence as fiction, based on the heretics of the past, the Jotun-born. While the Eldar races record it's existence with more detail, stating it began after the death of Sehan'nain in her conclave at the hands of humans seeking her power. 

_"Darkness filled the skies, clawed beast and winged demons; So trod the Men of the East; Seekers of her power; Defilers, raiders and murderers."  - An extract of an Eldar Ambassador_

 The truth of the Blight is that Alexios Valon and his companions sought the power of Sehan'nain as a means to win the Second Long Night, to save Dolmir, but instead wrought it's destruction. Creatures and kin of all kind turned to corrupted spawn of themselves.
