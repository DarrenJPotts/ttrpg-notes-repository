*Ce qui est construit dure*

The Vaeloraine name has existed long before the Winetrade existed, long before Alverion married Serelinne and begot Elenessa. The name itself, the tree has existed as long as the first men walked onto Dolmir, an offshoot of what would be come house Enoai. 

The Enoai line had also begot the Beldam Tribes of Karvosti and as such have long but distant relations although in present time it would be minimal as the tree's branched out. 
However the blood may have thinned, one part remains true of all the lines descended from the Enoai clans is their devotion to their main lines goal, "Strength is only obtained through Unity under one roof". The Beldam tribes became closely integrated with the Eldar kin of the Mittlands, leaving their homes on the plains of Karvosti to travel deep within the Mittlands while what would become the Vaeloraine line embraced their mission many years later, during that of the rise of the Order of the One God.

Truth be told of the Vaeloraine, the name is fairly new compared to many of the Houses and Bloodlines of the Heartlands as it was only adopted a few generations ago, its origin based within the Vale and the Eldar Elve'than word for radiant light, 'Áine'. A name gifted upon the would-be head of the house by the Eldar they had saved. 

The Vaeloraine's themselves were not only of the blood of the original members but of a few who had joined the clandestine order, the underground network that spanned the reaches of the Heartlands. They were a shadow resistance to the Order while the fellowship of the Lady Saint moved against the Church, operating as intelligence, smugglers and sometimes a vanguard of military. 

#### The Last Vaeloraines
Serelinne Vaerethiel Vaeloriane and Alverion Theren Vaeloriane found each other in Alverion's youth. Serelinne was the daughter of a more devout family of the Order, ones that would assist as informants against their own neighbours if suspected of habouring Eldar. Serelinne did not believe in such but to her dismay that was her family and as such still loved them, even with their dark deeds.
Alverion on the other hand was a true Vaeloraine, son of his father in both blood and goal, acting apart of the shadow resistance, smuggler but never violent and in one such occasion was spotted by Serelinne whilst he was smuggling Eldar kin through her village. He suspected doom but was instead granted freedom, as she smiled and turned her eye. It would be a year before he came back and found her, absconding with her away from her family. 

It would be this, that set upon the doom of the Vaeloraine resistance. Not this event in particular but it would lead a domino effect upon the clandestine order as over the next few years any and all of the shadow resistance fell, to what or who it is unknown but it was a dark period for Alverion and Sereline.... as the last living members they walked through towns and villages where once friends hung from branch, or their resting place marked with heads on pike. The worst being those set to rot within the small confines of the Gibbets, if starvation did not kill them, the crows would. 

Serelinne had inspired the best in Alverion, inspired peace, inspired love and the potential for family; eventually settling down in the Vale mountains where the future Ashveil would bear vineyards, settlers and homes. However that peace and love would break with the death of Auguste, Alverion's father, whom died tortured within a Gibbet. The fracturing heartbreak that this caused Alverion sent him on a path he had not yet taken, one of violence, of darkness. And that very path caused him to lose sight of what he loved, his wife and unbeknownst to him, his unborn daughter. The true last Vaeloraine, Elenessa.
