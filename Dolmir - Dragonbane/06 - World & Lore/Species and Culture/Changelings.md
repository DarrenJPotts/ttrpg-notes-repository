> *A creature born of the Hags of Dolmir, whose fate is guided by unknown forces. Raised amongst humans, hidden behind a human guise, whose true nature belongs neither to the world of men or the Hags.*

#### Background
*Born through the deals made, the Changeling is the spawn of a Hag to be replaced by those who give up their own, brought on by the influence of the Jotun.*

Those who make deals with the Hags of Dolmir are given one deal, a child for a child. Of course other exchanges might exist, but Hags will only show themselves to woman who are in need, such need that they are willing to give up their own. 

---
#### About Changelings
*Changelings grow up seemingly as ordinary children, but over time the terrible truth becomes difficult to hide.*

They are often quiet, observant and posses an unsettlingly trait that is hard to put a name on. Their skin may react strangely to the sunlight, they might tend to smell of the earth from which they came or when angered, their features stretch into something wilder. 

A Changeling is rarely driven by pure malice, rather, they suffer from a deep, incurable divide between the human love they were raised with and the self-isolation of being something *else*, not human or Eldar. 

Changelings often struggle to learn to speak and read, but possess a natural aptitude for working with animals. They frequently have trouble understanding relationships and find it difficult to form bonds of love with humans, rather they tend to grow from the emotions they experience, negative emotions cause drastic, more Jotun-born like features known as Dopplers, while positive emotions bring out more humanoid features. During their adolescence, their emotions heighten and act as a pivot point for the path their biology takes.

Often completely unaware of their true heritage, the instinctual self-isolation the Changeling to attempt to mimic the relationships they see around them, but because they do not fully comprehend the human mind, things often go awry; resulting in the opposite effect and forming a growing rift between them and the human/eldar they grow up with. This leaves their personality to be quiet malleable, often not in a good way.

#### Features and Skills

**Animal Empathy:** As difficult as it is for the Changeling to understand humans, it finds it easy to understand animals. To the unaware, it may even sometimes seem as though the Changeling can speak with animals. This does not necessarily mean that the Changeling is kind to them; on the contrary, there are many cases where they act more like tormentors.

**Borrowed Guise**: Once per day, the Changeling can take the form of another kin, such as an Elf, Human, Halfling or Dwarf. As a Doppler, they begin to take the form of more Jotun-born kin. However, the transformation is never entirely flawless: in sunlight, the shadow cast is revealing, displaying their truer form, and reflections still show the Changeling's true appearance. Furthermore, Changelings are poor at impersonation, retaining their own mannerisms, behaviour and way of expressing themselves.

However, if no other way out can be found, they can prove to be ferocious adversaries. When giving into the rage, they act as monsters*

##### Social Skills
**Truth Binder:** The unknown force of the Jotun-born blood prevents the Changeling from telling direct lies, forcing it to speak in half-truths and misleading metaphors. However, anyone breaking a verbal pact made with the Changeling is also affected and suffers a subtle curse, such as food tasing like ash or iron burning at the touch.
**Childhood Echo:** The Changeling intuitively reads human grief and guilt, using familiar childhood memories, gestures or stories to make attackers hesitate. In order to kill a defenceless Changeling, the attacker must successfully role a WIL with bane. 

##### Monster Attacks
**D6 ATTACK**

**1. Eerie Gaze** 
The Changeling fixes its gaze upon the nearest player character. Its eyes shift to a yellowish-green. The victim must succeed on a WIL check with disadvantage or become paralyzed (losing their next turn and suffering the Scared condition).

**2. Claws** 
The Changeling's fingertips extend into hardened claws that lash out at the player character who most recently attacked it (or the nearest person). The attack deals 1d10 slashing damage, and the target becomes restrained until they succeed on a STR check to break free.

**3. Vile Bite** 
The Changeling opens its abnormally large and grotesque maw to bite the nearest player character. The attack deals 2d6 piercing damage, and if the victim takes damage, they are infected with a disease with a potency of 10.

**4. Forceful Throw** 
The Changeling forcefully hurls an object (such as a stone) at the player character farthest away (up to a maximum range of 25 meters). The target takes 1d10 bludgeoning damage.

**5. Primal Strike** 
With superhuman strength hidden within a seemingly ordinary body, the Changeling strikes the nearest player character with clenched fists. The attack deals 2d8 bludgeoning damage and knocks the victim back 3 meters, leaving them prone.

**6. Jotun-born Blood**
Become Enraged. The Changeling's ancestral blood comes to the Changeling's aid. Loose objects fly toward the Changeling's enemies, tree branches lash out at them, and the wind whips at their cloaks (at the GM's discretion). All player characters take 2d6 bludgeoning damage and must succeed on a WIL check to avoid gaining the Scared condition.
