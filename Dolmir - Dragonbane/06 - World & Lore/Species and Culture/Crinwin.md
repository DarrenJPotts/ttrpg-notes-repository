One of the many mysterious creatures of [[Dolmir - Dragonbane/06 - World & Lore/Dolmir]], not fully known whether they exist or not, these are creatures of the tall mountains, the deep forests and underground. Speculated to be of the [[Jotun-born]] of the [[First Age]]. 
They are said to be the [[Jotun-born]] who were left behind after the [[Second Long War]] and have since over the ages lost their sentience, becoming beasts of the night.
