# House Hathor

**"Wealth Built on Stone and Vine"**

---

**Ruled by:** Lord [[Reindell Hathor]]

**Seat:** [[The Vale]], within [[Valehall]]

---

## History & Reputation

House Hathor built its wealth and status through trade—first mining the deep ore of the Vale mountains, then expanding into the wine trade. They export fine wines from the vineyards of [[House Vaeloraine]] and [[House Corvelle]], establishing themselves as merchants of renown throughout the realms.

Their seat is renowned for its fortifications, walls so impenetrable that few dare test them.

---

## Heraldry

**Symbol:** On blue field, a stone tower wreathed in grapevines. On white field, a crown above a tree, marked in red with the symbols of [[House Umbar]].
