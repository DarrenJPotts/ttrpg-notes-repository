The Veil exists as a metaphysical barrier that exists between the physical realm, known as the phenomenal realm and the noumenal realm, the Reverie. The **Reverie** itself being a realm of the spirit, of dreams and the bridge between death and the **distant shores** of beyond. 

The Veil is not an object—it cannot be touched or seen. It is not a physical barrier, but rather something similar to a opposite forces of the realms that repel each other, incompatible to exist together. It is a metaphor used by mortal scholars to explain the interactions between the two different realities.

#### The Reverie 
There is not much known about what lies beyond the first life and for the most part it differs widely between cultures, kin and belief. Those of the Faith of the One God believe what comes after lies within the judgement of the one. Dwarf kin believe they return to the stone while the Elves understand it as a re-joining with the spirits but none are quite right. 

Instead what lays beyond is the Reverie, the bridge between the Distant Shores and the phenomenal realm. A land of spirits, of dreams and the unconscious mind. When someone dies, their souls carries with it the weight of their life, their memories and dreams and discard them before sailing beyond. And as such, the Reverie beyond the Veil contains fragments of such.

However, it is no longer that simple, the Reverie has the potential to contain those that linger or worse, the Animist spirits whom were corrupted by the deeds of darker magics. Only made worse by the death of Sehan'nain.
