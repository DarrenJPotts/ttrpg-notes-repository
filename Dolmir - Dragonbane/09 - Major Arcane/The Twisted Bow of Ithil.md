The Twisted Bow of Ithil is a bone bow reinforced with intricate bindings of twisted wood and embellishments, tapering to a sharp edged point.

The Bow is a potent weapon against spirits and demons. They cannot bear its touch and its presence makes them deeply ill at ease.

When you wield the Twisted Bow as a weapon, you ignore magical protections and can harm spirits, demons, and insubstantial creatures.

---

The bow is mastercrafted and has the following spells and drawbacks:

✦ BLADED EDGE (can use the weapon in melee)

✦ ENCHANTED WEAPON power level 1 (dragon roll on 1–2)

✦ FARSHOT power level 3 (range 120 meter)

✦ UNBREAKABLE power level 2 (durability 24)

✦ SEEKING ARROW (attacks cannot suffer banes)

✦ MIND LEECH (each attack costs 1 WP)

✦ PERMANENCE

---

When you first plunge the Bow into a spirit or demon or blighted, your mind reels with strange visions. These tasks become known to you:

1/1 Impale a foul spirit with the Bow, and keep it skewered until it burns away to nothing.

0/1 Visit the elder tree from which the Bow was carved, and water its roots with your blood.

0/1 Confront the Pale Hunter and survive.

0/1 Complete the twin fragment of the Bow. The weapon of Anor.

0/1 Pierce your own hand through with the Bow, giving it a shard of your soul and reducing your max HP by 1d4+1.

When you complete a task, mark it off.

1/3

When you have marked 3 tasks, you unlock the mysteries of the Twisted Bow.

Choose one of the moves below; you can use that move as long as you wield the Bow. Each time you mark a task thereafter, gain another move.

#### Mysteries of the Twisted Spear

- **Whispers:** When you grip the shaft of the Twisted Spear, you can ask the GM, _"What spirits are active here?"_ and get an honest answer.
- **Promise of Doom:** When you hold the Twisted Spear and speak aloud an oath to destroy a spirit or demon, you do +2d4 damage against them and -4 damage to everything else. This lasts until you destroy them or forsake your oath. If you forsake your oath, mark a **Consequence**.
- **Roots of the Elder Tree:** When you stand with your feet firmly on the ground and strike a spirit, ghost, or demon, you can mark a **Consequence** to send your target coursing through you and into the depths of the earth, where it is bound fast.
- **Darkness Lingers:** When you strike down a spirit or demon, ask the GM for one of its moves and write it down; it is held in the Twisted Spear. When you use the stolen move, roll **CON**:
**On a dragon:** You do it, just like that.
**On a success:** You do it, but choose 1:
- The move is lost, bleeding away from the Spear
- Mark a **Consequence**

**On a failure:** The move manifests, but you lose control. Mark a **Consequence**.

**On a demon:** The Spear writhes with malice. The GM describes what happens. You likely mark a **Consequence**.

---

#### **Consequences of the Twisted Spear**

Each time you mark a Consequence, the bindings grow darker:

**⬜️ Deep Beneath**  
Deep beneath the elder tree from which the Spear was carved, something dark and terrible shifts and strains at the roots that bind it.

**⬜️ Dreams of the Bound**  
From now on, the first time you Make Camp each session, roll **WIS**:
**On a dragon:** Your dreams are unpleasant and claustrophobic, but your sleep is restful.
**On a success:** You dream of that which is bound by the elder tree from which the Spear was carved—take **disadvantage** on your next roll, but ask the GM what you learn.
**On a failure:** The thing bound by the tree sees you, too. You awake, unrested, and will get no rest this night.

**⬜️ Vision Overwhelming**  
You are overwhelmed by a vision of the thing bound by the Spear's tree, _its past and its terrible desires._

**⬜️ The Tree Cracks**  
The elder tree from which the Spear was carved shudders and cracks, still whole but wounded and weakened.

**⬜️ Lesser Evil Escapes**  
A lesser evil escapes from the roots of the tree. Ask the GM what is now loose in the world.

**⬜️ The Tree Sickens**  
The tree grows sick and weak, losing its leaves and sprouting only a few in spring.

**⬜️ The Tree Dies**  
The tree dies. Its prisoners escape. And the Spear loses all power.
