Book of the First Sinner
*implanted, magical, corrupting*

## What It Is
 A book of hardened dark leather sealed shut with a thin chain that looped around it. The book is heavier than it appears and when picked up, is cold to the touch.

Upon opening syllables of ineffable words appear, of pure thought and will and are emblazoned upon your soul and tongue by some force beyond mortal understanding. A gift, perhaps. Or a terrible, terrible affliction. 

The ineffable word's power thrums inside you, pulsing against the crude vessel of your flesh and blood. 

**The Words want to be spoken.** 

---
Secrets:
Sometimes, under stress or simply out of the blue, you are struck with shakes and seizures. And when the tremors pass and your senses return, you find that you have scratched strange symbols in the dirt or on the walls. Sometimes in your own blood.

---

## Speaking the Unutterable

When you *enter an ecstatic delirium and allow the Ineffable Words* to pour forth unbidden, roll **CON**:

**On a success:** You speak **Truth**. Name or describe something new and important about the current situation. Everyone present understands it as absolute fact, spoken in a language that bypasses their ears and reaches their minds directly. Pay 1 WP.

**On a failure:** The Words surge uncontrolled. Choose one:
- The Truth is cryptic, vague, incomplete
- You are overcome, collapsing in a full-body seizure, you are **helpless** for one round
- You draw unwanted attention

---

## Mastering the Words

When you spend a full day in ascetic contemplation of the incommunicable words within you, roll **WIS**:

**On a success:** You gain insight into the power within you. Mark 1 progression below.

**On a failure:** The Words resist your control. You gain a **bane** on your next attempt to contemplate the words.

0/3

When you make the last mark, you unlock the mysteries of the Unutterable Words and can Speak the Unutterable. Choose 1 Word that you have mastered and erase all marks above. Thereafter, when you make the last mark, choose another Word to master and erase all marks.

---

## The Four Mastered Words

### **Seal**
Name a portal, clasp, or seam in your presence. 

**Forward:** It seals shut and holds against any mundane attempt to open it. **Cost:** 1 WP

**Backward:** It is pried open or burst apart. **Cost:** 2 WP

---

### **Purify**
Name an instance of corruption, infection, poison, or taint in your presence.

**Forward:** The target is cleansed. A creature recovers 1d6 HP and loses one **condition** of your choice. **Cost:** 1 WP

**Backward:** The corruption grows violently. A creature takes 1d6 damage (ignores armor) and gains the **Poisoned** condition. **Cost:** 2 WP

---

### **Gather**
Name an unliving object in far range.

**Forward:** It is torn from its place and flies toward you. If the object weighs more than you or is secured by something that does, you are pulled toward it instead. **Cost:** 1 WP

**Backward:** It is flung away with violent force. It takes 2d6 damage if it strikes something hard. **Cost:** 2 WP

---

### **Empower**
Name a living creature or vessel for power in your presence.

**Forward:** The target surges with power. Choose: it heals 1d6 HP and gains **advantage** on its next roll, OR it gains **advantage** on a skill roll it's about to make. **Cost:** 1 WP

**Backward:** The target is drained. It takes 1d6 damage (ignores armor) and suffers **disadvantage** on its next roll. **Cost:** 2 WP

---

## Speak the Unutterable

*Requirement: You must have mastered at least one Word*

When you speak an Ineffable Word that you have mastered, roll **CON**:

**On a success:** The Word's power manifests fully as described. Pay the WP cost listed for that Word. You maintain control.

**On a failure:** The Word manifests, but choose one before paying the cost:
- You collapse into a full-body seizure; you are **helpless** for one round
- Mark a **Consequence** (see below)
- The effect amplifies beyond your control—every possible target in far range is affected

If the Words tear reality (GM discretion on how badly you failed), mark a **Consequence** automatically.

---

## Consequences of Power

Each time you mark a Consequence, the Words claim more of you. These are not merely mechanical—they represent the slow erasure of your humanity:

**⬜️ Marked by the Word**
Every magical being within miles recognizes you on sight as a bearer of the Words. They seek you out, drawn to your power like moths to flame.

**⬜️ Voice of Chaos**
Your voice becomes metallic, inhuman, wrong. All creatures understand you as though you spoke their native tongue. But you can never again use language to lie, deceive, or hide. **Truth is all you can speak.**

**⬜️ The Rift Opens**
Reality tears in your presence. Whenever you fail to control a Word (roll a 6-), strange effects manifest: weather turns violent, animals flee in terror, the boundary between worlds grows thin.

**⬜️ The Eternal Watcher**
An ancient being of Order has noticed you. It is patient, implacable, and will eventually find you to reprimand you for your reckless use of primordial power. It will arrive when you least expect it.

**⬜️ Overflow**
Your next successful use of a Word affects not just your intended target, but all possible targets in far range. You cannot control or limit the effect. When this consequence triggers, erase it and choose again on the next Consequence mark.

## GM Notes

- **Not for casual use.** The Words are dangerous. Every speaking carries risk—the player must genuinely choose between power and control.
- **The book wants something.** It does not think like a human. Consider what it desires: knowledge? Chaos? To return to its maker? To spread corruption?
- **Seizures are a sign.** Brief but frequent tremors show the Words pressing against the boundary of the bearer's mind. Use them to build unease.
- **Mastery is costly.** A day of contemplation is a day the party cannot travel, cannot defend themselves, cannot act. Make it matter.
- **Consequences are permanent.** Once marked, they reshape the bearer. The Words don't let go.
- **The Words are not the bearer's ally.** They tolerate the bearer, use the bearer. But their agenda is their own.
