Creating Vile Water To use a managaal for the purpose of creating Vile Water, the creature must be at least half full of corruption [Resolute/2]. Moreover, it takes a codex or manuscript which describes the actual procedure – sources of knowledge that can be found in Davokar’s dark ruins, or bought from cultists and sorcerers for the price of a small fortune. It takes a full day to slowly boil down the creature’s blood to the desired thickness; then, a Master Alche mist and a Master Artifact Crafter (can be one and the same individual) must roll a Cunning test. If both tests are successful, ten drops of Vile Water are created if the managaal was an adult, or five if it was a spawn.
