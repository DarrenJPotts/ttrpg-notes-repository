## Artifact: Vile Water

Vile Water consists of pure corruption, like an opposite **Water of the Dusk** (page 186 in the Core Rulebook). It looks like black mercury and gives off dark fumes of corruption if not contained in a vial of glass, crystal, or similar material.

### Binding

The only way to bind to this artifact is to accept **1 point of permanent corruption**.

## Powers

### Revelations of the Night

The liquid can be dripped on living tissue. Each drop grants **1D12 Experience**, to be used however the receiver wishes, at the cost of **1 permanent corruption**.

When all drops are used, the vial is empty and the artifact spent.

- **Action:** Active
- **Corruption:** 1 permanent corruption per drop

### Black Synergy

He who bows before the supremacy of the Dark can use its blackness to enhance the powers of Sorcery. **Once per scene**, the synergy can add **+1 tier to the effect die**.

- **Action:** Reactive
- **Corruption:** None

### Black-Light

In the hands of one who has accepted Darkness, the vial can radiate with the dark of the Abyss, blackening an area even if the sun is high in the sky.

A place—for instance, a room indoors or a forest clearing—becomes dark for the **remainder of the scene**, like a night without stars or moonlight.

- **Action:** Free
- **Corruption:** None

### Salvation of Darkness

The master of the artifact can crush the vial with a thought, releasing its darkness.

All creatures present with at least **1 corruption**, temporary or permanent, immediately suffer **1D12 permanent corruption**, with the following exception:

- Creatures that already have permanent corruption are affected only if the roll exceeds their current permanent corruption. They suffer **the difference**.
- If the roll is equal to or lower than their current permanent corruption, nothing happens.
- This also affects the master if they have any corruption.
- Only creatures with **zero corruption** are safe from harm.

- **Action:** Free
- **Corruption:** 1D12 (see above)

## Creating Vile Water

### Requirements

- A **managaal** at least half full of corruption **[Resolute/2]**.
- A **codex or manuscript** describing the procedure. These can be found in Davokar's dark ruins or bought from cultists and sorcerers for a small fortune.
- A **Master Alchemist** and a **Master Artifact Crafter**; these can be the same individual.

### Procedure

1. Spend **one full day** slowly boiling down the creature's blood to the desired thickness.
2. The Master Alchemist and Master Artifact Crafter must each roll a **Cunning test**.
3. If both tests succeed, create Vile Water according to the managaal used:

| Managaal | Yield |
| --- | --- |
| Adult | 10 drops |
| Spawn | 5 drops |

> [!warning] Corruption Risk
> If either test rolls **20**, the individual making that roll suffers **1D12 permanent corruption**.
