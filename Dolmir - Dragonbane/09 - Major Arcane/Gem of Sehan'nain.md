*magical, living, sorrowful*

Within the gem, colors shift like sunlight passing through autumn leaves. When held close, something within it pulses. not quite in time with your heart.

Sometimes, you feel a breeze where there is no wind.

## The Blessing Remains

When you hold the gem against a living creature and remain quietly with them for a few moments, you can sense whether they suffer from sickness, a curse, possession, or a wound to their spirit.

You learn the **nature of their suffering**, not its precise source or how to end it.

## A Borrowed Connection

While held and active, the gem provides an effective **Animism 13** for entering Communion and meeting the skill requirements of [[Dolmir - Dragonbane/06 - Heroic Paths/Path of the Alchemist/The Last Child of Sehan'nain|The Last Child of Sehan'nain]]. Use the bearer's own Animism if higher; this is not an additive bonus.

This grants neither ordinary Animism spells nor free path ranks. The bearer pays the path's normal WP costs and must still earn its advancements and deeds. The gem's value does not increase automatically: Journeyman and Master require the bearer's own Animism at 15 and 17 respectively. Dormancy or losing physical contact removes the borrowed skill; the path note describes how this affects Communion.

## Awakening the Gem

Each mystery awakens through its own deed, described below. When you complete a deed while carrying the gem, mark it off and unlock the associated mystery. The mysteries may awaken in any order.

Each deed must involve a meaningful cost or risk, undertaken freely rather than staged to awaken the gem. One deed unlocks only one mystery; the GM determines when its requirements have been fulfilled.

## Mysteries

### Selfless Sacrifice

- [ ] **Deed:** Freely take suffering or a lasting personal loss upon yourself to spare another, without asking or expecting repayment. 

When you touch a willing creature, you may take one of its conditions upon yourself, removing it from them.

Alternatively, suffer up to **6 damage**, ignoring armor, to restore the same number of HP to them. This cannot reduce you below 1 HP.

*The gem does not create life. It reminds life that it need not suffer alone.*

### Sehan'nain’s Shelter

- [ ] **Deed:** Remain to protect someone who cannot protect themselves when you could safely escape. Place yourself in genuine danger and see them through it.

When you raise the gem and call upon Sehan’nain, spend **2 WP** and use an action. Spectral branches shelter a creature within **10 meters**.

Reduce the next damage it suffers by **2D6**, then the branches scatter into golden leaves. The blessing fades at the start of your next turn if unused. A creature can bear only one such blessing at a time.

### The First Blessing

- [ ] **Deed:** Entrust someone vulnerable with knowledge, power, or a precious resource that enables them to stand on their own. Give up your control over the gift, asking neither service nor repayment.

When you touch a willing creature, you may transfer up to **3 WP** from yourself to them, without exceeding their maximum.

If they cannot normally use magic, you may instead mark a **Consequence** to let them cast one spell you know, once, before the next sunrise. They use your casting skill but pay the spell’s WP cost themselves.

*An echo of the god who gave humanity magic.*

### Roots Remember

- [ ] **Deed:** Recover something lost of Sehan’nain—a forgotten name, a desecrated shrine, or the memory of one of the dead—and restore it to those who should remember. Risk something meaningful to preserve it rather than claim it for yourself.

When you sleep with the gem among the roots of a living tree, name a person, place, or event connected to Sehan’nain.

Roll **MYTHS & LEGENDS**:

**On a dragon:** You witness a clear memory and may ask the GM one question about it.

**On a success:** You witness a true but incomplete memory.

**On a failure:** You may mark a consequence to witness the memory; otherwise, you dream only of falling leaves.

**On a demon:** Something within the memory notices you. Mark a consequence.

These are memories, not unrestricted visions of the present or future.

## Consequences

- [ ] **Autumn in Your Blood:** Your maximum HP decreases by 2. A golden vein appears beneath your skin.
- [ ] **Borrowed Grief:** You inherit a vivid memory of someone who died with Sehan’nain. Their unfinished concern begins intruding upon your dreams.
- [ ] **The Blessing Is Seen:** Spirits and those sensitive to divine magic recognize what you carry. Some come seeking sanctuary; others come to claim it.
- [ ] **Roots Take Hold:** After marking this consequence, the gem becomes dormant until you spend a Shift rooted in living earth, unable to move while you recover.
- [ ] **The Last Leaf Falls:** The gem can perform one final, extraordinary blessing agreed with the GM. Afterward, it becomes an ordinary stone—or a seed.

## GM Notes

The gem is a surviving blessing of a dead god, not another corrupting artifact. Its temptation is giving too much of yourself, rather than becoming evil.

Its powers focus on sharing strength, sheltering others, and restoring broken connections. Ordinary healing transfers life rather than creating free recovery.
