Clawbert Danfangle is a young wolfkin merchant who travels a lot and sells whatever he can get his paws on. His main interest is monster parts and strange natural things. Teeth, claws, scales, bones, venom sacs, odd plants, and other rare curiosities. If it looks unusual, valuable, or like someone might want it, he’ll try to collect it and sell it later.

He talks a lot and tends to pitch deals constantly, even when nobody asked.

He’s been friends with [[Garrick Stonebill]] for a while now. The blacksmith makes prosthetics and monster-hunting gear, which works well for Clawbert since he’s always bringing back strange parts from his travels. Sometimes they’re useful, sometimes they’re just weird curiosities, but the two of them have a good working relationship.

Clawbert also has a bit of a collector’s habit. If he thinks something is interesting or impressive, he sometimes keeps a piece of it. Usually that means monster parts, but occasionally it means things from people he admires too. He doesn’t really see the problem with this, but other people tend to disagree.

Because of that, Clawbert doesn’t stay in towns very long. Every now and then he leaves a place a little quicker than planned when people start asking questions about missing things.

He’s currently in Ashveil to sell some goods, learn about the local plants and monsters, and see what new things might be worth collecting.

Also, if anyone from the last town happens to show up looking for him, he will absolutely pretend he’s never been there before.

Before Ashveil

Clawbert originally comes from the small town of **Mountside**, where the Danfangle family had lived for several generations. His childhood was mostly normal, aside from a few strange incidents. Family pets would occasionally turn up missing small patches of fur, or sometimes a tooth. Nothing serious, just… odd.

Things got worse when one of his older cousins, Merry, came to visit.

Merry had a particularly impressive pair of claws. Long, sharp, and well kept. Clawbert, despite the name, did not have claws like that, and he became more and more fascinated with them during the visit.

Eventually he decided he wanted them.

The night before Merry was meant to leave, Clawbert put together a plan. Some powdered herbs mixed into dinner helped everyone sleep more soundly than usual. Once the house was quiet, Clawbert went to Merry’s room and started trying to remove one of the claws.

Unfortunately, it turned out to be a lot more difficult and bloody than he expected. Merry woke up almost immediately in extreme pain, and things escalated from there. By the time the rest of the house woke up, there had already been plenty of yelling and thrashing, and Clawbert had barely managed to remove the first claw.

The situation ended about as badly as you would expect.

Rather than exile him completely, the family decided on a different solution. Clawbert was sent to live with the border hermit outside Mountside, someone they hoped could teach him discipline and help him get control over his strange urges.

Clawbert stayed there for five years.

It did not really help.

Eventually he left the hermit’s home with a nice new leather hat, made from the hermit’s hides. The hermit themselves had passed away peacefully in their sleep from natural causes, which Clawbert considered fortunate timing.

With that, Clawbert set out into the world, ready to travel, trade, and continue building what he considers a very respectable collection.

## Secrets
- Clawbert can't stop. The urge to collect, to possess the beautiful, the powerful and rate. It's a hunger he doesn't understand and can't control. 
- He knows what happened with Merry was wrong, and the five years with the hermit were meant to help him. They didn't. The book [[Altes Lesym]] warned him about only makes it worse—it whispers, and the whispers feel like home.

**The Hook:** When Clawbert learns magic through the book, he'll have to confront what he's becoming. The party can help him break the cycle.

**What Clawbert Knows**
- He still has Merry's claw. It's his most prized possession, hidden in his collection. He remembers the night vividly—the drugged wine, the blood, Merry's screams. He doesn't think he did anything wrong.
- The hermit is dead. Clawbert insists it was natural causes, but the truth is more complicated: the hermit was old and frail, and Clawbert wanted their hide for a hat. Whether he hastened the process is something even Clawbert has rationalized away.
- His "collecting" hasn't stopped. It's only gotten quieter. Small things from people he travels with—a lock of hair, a fingernail clipping, a lost tooth. He's learned to take things so subtly that people rarely notice.
- The book [[Altes Lesym]] warned him about is old, and it whispers to him sometimes. It shows him images of things worth collecting. It makes the urge stronger.

**What Others Don't Know**
- **The Danfangle Family has not forgotten.** His parents still live in Mountside. They want him found—not for reconciliation, but because Merry has been institutionalized, traumatized, and his father (a minor Lord) wants Clawbert brought to justice. The Danfangle name is tainted in the region.
- **Merry is alive, and he knows.** Merry lost full use of his dominant hand after the incident. He's spent five years learning to fight left-handed. If he discovers where Clawbert is, he will come for him.
- **The hermit's death is being investigated.** A ranger was sent to check on the hermit two years ago and found them dead. The body had already been processed (skinned), and the ranger assumed Clawbert had done it. The ranger reported it to local authorities, but the trail went cold. If Clawbert encounters a ranger or lawman from that region, his cover is blown.
- **His collection contains something dangerous.** Among his "curiosities" is a bone carved with Old God runes—something he took from a plague site years ago. It doesn't belong in mortal hands, and it whispers. The book wants him to find more.
- **People have gone missing in his wake.** Not many, and not all of them his doing. But there's a pattern: Clawbert arrives, he stays a few weeks, then people notice small things missing (personal items, body parts from the recently dead being exhumed). Two deaths have been attributed to him by rumor—unproven, but the whispers follow him.

**Plot Hooks**
- If Clawbert's collection is discovered (by the party or an NPC), it reads as deeply disturbing: bones, teeth, hair, preserved body parts. There's Merry's claw, clearly from a wolfkin. There's the hermit's hide (now a hat). The collection is evidence of a pattern.
- If an NPC from Mountside or the hermit's region recognizes him, they will either try to apprehend him or alert authorities. Clawbert becomes a fugitive mid-session.
- If the book's whispers intensify (especially if Clawbert accesses it with Runekeeper's help), it may lead him to a cursed site or artifact. The party must choose: help him use the book, or stop him from being consumed by it.
- If Merry ever finds him, it becomes a blood feud. The party is caught between a wolfkin they know and a man they've never met—but whose cause is just.
- The ranger who investigated the hermit's death may arrive in Valehall seeking answers. If Clawbert is exposed, the party must decide whether to protect him or turn him over.
