Lia and Ellitot
- Elliot Lovecraft is a man who desperately sought better life. He loved an Elf but due to his father he had to be rid of her. Eventually as an adult he fell for an elf again but was conflicted in who he was, when he met the Order it became worse. 
- Lias mother was not much better, a woman bound to a hag. She refused the first deal to give up Lia but agreed on the second for Lily. Elliot was furious and blamed her 'heretic' ways. This was the last Lia saw them 
- The Hag collected Lia's mother who is bound within her lair
- Elliot is part of the Order but will seek Lia's help upon seeing her to find her mother. He is still a bad guy but conflicted..
