Backstory — Elenessa Liriel Vaeloriane

Elenessa Liriel Vaeloriane, aged nineteen, is the daughter of Serelinne Vaerethiel Vaeloriane and Alverion Theren Vaeloriane, owners of the Vaeloriane Wine Estate.

From a young age, her path was understood. She would one day take part in managing the estate, and so her education began early. She was taught to read, write, and keep records, spending much of her time alongside her mother working through ledgers, accounts, and correspondence. Her world was structured, orderly, and predictable.

One night, while reviewing older records, she began to notice patterns she had never paid attention to before. The same visitors signed their names year after year. The same families attended the same gatherings. The same clients made the same large purchases. The records did not describe change, only repetition.

The thought came quietly: perhaps the outside world was different. Perhaps not everything followed the same pattern.

At the time, it was only a passing idea, but it altered the way she looked at everything around her. She began paying attention—to clothing, speech, posture, and the way people reacted under pressure. Human behavior became something she studied without announcing it. She started keeping a journal, first as a place for small notes, then as a record of observations. She wanted to understand people, but speaking to them did not come easily.

At first, she observed from a distance. She found ways to attend gatherings where she was not expected to participate—taverns, festivals, and public events—watching rather than engaging. Over time, she began trying to speak to people directly. The attempts were awkward. She found it difficult to hold conversation without sounding formal or rehearsed, and she often misread social cues. Still, she continued, returning to it day after day despite the discomfort.

A year ago, House Vaeloriane and House Corvelle reached an agreement to end a long-standing feud between them. As part of this agreement, Elenessa was betrothed to Cael Corvelle.

At first, speaking with Cael was no easier than with anyone else. But his patience and quiet kindness made conversation less difficult over time. He listened, responded thoughtfully, and never pushed her. Gradually, she grew comfortable around him. They could speak openly, and in every practical sense, he was exactly what she had been prepared for. He was capable, steady, and well-suited to the life expected of them.

That was the problem.

Everything about the arrangement matched the patterns she had spent years recording. The same kind of agreement. The same kind of future. The same cycle, continuing as it always had.

She would not just observe it anymore. She would become part of it.

Elenessa does not dislike Cael. She respects him, and she trusts him more than most. In another life, she might have been content. But the thought of stepping into a future already defined, already repeated, has become increasingly difficult to accept.

Her family needs this marriage. It secures stability, strengthens alliances, and ensures continuity. It is what she has been prepared for her entire life.

And yet, she cannot quiet the thought that there must be something beyond it.

Today is the day of the wedding.

Everything is ready. Nothing is out of place.

And as the day begins, her mind races—not with fear of the ceremony itself, but with the weight of what comes after. The days that follow, one after another, already imagined, already familiar.

She stands at the edge of a life she understands completely.

And for the first time, she is not sure she can step into it.
