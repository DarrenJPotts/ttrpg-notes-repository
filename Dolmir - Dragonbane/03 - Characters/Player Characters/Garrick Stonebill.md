Garrick’s early life was marked by hardship and misunderstanding. Born among the mallard folk—a race often underestimated and dismissed for their perceived lack of intelligence—he faced prejudice from the very beginning. Orphaned at a young age, Garrick had no parents to guide or protect him. Without family, he was left to rely on the kindness of others, but the world was not kind. Many saw him less as a person and more as a pet, a creature to be cared for but never truly respected.

This treatment came with many disadvantages. Garrick was often left to sleep outside, exposed to the elements and the chill of the night. Over time, this harsh upbringing instilled in him a deep-seated aversion to sleeping indoors, where he felt confined and uneasy. The open air, despite its dangers, was where he felt most free.

As Garrick grew, so did his voice and his mind. He learned to speak clearly and express himself, which earned him a place in the local school—a rare opportunity for someone of his background. It was there that Garrick discovered his true passion: working with his hands. He had a natural talent for envisioning intricate designs, a skill that set him apart from his peers. This creative spark led him down the path of blacksmithing.

Determined to prove his worth, Garrick poured his heart and soul into his craft. He worked tirelessly, ensuring that the quality of his work spoke louder than any prejudice. His dedication paid off; the townspeople began to see him not as a mere mallard folk, but as a master blacksmith whose skill was unmatched in the region. Being the only competent blacksmith for several towns around, Garrick’s forge was always busy, giving him ample opportunity to hone his craft and perfect his art.

With his growing reputation, Garrick’s name spread far and wide, attracting visitors from distant lands. Among these visitors was Clawbert Danfangle, a curious and enigmatic figure. Initially, Clawbert came seeking ordinary armor, but one day he brought Garrick an extraordinary request. Clawbert carried with him the talons of a griffin—rare, powerful, and imbued with ancient magic—and asked if Garrick could forge them into special weapons.

This unique challenge sparked a new chapter in Garrick’s life. Working with such exotic materials pushed his skills beyond anything he had ever imagined. The collaboration with Clawbert not only expanded Garrick’s expertise but also introduced him to a world of adventure and mystery, where his talents could shine in ways he had never dreamed possible.
