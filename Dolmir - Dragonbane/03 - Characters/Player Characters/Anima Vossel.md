Anima “Ani” Vossel
#### Appearance 
Anima is a slender elven woman with long black hair and a quiet, watchful presence. She lives in a small hut just outside Ashveil, surrounded by a carefully tended herbal garden of medicine plants, flowers, and rune-marked stones. She looks gentle and calm at first glance, but there is always a tiredness in her eyes, like someone carrying memories she cannot put down.

#### Personality 
Ani is kind, patient, and deeply caring, especially toward the sick, injured, and those who have nowhere else to turn. She has become a trusted herbalist in Ashveil, though she still keeps a little distance from most people. Betrayal taught her not to trust easily. She is quiet rather than cold, and deeply curious when it comes to magic, runes, and old healing lore.

#### Backstory 
Ani was raised in a secluded elven cloister hidden from the world, a magical haven where the children were trained from a young age in various forms of spellcraft and rune-lore. It was the only real home she ever knew, until the cloister was discovered and attacked during one of the Eldar-cleansing expeditions.

The attack was made possible by her childhood best friend, [[Maya Serellye]], who sold out the cloister’s location to the legion. Ani survived only because one of her mentors sacrificed his life to buy her enough time to escape. During the attack, Maya also left her with a hex that has never fully healed. The wound weakens Ani physically and magically, and blocks much of her power.

She came to Ashveil afterward, where she slowly built a new life on the village’s edge. Though she has only lived there a few years, the people have grown tightknit with her, and she has become one of the village’s quiet protectors.

#### Goals 
Ani tends the hex carefully while searching for a way to break it. One day, when she is strong enough to travel safely again, she hopes to find Maya and understand why she was betrayed. Her deeper dream is to one day rebuild the cloister as a new safe haven for elves, a place where magic can live without fear.

#### Party Hook 
Ani is the sort of person who will heal a stranger without hesitation, this leads to her becoming Ashveil’s herbalist. She is gentle, thoughtful, and brave in a quiet way, but her trust is hard won. If she decides someone is worth believing in, she will stand beside them with everything she has.
