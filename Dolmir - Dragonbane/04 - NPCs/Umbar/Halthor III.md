# Halthor III

Son of [[Haldar Umbar]] and twin brother of [[Vaelor II]]. Aged 18 at the campaign opening in 2169. Struggles to hold together a coalition of lords. Seeks Eldar and older powers as potential answers to the Blight; his dangerous bargains are campaign hooks.
