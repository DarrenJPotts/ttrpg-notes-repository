# Corvin Umbar

The Faithful. Oversees the Rite of Purification. [[Saint Odellia]] is executed during his reign in 2119. Dies in 2134 aged 53. The timeline inconsistently uses Corvin I and Corvin II; his regnal number remains unresolved.
