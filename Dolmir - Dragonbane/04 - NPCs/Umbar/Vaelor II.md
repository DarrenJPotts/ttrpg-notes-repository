# Vaelor II

Son of [[Haldar Umbar]] and twin brother of [[Halthor III]]. Aged 18 at the campaign opening in 2169. Purges the old court and views his father as weak. Supports suppression and control through the Faith, mobilizing armies to contain the Blight. Relocation of infected populations is a possible policy, not a confirmed event.
