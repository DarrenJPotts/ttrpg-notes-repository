Part of [[The Primrose Court]] 
Mother of [[Lia and Lily]] 

She made a deal with a hag in order to live free with [[Elliot Lovecraft]] but the cost was her first daughter. When she refused to give up Lia, the cost became their second daughter Lily and the servitude of Lia when she was old enough

Lyra joined [[The Primrose Court]] as 'servitude' paid back to the Hag 'Granny Rose', The oldest of the Devouring daughters.

#### Personality
- She wishes her daughters freedom and longs for them but her deeds have lost her not only her daughters but her husband too.
- She shows a strong and stern face but breaks behind the mask
