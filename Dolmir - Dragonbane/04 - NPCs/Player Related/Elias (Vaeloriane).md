#### Secrets 
 Alverion had a lot of enemies out of his youth. And after much heartbreak, Serelinne had decided alongside Gregor and Alverion to give up the child and raise him in secret, away from the world of the Vaeloraine house. 

What Gregor did not expect was the shame he had carried for his betrayal of his friends marriage so when he was invited to the birth of Elenessa and the chance of their own child, he said that [[Elias (Vaeloriane)]] had died of the pox. 

Gregor kept the letters of Serelinne Vaerethiel Bonniere and Alverion Thesarin Vaeloriane from [[Elias (Vaeloriane)]] from his first two years of life. The first date is recorded 1 year after Gregor had taken the child. 

Gregor had loved the child at first but felt the guilt of lying to his friend, his son and the original betrayal. Gregor treated Elias as a burden in his early years.
