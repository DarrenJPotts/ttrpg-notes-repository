#### Secrets 
- Altes is an ancient man, well over 400 years of age alongside the remaining [[Inquisitors]] of the [[Order of the One God]]. He is also one of their founding members, although unintentionally or misguided. He, alongside [[Father Alexios Valon]], [[Inquisitor Templar Sige]], [[The Smiling Blade]] and a few others were a group of over ambitious friends who sought to save the Heartlands from the [[Second Long War]], but in turn released the blight with the death of the God Tree Uron, whom [[Anima Vossel]] was conclave to.
- He is cursed with unending life similar to that of a Higher Vampire
- He wears a ring bound on a necklace, it is ancient and from his youth. Now it binds the corruption of the Blight and his alter form, his kindness turns to cruelty without it.
- His goal is to save his friends from the blight.
