####  **Bitter Succession Within Their Own Camps**

Each brother is consolidating power _within_ his faction, and that's where the real tension lies.

- **Vaelor II** is purging the old court, removing anyone who was close to King Haldar (whom he sees as weak). He's paranoid about saboteurs.
- **Halthor III** is struggling to hold together a coalition of fractious lords who keep asking "what's in it for me?" — he's desperate and making dangerous promises.

**What the party might stumble into:**

- A cardinal or House lord suddenly dies under suspicious circumstances — which brother gains most?
- The party is asked to deliver a message or retrieve something sensitive. If it's intercepted, it could trigger war early.
- A refugee fleeing one brother's purges seeks shelter with party allies.

---

#### **The Blight Becomes a Weapon**

Each brother has different strategies for the _real_ crisis — Dolmir itself.

- **Vaelor II** believes the Faith's answer (suppression, control) is the only way. He's **mobilizing armies to contain the Blight**, possibly relocating populations from infected zones.
- **Halthor III** is **making deals with the Eldar and wild elements** (some of which are _way_ too dangerous). He's gambling that old power can reverse the Blight.

**What the party might stumble into:**

- Forced conscription of able-bodied people into Vaelor's armies
- A Halthor agent offering them resources/power in exchange for collecting something from a Blighted zone
- Discovering evidence that one brother _intentionally caused or worsened_ a Blight outbreak to discredit the other
