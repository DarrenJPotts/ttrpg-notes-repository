Merchant Jefferson is a shrewd and cunning merchant and acquirer of the Arcane who would sooner lose his soul than his coin when not needed. 

#### Looks:
- Obese man with a permanent scowl
- Mostly bald except for the strands of thin white hair

#### Personality
- Permanently scowls but deeply caring of [[Anima Vossel]], referring to her as *My Dear Child*
- Will rub his chins often and slick back his balding hairs

#### Secrets
- Investigating the Arcane on behalf of [[Anima Vossel]] 
- Discovers information of [[Anima Vossel]]'s age discrepancy which led him down the path connecting to the [[Order of the One God]] 
	- The one book is a tome with the letters *A.V* 
	- Went missing when overplaying his hand with the merchant of the Underlight, [[Cilius Val]]
