# Saint Odellia

Teaches unity between Eldar and Man. Burned at the stake in the Vale in 2119 under [[Corvin Umbar]]. Her suppressed teachings resurface in Haldar?s childhood. [[Haldar Umbar]] formally recognizes her sainthood in 2150. Her burning precedes the campaign by fifty years.
