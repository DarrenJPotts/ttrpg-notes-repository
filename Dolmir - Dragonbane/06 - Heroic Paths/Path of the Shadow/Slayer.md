Slayers hunt dangerous creatures through investigation, preparation and practical swordsmanship. They learn what a creature is, how it attacks and what can stop it before committing to a fight. Some undergo alchemical alteration; others rely on training alone.

Their Signs are brief magical techniques used alongside their weapons, not a substitute for a mage's craft. A Slayer may carry oils and potions, but brewing them remains a separate discipline.

Slayer techniques use **melee weapons** unless stated otherwise. Each rank requires the previous rank and a separate Heroic Path advancement. Training and story milestones should involve investigation, a prepared hunt and, eventually, sound judgement when a contract conceals the real threat. These are homebrew mechanics for playtesting.

#### Dedicated Passive:
**Heightened Senses**
Gain a boon on Awareness rolls to notice a creature through faint sounds or scents, and on Hunting & Fishing rolls to follow a physical trail. The relevant sounds, scents or traces must actually be present.

This does not reveal exact positions, negate darkness or concealment, detect magic, or permit attacks against an unseen target without the normal penalties. It does not improve every Awareness roll or the study roll below.

## Studying a Quarry

All Slayers can study a quarry, whether or not Slayer is their dedicated path. This is the setup for their techniques, not a separate advancement or an additional passive bonus.

Spend ten minutes examining meaningful evidence, or one action observing a visible creature in combat. Roll Hunting & Fishing for physical evidence or Myths & Legends for supernatural lore, choosing the relevant skill before rolling.

On success, designate one specific creature as your quarry and ask the GM two questions:
- What kind of creature does this evidence suggest?
- What does its behaviour suggest it wants or where it will go?
- What sign warns of one of its dangerous attacks?
- What existing weakness, resistance or protective measure can I identify?

Answers are true but may be incomplete. Studying reveals facts; it does not invent a vulnerability, reveal an entire attack table or automatically solve a curse. On failure, you gain no reliable conclusion and cannot try again until you find meaningful new evidence or observe a previously unseen attack or behaviour.

You may have one quarry at a time. It remains designated until your next stretch rest or until you successfully study another creature. Studying a corpse or tracks can designate the particular creature responsible, not every member of its species. A quarry can be a person; supernatural origin is not required.

### Shared Preparation

When [[Anima Vossel]] prepares an oil or salve specifically for a creature, you may participate in the preparation. If either of you successfully studies that creature and shares the findings, you may designate it as your quarry without making a separate study roll. Resolve the study once; it does not grant another set of questions for each participant.

This is a cooperation rule, not a separate ability or advancement. It costs no additional Willpower, but the preparation still requires its normal time, ingredients and rolls. It does not invent knowledge or weaknesses. Merely applying a generic oil or salve does not designate a quarry.

### Hunting Instincts

If you have Hunting Instincts, you may use a creature designated as your prey through that ability as your Slayer quarry. This replaces any previous quarry designation and remains valid while the prey designation lasts, rather than ending at your next stretch rest. You still have only one Slayer quarry; choosing another quarry ends this use of the prey designation but does not itself end Hunting Instincts' tracking effect.

This does not grant the information normally gained through study. Hunting Instincts must be learned separately and retains its normal activation, tracking duration and Willpower costs. Slayer does not grant it for free.

For the same eligible attack, you may spend Hunting Instincts' further 1 Willpower for a boon, then spend 2 Willpower on Prepared Strike if the attack hits. An applicable oil or salve provides its own stated benefit as well; combining the abilities grants no further automatic bonus.

#### Novice:
**Prepared Strike**
Willpower: 2 points per use
Requirement: Hunting & Fishing 13, any melee weapon skill 13

Once per round, after a melee weapon attack hits your quarry and overcomes any defense, you may spend 2 Willpower before rolling damage to add 1D6 damage.

Resolve the combined damage against armor normally. The additional die is not multiplied by a Dragon result. This technique grants no additional attack and does not bypass immunity or a requirement for a specific weapon material.

Designating a quarry through study, Shared Preparation or Hunting Instincts is sufficient for this technique; a special oil or monster ingredient is not also required. Actually exploiting a vulnerability still provides whatever benefit that vulnerability normally grants, not an additional undefined bonus.

#### Journeyman:
**Signs**
Willpower: 2 points per use
Requirement: Hunting & Fishing 15, any melee weapon skill 15, training in Signs

Spend an action and roll WIL to make a Sign with a free hand. Pay the Willpower before rolling; failure has no effect. Learn two Signs when gaining this rank. Each further Sign requires training and a separate story reward agreed with the GM, not a free addition to the whole list.

- **Force:** Choose a visible creature within 4 meters. It must succeed on a STR roll or be pushed up to 2 meters directly away from you. It must be roughly your size or smaller. This deals no damage, cannot push through an obstacle or into an immediate hazard, and triggers no free attacks.
- **Flame:** A visible creature within 4 meters suffers 1D6 fire damage, reduced by armor normally. This allows no dodge or parry but does not automatically ignite the creature. Alternatively, ignite a readily combustible unattended object within range without dealing creature damage.
- **Ward:** Until the start of your next turn, reduce damage from the next damaging hit against you by 1D6 after armor, to a minimum of zero, then end the Sign. Multiple Wards do not stack. It protects against attacks, not self-inflicted costs, poison, drowning or ongoing environmental harm.
- **Binding:** Mark a 2-meter-radius circle centred on a point within 4 meters that you can see. Until the end of your next turn, each meter of ground movement inside it costs your quarry 2 meters of movement. Other creatures are unaffected. It does not restrain, stop attacks, prevent flight or make an incorporeal creature solid.
- **Distraction:** A creature within 4 meters that can perceive you makes a WIL roll. On failure, it cannot make free attacks until the start of your next turn. Its other actions and defenses are unaffected. Mindless creatures and creatures immune to mental influence are unaffected.

Signs require clear line of effect and cannot pass through solid barriers. Immunities still apply. Except for Binding, a Sign does not require the target to be your quarry. These techniques do not grant access to a school of magic.

#### Master:
**Combined Attack**
Willpower: 2 additional points, plus the Sign's normal cost
Requirement: Any melee weapon skill 17, Journeyman, training from a master

Once per round, immediately after resolving a melee weapon attack on your turn, you may cast one known Sign without spending another action. Pay 2 additional Willpower plus the Sign's normal 2-Willpower cost, make its WIL roll and apply all its usual requirements and limits.

The weapon attack need not hit. Resolve it fully before making the Sign; a Sign cannot retroactively improve that attack. The Sign may affect a different eligible target, or yourself if it is Ward. You must have a free hand when making it; this technique does not grant a free action to change your grip or put away a weapon.

You may use Prepared Strike on the weapon attack if eligible. Doing both costs 6 Willpower total: 2 for Prepared Strike, 2 for the Sign and 2 for casting it without an action. An ordinary attack followed by a Sign costs 4 Willpower.

This technique grants no additional weapon attack. Attacks granted outside your turn do not trigger it, and the Sign cannot trigger another Combined Attack.

## Alchemy and Alteration

**Alchemy remains a separate investment.** Hunters can buy oils, commission preparations or learn [[Iskandor's Synthesis]]. This path grants no free recipes, potions or immunity to toxicity. Oils and salves can provide their stated benefits alongside Prepared Strike and Hunting Instincts, subject to their own restrictions. They grant no extra unspecified damage bonus and only help designate a quarry through Shared Preparation as described above.

**Optional rite — The Ashen Crucible:** A consenting initiate undergoes supervised alchemical alteration. Pale eyes, changed veins, an unfamiliar scent or disturbed sleep can mark the transformation. Initially these are narrative traits only; any enhanced senses or resistances should replace a separately earned ability, not become free bonuses. No mandatory sterility, loss of emotion or lethal player-character trial.

## A School in Dolmir — The School of the Ash Hound

*Proposed lore, available to introduce rather than assumed to exist already.*

A diminished order of roadwardens once maintained wayhouses along routes between human and Eldar settlements. They survived by taking contracts nobody else would accept. Now their records are scattered, their practices condemned and their remaining masters divided.

Their emblem is an iron hound curled around an ember. An ordinary medallion is a badge, not a free magical detector.

**Three teachings:**
1. Name the threat before you name your price.
2. An employer purchases your labour, not your judgement.
3. Never mistake a curse's victim for its author.

### Ways to introduce the school

- **The Underlanes:** A hunter follows reports of the Shelob. They want evidence from its lair; rescuing survivors competes with collecting it.
- **The Arcane Horror:** Someone offers to purchase its remains. The promised payment is real; the intended experiment is troubling.
- **The False Contract:** A bounty describes an Eldar fugitive as a shapeshifting predator. The alleged attacks and the witness accounts do not agree.

None requires a particular outcome. A hunter can become teacher, rival, customer or enemy depending on the party's choices.


## Design and Playtest Notes

- **Compared with Assassin:** Slayer studies evidence and gains personal damage against a quarry; Assassin marks a visible enemy to support the whole party. Slayer does not grant sneak attacks or Backstabbing.
- **Compared with Dirty Fighting:** Prepared Strike adds 1D6 for 2 Willpower after a successful hit, but requires a designated quarry. Dirty Trick plus Follow Through costs 4 total and adds an impairment and repositioning as well as damage.
- **Compared with Spirit Warrior:** Signs provide several small tactical options. They do not make weapons magical or grant the Spirit Warrior's sustained anti-spirit damage bonus.
- **Compared with Reaver:** Slayer spends Willpower on preparation and short combinations, not healing or self-damaging area attacks.
- **Compared with Iskandor's Synthesis:** Slayer uses preparations; the alchemist makes them. Watch the combined damage of oils and Prepared Strike rather than assuming all existing preparations are already balanced.
- **Compared with Twin Shadow:** Combined Attack saves the Slayer one action for a Sign, paid at a premium. It does not generate partner attacks or move two characters.
- The dedicated passive is granted only when Slayer is the character's dedicated path, following [[Heroic Paths]]. Quarry study remains available to anyone who learns the sub-art.
- Test the study action in short, unexpected fights. It is a deliberate setup cost, but should not require guessing a creature's name or obtaining inaccessible evidence.
- Signs retain two costs at Journeyman: an action and a possible failed WIL roll. Their damage is intentionally modest; assess whether their control and utility make them worthwhile before increasing their numbers.
- Mastery upgrades action economy rather than merely increasing damage. Monitor repeated Flame combinations and Ward use; the extra Willpower is an intentional constraint.
