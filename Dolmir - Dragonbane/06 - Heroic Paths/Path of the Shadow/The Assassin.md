Assassins study how people live so they can choose when they die. Weapons, poison and careful preparation are tools of the same trade. Some sell their skills to the highest bidder; others serve a cause or reserve their talents for those they believe deserve death.

Assassin techniques work with **melee and ranged weapon attacks**, except where stated otherwise. Each rank requires the previous rank and a separate Heroic Path advancement. This is a homebrew conversion for playtesting, not an official Dragonbane talent.

#### Dedicated Passive:
**Surveillance**
After ten uninterrupted minutes observing a person who is unaware of your surveillance, the GM tells you one useful, observable detail about their routine or precautions: a repeated route, a moment when they leave their guards, or an object they consistently protect.

The detail must be supported by what you could actually observe. This does not reveal thoughts, hidden equipment or future plans. You must still make any normal Sneaking rolls required to remain unnoticed. Further information requires a new situation or a meaningful change in their behaviour, not repeatedly observing the same ten minutes.

#### Novice:
**Mark Target**
Willpower: 2 points per use
Requirement: Sneaking 13, any weapon skill 13
Range: A visible target within 10 meters

Spend an action to study an opponent and identify an opening. Until combat ends, the target dies or you mark another target, all melee and ranged weapon attacks against the marked target deal +1 damage. You identify the opening to your allies through a brief signal or warning; they must perceive this communication to benefit. Your own attacks also benefit.

Add this bonus before armor and other damage reductions. It is not a separate instance of damage and does not bypass protection. The mark is not a magical tracker and does not reveal an unseen target's location.

You may have only one active mark. A creature cannot receive another Assassin mark during the same combat, even from a different assassin. Marks never stack. Changing targets ends your previous mark immediately.

#### Journeyman:
**Disrupt**
Willpower: 2 points per use
Requirement: Sneaking 15, any weapon skill 15

Once per round, before making a melee weapon attack against your marked target, you may spend 2 Willpower and use your action to feint, throw grit or otherwise distract it. If the target fails a CON roll, its next dodge or parry before the end of your next turn suffers a bane. On a success, there is no effect. The Willpower is spent either way.

Once per combat, you may use Disrupt without spending an action. This allows you to distract the target and then make your normal attack on the same turn; it does not grant an additional attack or movement. The Willpower cost still applies.

The target must be capable of perceiving and being affected by the chosen distraction. This technique cannot force an automatic monster attack to roll, remove an immunity or prevent a creature from acting. Repeated uses do not stack.

Disrupt is this path's replacement for the source talent's Dirty Fighting interaction. It does not require or grant ranks in [[Dolmir - Dragonbane/06 - Heroic Paths/Path of the Brawler/Dirty Fighting|Dirty Fighting]].

#### Master:
**Precision Kill**
Willpower: 2 points per use
Requirement: Sneaking 17, any weapon skill 17

Once per round, when you hit your marked target with a melee sneak attack, you may spend 2 Willpower before rolling damage to add 1D6 damage. An attack made using the Backstabbing heroic ability also qualifies.

The additional die is reduced by armor normally and is not multiplied by a Dragon result. Mark Target's +1 damage also applies. Despite its name, Precision Kill does not guarantee a kill or cause an automatic critical injury.

This technique does not grant Backstabbing, turn ordinary attacks into sneak attacks, or remove their normal restrictions. Outside combat, you must still approach undetected. During combat, you need Backstabbing or another explicitly qualifying ability; merely attacking a distracted or held enemy is not sufficient.

## Conversion Notes

Based on the supplied Dragon Age Assassin talent. Antivan and Thedas-specific lore has not been imported into Dolmir.

- Mark Target keeps the source talent's party-wide +1 weapon damage and one-mark-per-target restriction, with a Dragonbane action and Willpower cost.
- Disrupt replaces the source's fixed-difficulty Constitution test with a CON roll. It uses a brief defensive penalty rather than denying an entire turn. Its once-per-combat action-free use preserves the original rank's timing advantage.
- Precision Kill retains the source's extra 1D6 against a marked target, with a Willpower cost and once-per-round limit.
- Surveillance is a new utility passive, not a feature from the supplied talent.

## Playtest Notes

Mark Target becomes more valuable as the number of allied attacks increases. Watch its contribution in large groups and against high-ferocity enemies; the action cost should be a meaningful investment rather than an automatic opening move.

Mastery depends on access to sneak attacks or Backstabbing. Check that this prerequisite suits the intended character before investing in the path.
