Spirit warriors form pacts with spirits of the Reverie, allowing them to experience the physical world in exchange for protection and power. The spirit remains a separate presence rather than taking control of its partner. Not every spirit is benevolent, and choosing one poorly can have lasting consequences.

Their weapons and bodies become conduits for power drawn across [[Dolmir - Dragonbane/06 - World & Lore/The Veil|the Veil]]. They are not necessarily trained spellcasters, although those who witness their abilities may see little difference.

Spirit Warrior techniques work with **unarmed attacks and melee and ranged weapons**. Each rank requires the previous rank and a separate Heroic Path advancement. Learning the path requires a pact with a willing spirit, established through play. This is a homebrew conversion for playtesting; the pact tradition is proposed lore, not an established faction in Dolmir.

#### Dedicated Passive:
**Spirit Speech**
You can exchange simple thoughts, emotions and intentions with a willing spirit you can already perceive, even without a shared language. Complex information still requires a means of communication the spirit understands.

This does not reveal invisible spirits, summon the dead, compel answers or establish whether a spirit is truthful. Your bonded spirit knows only what it has actually experienced; it is not an unlimited source of supernatural knowledge.

#### Novice:
**Spirit Shroud**
Willpower: 3 points to start, 1 per subsequent round
Requirement: WIL 13, pact with a willing spirit

Spend an action to surround yourself with your bonded spirit's energy. While active, gain +1 armor rating against damage that armor normally reduces, and a boon on WIL rolls made to resist hostile spells or supernatural effects.

This does not grant a resistance roll where an effect normally allows none, and does not improve dodging or parrying. The armor bonus does not protect against damage that ignores armor.

At the start of each subsequent turn, spend 1 Willpower to maintain the shroud or let it end. Maintaining it requires no action. You may end it at any time without an action. It ends immediately if you become unconscious or otherwise unable to sustain the bond. You cannot activate multiple shrouds to stack their benefits.

#### Journeyman:
**Spirit Strike**
Willpower: No additional cost while Spirit Shroud is active
Requirement: WIL 15, any weapon skill or Brawling 15

While Spirit Shroud is active, your unarmed attacks and melee and ranged weapon attacks count as magical for the purpose of striking incorporeal creatures or overcoming resistance to non-magical attacks.

Against demons and spirits originating from the Reverie, these attacks also deal +2 damage. Add this bonus before armor and other damage reductions. It is not multiplied by a Dragon result.

This does not ignore armor, negate unrelated immunities or bypass a requirement for a specific material unless that requirement explicitly accepts magical attacks. Counting as magical does not change an attack's damage type. A weapon or projectile loses this benefit after the attack resolves.

The GM determines which creatures qualify as beings of the Reverie. Eldar, spellcasters and living creatures affected by a curse do not qualify merely because they are magical. Undead qualify only when they are themselves spirits or otherwise established as originating from the Reverie.

#### Master:
**Spirit Burst**
Willpower: 4 points per use
Requirement: WIL 17, any weapon skill or Brawling 17
Range: 4 meters, centred on you

While Spirit Shroud is active, spend an action to release a wave of spiritual energy. Choose any number of enemies you can perceive within 4 meters and roll 1D6 once. Each chosen enemy suffers that much damage, ignoring armor. Demons and spirits originating from the Reverie suffer an additional 2 damage.

Solid barriers block the burst. It cannot affect a creature through a wall or closed door. The burst allows no dodge or parry, but existing immunity to magical or spiritual damage still applies. Its energy can affect incorporeal creatures.

Spirit Strike's damage bonus does not apply again: the additional 2 damage above is the burst's entire bonus against beings of the Reverie. The burst does not end Spirit Shroud, but its normal maintenance cost remains.

## The Pact

Agree on the spirit's nature and one concrete expectation when establishing the bond: witnessing unfamiliar places, preserving certain memories or observing a particular custom, for example. Make the terms known to the player before they accept.

The pact should create choices rather than allow arbitrary loss of abilities. Deliberate violations can threaten the relationship, but give clear warning and an opportunity to negotiate or repair it. Possession or corruption is a potential story complication, not an automatic consequence of taking this path.

## Conversion Notes

Based on the supplied Dragon Age Spirit Warrior talent, with the Fade replaced by Dolmir's Reverie.

- Spirit Shroud replaces numerical Defense and Magic-test bonuses with modest armor and resistance benefits. These are not exact equivalents; the defense bonus has deliberately become damage mitigation rather than increased dodge success.
- A fixed +2 replaces attribute-based bonus damage, avoiding Dragonbane's much larger attribute scale.
- Spirit Burst uses an action and Willpower instead of stunt points. Its damage ignores armor, matching the source's penetrating damage.
- Spirit Speech is a new utility passive, not a feature from the supplied talent.

## Playtest Notes

The path is deliberately specialised against spirits and demons. Check that such enemies appear often enough for Journeyman to matter.

Spirit Burst is modest against one enemy but scales with the number caught in the area. Monitor crowded fights before increasing its damage or reducing its cost.
