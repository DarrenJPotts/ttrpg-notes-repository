### A Heroic Path for Dragonbane

> *"A drake stands in the gap so others need not. This is not strength. This is choice."*

---

## The Art

The Drake's Oath is old — older than the names that carry it now. It traces back to the legends of the Vale: Drake of the Vale himself, Ronther of the Bearkin, others whose stories have worn thin with age. What remains constant is the principle: a protector takes a vow, and that vow *becomes* their strength.

For Garrick Stonebill, a Mallard-kin blacksmith in a world that sees his people as lesser, the Drake's Oath is not a fantasy. It is a reclamation. The oath says: *I will stand. I will not yield. And I will make sure you understand what it costs to break me.*

The path is built on **Hammer skills** (the weight and reach of a great weapon used not for aggression but for absolute defense) and **the spoken vow** (a commitment made in the presence of witnesses, binding word to flesh). What makes it powerful is that the oath is *real* — once sworn, it cannot be unspoken. Garrick's power grows from the integrity of his choice, not from magic or luck.

He stands in the gap. This is his art.

---

## The Oath Gate

The Drake's Oath has no external master. It awakens when Garrick makes a **sworn vow** in play — a commitment spoken before witnesses (the party, an NPC, or both) to protect something or someone. The vow must be *specific* (not "I will be brave" but "I will protect the Eldar refugees from Valehall" or "I will stand against anyone who threatens this place").

Once spoken and witnessed, Tier 1 becomes available.

Tier 2 requires Garrick to have *kept* that vow at cost — to have taken damage, sacrificed resources, or put himself between an ally and harm *because of the oath*.

Tier 3 requires a moment of **absolute commitment** — standing alone against overwhelming odds, the oath burning bright enough that allies *see* it. Not metaphorically. The table sees it. A final stand that matters.

## Heroic Abilities

### Guardian's Stand
- **Requirement:** Any Weapon skill 13, spoken oath witnessed by the party
- **Willpower Points:** 2
- **Activation:** Reaction (costs no action of Garrick's)

When an ally within 2 meters is targeted by an attack, Garrick may immediately step into the strike's path. The attack targets him instead of the ally.

Garrick must still defend against the attack normally (dodge/parry if he chooses), and he takes any damage meant for the ally. But the choice is his — and it is *always available*.

*This ability has no limit per round. Garrick can throw himself in front of as many strikes as he has the will to stand for.*

---

### Oath Hardened
- **Requirement:** Any Weapon skill 15, Guardian's Stand, kept the oath at true cost
- **Willpower Points:** 2
- **Activation:** Passive (no action required; always active while oath is sworn)

The vow sinks into Garrick's bones. Garrick and all allies within 2 meters gain **+1 Armor Rating** as long as the oath is spoken and unbroken.

Additionally, Garrick may spend an action to invoke the oath's name. Roll **Persuasion or CHA**. On a success, enemies within sight who are not mindless suffer a **bane** on their next action as they feel the weight of what they are challenging.

*The oath does not forgive. The oath remembers.*

---

## Master Technique

### Last Stand
- **Requirement:** Any Weapon skill 17, Oath Hardened, stood alone against overwhelming odds to protect others
- **Willpower Points:** 3
- **Activation:** One action (once per combat)

Garrick plants himself and becomes **immovable**. He declares a defensive line — a 4-meter radius centered on him becomes his sanctum. For the next round:

- Garrick **cannot move**, but all allies within the sanctum gain **+2 Armor Rating** and **boon on all dodge/parry rolls**
- Enemies cannot move *through* the sanctum to reach allies beyond it
- If Garrick takes damage that would reduce him to 0 HP, he **does not fall**. He remains standing, conscious, and fighting until the encounter ends. Then he collapses.
- If an ally within the sanctum would take damage that reduces them to 0 HP, Garrick may spend 1 additional WP to redirect that damage to himself instead

*This is the final shape of the oath: the protector becomes the threshold between worlds. To reach those he guards, the enemy must go through him. And he does not break.*

---

## The Oath's Weight

The Drake's Oath is a commitment, not a gift. Garrick gains power from the vow, but he also becomes **bound** by it.

**While the oath is sworn:**
- Garrick *must* attempt to protect the oath's target (or targets) if they are in danger
- He cannot abandon them without narrative consequence (the oath fades, the abilities become unavailable until a new oath is sworn)
- He cannot use his protections for himself — only for others
- Breaking the oath in a moment of cowardice or self-interest is a character failure, not a mechanical one. But it *is* a failure. The GM and player should discuss what that means.

**Between oaths:**
- Garrick retains his abilities, but they are dormant
- Taking a new oath (different target, different commitment) reactivates them
- An oath can last a session, a campaign arc, or a lifetime

## Design Notes

The Drake's Oath is deliberately about **sacrifice, not power**. Garrick becomes stronger by taking on limitations (cannot move in Last Stand, cannot protect himself, bound by the oath). This is the inverse of most heroic paths, which grant freedom.

The strength of a protector is that they *choose* to stand there. The oath makes that choice real.

The Mallard-kin prejudice is central to this path's meaning. Garrick, from a race dismissed as weak, proves through absolute commitment that his people can be the strongest. Not through magic or luck. Through will.

Last Stand is deliberately powerful but *expensive* — it costs 3 WP, roots Garrick in place, and triggers the collapse mechanic. It should be a moment the table remembers. Use it sparingly. When it happens, let the scene breathe.

---

## Finding the Oath

An oath is not something Garrick seeks. It finds him in play. Examples:

- He stands before refugees from Ashveil and promises to keep them safe
- He swears to Elenessa Vaeloraine that he will help restore her house's honor
- He makes a blood pact with a dying ally
- He publicly challenges the prejudice against Mallard-kin and swears to *prove* them wrong through his actions
- He vows vengeance not for himself, but on behalf of those who cannot seek it

The GM does not force the oath. But when Garrick speaks a commitment with weight and witnesses hear it, the GM says: *"That oath is sworn. The Drake's Oath is now available to you."*

The path awakens in response to authenticity.
