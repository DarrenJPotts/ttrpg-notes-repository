Iskandor's Synthesis combines practical medicine with the properties of arcane herbs and harvested creature parts. It survives within the older tradition sometimes called the Green Wyrd: knowledge passed through Eldar households, hedge-witches and practitioners whose methods the Faith would rather suppress.

For [[Anima Vossel]], this craft is an extension of how she was raised. Her expertise lies in knowing what to combine, what a body can tolerate and when the correct treatment is also a dangerous one.

Synthesis techniques use **Healing, suitable tools and physical ingredients**. Each rank requires the previous rank and a separate Heroic Path advancement. Training and significant practical discoveries provide the story gates; no additional advancement-mark roll is required. These are homebrew mechanics for playtesting.

**Alchemical Crafting:** From Novice onward, you may imbue brewed potions and tonics with the magical properties of arcane-blessed herbs. This is part of the path and does not require its dedicated passive. The herb's properties and the added effect must be established in play and agreed with the GM before brewing. Use the recipe's normal costs and preparation requirements, plus the relevant herb; this grants no unspecified effects, automatic identification of unknown poisons or replacement for unique monster components.

#### Dedicated Passive:
**Gather, Distil, Compound**
You recognise common medicinal herbs and can distinguish sound ingredients from obvious spoilage through ordinary examination. With suitable tools, you can preserve gathered herbs and substitute a local equivalent for a common ingredient in a recipe you already know, provided it serves the same function.

The passive is granted only when Synthesis is your dedicated path, following [[Heroic Paths]]. It is not required to use the active ranks.

#### Novice:
**Field Brew**
Willpower: 2 points per use
Requirement: Healing 13
Ingredients: One bundle of common components

Spend an action and consume one bundle to prepare and apply a treatment to yourself or a willing creature within reach. No additional Healing roll is required. Choose one:

- **Staunching Draft:** Restore 1D4 HP, up to the recipient's maximum. End ordinary bleeding from a wound, but not magical bleeding or an effect that specifically requires another cure. This does not heal a critical injury.
- **Clarity Tincture:** For two rounds, ignore the bane from either Scared or Disheartened if caused by ordinary fear or distress. The condition remains, and its normal penalty returns when the treatment ends. This does not suppress an ongoing spell, curse or supernatural compulsion.
- **Numbing Compress:** For two rounds, including the round of application, ignore one bane explicitly caused by a physical injury. The injury remains; an unusable limb stays unusable and the recipient does not regain lost movement. When the effect ends, the injury's normal penalty returns without an additional rebound penalty.

The treatment is used as part of this action, not stored as a free potion. It requires physical access and a recipient able to receive the chosen treatment. Repeated applications of Clarity Tincture or Numbing Compress refresh the chosen treatment's duration; their benefits do not stack.

#### Journeyman:
**Targeted Compound**
Willpower: 2 points per dose
Requirement: Healing 15, practical experience harvesting and using a creature component
Ingredients: One common bundle; optionally one relevant rare dose
Preparation: Ten minutes per dose

Study meaningful evidence of the creature you expect to face, or work with someone who has done so. The GM confirms the intended creature or narrow creature type and the preparation's plausible basis before you spend ingredients. Knowing only that the target is a monster is insufficient.

Choose a compound, pay its cost and roll Healing. On failure, the ingredients and Willpower are spent and no usable dose is produced. On success, record its intended target and whether it is common or enhanced with a relevant rare component.

- **Weapon Oil:** Coat one melee weapon or up to three pieces of ammunition. Once per round, before rolling damage for a successful attack against the intended target, the wielder may add +2 damage. An enhanced oil instead adds one weapon damage die: for a 2D6 weapon, add 1D6; for a 2D8 weapon, add 1D8. Do not multiply this bonus on a Dragon result. Resolve the combined damage against armor normally.
- **Protective Salve:** Applied to one creature, reduce damage from the first damaging attack by the intended target by 2 after armor, to a minimum of zero, then end the salve's effect. An enhanced salve reduces that damage by 4 instead.
- **Repellent:** Light or break open the preparation while holding it. For one round, the intended creatures within 2 meters suffer a bane on attack rolls against the holder. An enhanced preparation lasts two rounds. Creatures must be able to perceive the relevant scent or smoke and be susceptible to it. This does not make automatic monster attacks roll to hit or prevent their other effects.

Applying a dose to a willing recipient or weapon, or activating Repellent, takes one action and no additional Willpower. An oil or salve must be applied before the attack it affects. Applied oil lasts until the end of the combat or ten minutes, whichever comes first; a salve lasts as long unless consumed earlier. Repellent uses its duration above. One dose of each preparation can affect a recipient or weapon at a time; duplicates replace rather than stack.

You may maintain up to three unused Targeted Compound doses at once. Unused doses spoil one shift after brewing; record their creation time. Brewing requires active work, not a rest benefit, but doses are transferable and retain their original maker's three-dose limit until used or spoiled.

#### Master:
**Decoction**
Willpower: 3 points per dose
Requirement: Healing 17, Journeyman, a major discovery involving rare ingredients and their bodily cost
Ingredients: One common bundle and one specific rare dose
Preparation: One shift of active work

Prepare one decoction calibrated to your own body. Choose its effect and agree on the relevant ingredient before spending resources. No additional brewing roll is required. You may have only one unused decoction; it spoils one shift after brewing. Preparing it is active work and does not also provide the benefits of resting.

Drinking it takes one action. Its effect lasts for three rounds, including the round of activation. You may have only one decoction active at a time and cannot drink another until you complete a stretch rest after it ends.

- **Reflex:** Requires a component from a creature noted for speed or heightened reactions. Gain a boon on dodge rolls. This grants no additional defensive action and does not change initiative. When it ends, roll CON or gain the Exhausted condition.
- **Resilience:** Requires a component from a creature noted for physical toughness. Reduce damage from physical attacks by 2 after armor, to a minimum of zero. Your movement is halved while active. It does not reduce self-inflicted costs, poison, drowning or environmental harm.
- **Blood Recovery:** Requires a fresh component from a blood-feeding creature. Once per round, when your melee attack causes a living enemy to lose HP, recover half the HP it lost, rounded down, to a maximum of 3 HP. No healing comes from undead, constructs or creatures without living vitality. Your eyes and veins visibly darken. When it ends, roll CON or gain the Sickly condition.

A decoction does not cure its own side effects. Conditions recover through the normal rules. These formulas are for the practitioner's own physiology; transferable decoctions require a separately designed recipe rather than assuming another character can drink them safely.

## Ingredients and Supply

**Common bundles:** Each bundle contains enough ordinary herbs and reagents for one use of an active technique. Ten minutes of deliberate foraging in suitable terrain and a successful Hunting & Fishing roll yields 1D3 bundles. A failed search requires a new location or a meaningful change in conditions before another attempt. You can also buy supplies. Six bundles fill one normal inventory slot; tools occupy their normal space.

**Rare doses:** Record the component and its source, such as bog-adder venom or troll bile. Ten minutes harvesting an accessible, suitable carcass and a successful Healing roll yields one dose unless the GM establishes a different yield beforehand. Failure spoils the available material; it is not an invitation to reroll on the same organ. A rare dose must be relevant to the intended recipe. Purchased or willingly supplied components can also qualify.

Preservation, tool requirements and prices depend on the material and circumstances. The GM should establish unusual requirements before preparation starts. Ingredients and brewed doses cannot be treated as an unlimited abstract supply.

## Working with a Slayer

When Anima and a [[Dolmir - Dragonbane/06 - Heroic Paths/Path of the Shadow/Slayer|Slayer]] jointly prepare a creature-specific oil or salve, use Slayer's Shared Preparation rule. One successful study can be shared; the Slayer does not repeat it merely to designate the same creature as a quarry. The compound still requires its own brewing roll, ingredients, time and Willpower.

Weapon Oil can benefit an attack that also uses Hunting Instincts and Prepared Strike. Each ability retains its own cost and restrictions. For example, an enhanced oil on a 2D6 weapon adds 1D6, and Prepared Strike adds another 1D6 for 2 Willpower. Neither bonus is multiplied by a Dragon result.

The oil does not grant a boon by itself. A salve provides its stated protection, not bonus damage. Cooperation grants no additional unspecified benefit.

## Design and Playtest Notes

- Field Brew covers immediate treatment; Targeted Compound rewards foreknowledge and equips allies; Decoction transforms the practitioner briefly at a known cost.
- This revision replaces the old undefined half-strength compounds with explicit common and enhanced effects. Enhanced oil keeps its extra weapon die but is limited to one attack per round.
- Blood Recovery replaces uncapped healing on every damaging effect with a capped melee trigger. It cannot be fuelled by area damage, harming oneself or damaging a non-living object.
- No recipe grants magical weapon status or bypasses an immunity unless specifically written to do so. A correct countermeasure can still interact with a creature's established vulnerability normally.
- Watch enhanced oils with extra-damage abilities and multiple party members. The brewing costs, three-dose limit and one-attack-per-round bonus are intentional constraints.
- The Green Wyrd's danger should arise from ingredients, known side effects and choices. Additional lasting mutations or escalating penalties require discussion, not an unwritten punishment for using a learned ability.
