This path is granted by the Gem of Sehan'nain, the last piece of the God Tree of Blessings.
Through the gem, its bearer learns to call on willing spirits and lingering memories of the blessing.

This path uses **Animism, willing participants and a connection awakened through the gem**. Each rank requires the previous rank and a separate Heroic Path advancement. These are proposed homebrew mechanics and lore for playtesting, inspired by the supplied Spirit Healer talent rather than a direct spell conversion.

#### Dedicated Passive:
**Vigil**
Gain a boon on Healing rolls made to give first aid to a dying creature you can physically reach. You must be conscious and able to tend to them normally.

This grants no additional action, automatic success, HP or protection against death. It does not replace tools or other first-aid requirements. This passive is granted only if Last Child is your dedicated path, following [[Heroic Paths]].

## Communion

### The Gem's Animism

While holding the active [[Gem of Sehan'nain]], treat your Animism as **13**, or use your own learned Animism if higher, for entering Communion and meeting this path's skill requirements. You do not need to have learned Animism to use this borrowed value.

The gem supplies a starting connection, not a bonus: it does not add 13 to your skill, grant ordinary Animism spells or provide Willpower. Pay all costs from your own WP. It does not automatically teach a path rank or bypass its deeds and advancement costs.

The borrowed value remains 13. To meet Journeyman's 15 and Master's 17 requirements, you must learn and develop your own Animism through the campaign's normal rules. Rolls using only the gem's borrowed value do not advance a skill you have not learned.

If the gem becomes dormant or leaves your hand, its borrowed Animism ends. You retain learned ranks, but must have your own Animism of at least 13 to enter or sustain Communion without it; otherwise Communion and its dependent effects end. A previously learned higher rank remains usable with your own Animism once the gem is unavailable; the 15 and 17 requirements are learning gates, not recurring activation checks.

On learning Novice, you gain the ability to enter Communion. Spend an action and 2 Willpower, then roll Animism. On success, a willing spiritual presence answers and you may use this path's active techniques for the rest of the combat, or ten minutes outside combat. Failure spends the Willpower but produces no Communion.

Golden leaves, a breeze or the scent of living earth may announce the presence. It is not a separate combatant, does not grant spells or actions, and cannot be ordered to scout or fight. You may end Communion without an action. It also ends if you become unconscious or ten minutes pass; it cannot be stacked or extended by activating it again while active.

All three ranks below require Communion. They use their stated actions and costs but require no additional Animism roll. Their ranges are measured from you and require an unobstructed line of effect. These are path abilities, not Animism spells; spell discounts and metamagic do not apply automatically. Normal restrictions on wielding magic, including metal equipment, remain in force.

A failed Communion is not automatically demonic possession. If a false spirit becomes part of the story, give discoverable warning signs and choices; the gem remains a surviving blessing, not a corrupting artifact.

#### Novice:
**Shared Life**
Willpower: 2 points per use
Requirement: Animism 13 (your own or supplied by the active gem), a meaningful connection awakened through the gem
Range: 10 meters

Spend an action to link yourself with one conscious, willing living creature within range. One of you donates HP and the other receives it.

Transfer up to 6 HP in total. Each point the recipient regains costs the donor 1 HP; the donor cannot be reduced below 1 HP, and the recipient cannot exceed its maximum HP. Neither creature can both donate and receive in the same use.

The donor's HP loss ignores armor and cannot be reduced, redirected, absorbed by protection or used to trigger healing, retaliation or another benefit from taking damage. The donor must freely agree to their contribution; an unconscious creature, bound spirit or unwilling captive cannot supply life.

This restores ordinary HP only. It does not revive a creature at 0 HP, cure an injury, end a curse or remove a condition. It cannot heal undead or constructs.

#### Journeyman:
**Recall**
Willpower: 3 points per use
Requirement: Animism 15, Novice, having stayed to aid someone at genuine personal risk
Range: 10 meters

Spend an action to reach a living creature at 0 HP whose death has not been established. Choose a conscious, willing donor within range, including yourself. They contribute up to 1D6 HP, determined by a single roll and capped by what they can give without falling below 1 HP. The recipient regains exactly that many HP, up to its maximum.

Apply Shared Life's consent and donation restrictions. Ordinary rules determine what regaining HP means for consciousness and death rolls; Recall grants no immediate action and does not erase critical injuries or their consequences.

The recipient must be willing to return. You cannot compel an unwilling soul, retrieve someone already dead or undo an injury that has made survival impossible. No applicable donor or no transferable HP means you cannot activate Recall.

This is the blessing reaching someone ordinary Shared Life cannot reach, not the creation of life from nothing.

#### Master:
**Life Ward**
Willpower: 4 points per use
Requirement: Animism 17, Journeyman, a major deed of freely given protection or sacrifice
Range: 10 meters

Once per combat, spend an action to ward one willing living creature in range, including yourself. The ward lasts until Communion ends, the combat ends, or the target moves more than 10 meters from you. You can maintain only one Life Ward.

The next time an attack would reduce the warded creature from above 0 HP to 0 HP, resolve armor and other protection first. If the attack is still lethal, the ward breaks and the creature remains at 1 HP instead. Any critical injury still occurs if the normal rules require it.

The ward prevents that one fall; it restores no additional HP and grants no action. It does not prevent instant death, revive a creature already at 0 HP, protect against poison, disease or environmental harm, or pay the cost of Shared Life, Recall, the gem's sacrifices or another self-inflicted ability.

A creature can benefit from only one Life Ward at a time, even from different practitioners. Replacing or losing a ward does not restore the once-per-combat use. This blessing cannot be stored on several people before combat; a ward prepared outside combat lasts no longer than the current Communion and still counts as your one use in the immediately following combat.

## Relationship with the Gem

The gem's mysteries and this path are separate rewards. Awakening a mystery can provide a suitable story milestone, but does not automatically grant a path rank; neither does buying a rank automatically unlock an artifact mystery. One deed still unlocks only one mystery, as stated in the gem note.

The suggested starting gate is awakening a mystery through genuine action and choosing to carry Sehan'nain's blessing onward. The title does not establish a literal genealogy or prove that no other blessing survives.

Once a rank is learned, it remains learned if the gem becomes dormant or passes to another bearer. Continuing to use the path independently requires your own Animism, as described under The Gem's Animism. Without the gem, the character cannot use the gem's own mysteries merely because they know this path.

- **Selfless Sacrifice:** The gem's existing single-target transfer remains useful without Communion or these additional Willpower costs. Shared Life allows transfer between you and one willing creature at range; it does not multiply HP.
- **Sehan'nain's Shelter:** Resolve the gem's damage reduction before checking whether Life Ward must trigger. If Shelter prevents the fall to 0 HP, Life Ward remains intact.
- **The First Blessing:** WP transfer follows the gem's own limits. This path does not produce or refund WP.
- **Roots Remember:** Memories revealed by the gem can guide a connection with a willing presence. They do not provide an unlimited supply of spirits with complete knowledge.

Using a learned technique does not automatically mark a gem Consequence. Consequences apply only when the artifact's own text explicitly calls for them or when the player knowingly agrees to an additional extraordinary blessing.

## Design and Playtest Notes

The progression follows the Spirit Healer's broad shape: establish Communion, share life, recall the dying and finally ward against a lethal blow. It uses path techniques instead of importing another game's spells or summoning difficulty numbers.

- **Synthesis** restores modest HP through ingredients and preparation; **Last Child** moves HP between willing people. Neither approach makes the other redundant.
- **Spirit Warrior** channels a spirit for personal combat power; **Last Child** uses Communion to sustain others.
- **Bone Tongue** draws on collected remains; this path does not need trophies or control over a spirit.
- Shared Life transfers up to 6 existing HP between the practitioner and one willing creature; it creates no additional HP.
- Life Ward is a powerful single rescue, constrained by setup, range and one use per combat. It is not resurrection or immunity to the remainder of an enemy's attacks.
- The temptation is giving too much of yourself. Do not predetermine that compassion corrupts the character or demand an endless succession of permanent penalties for normal ability use.
