The Still Gate

"The body is a question. The opponent is the answer. You learn to listen."

The Art

The Still Gate is not a school with a name above a door. It is a philosophy passed between those who have learned that violence does not have to mean ending — that the body can compel, redirect, and reveal without the blade. Its practitioners are few, its teachers fewer still. The Gate has roots in the travelling traditions of the Eldar, though it has long since spread into wandering soldiers, disgraced healers, and anyone who had cause to take a person apart without killing them.

It is built on Brawling, Iron Fist, and Iron Grip — the close work of hands, weight, and leverage. What the Path adds is awareness: of breath, of tension, of the small truths a body tells when the mind is trying to lie.

You are the unchanging threshold. Force flows through you, not against you.

Masters of the Still Gate

To learn the Still Gate, you must find a practitioner willing to teach. They are not found in guilds or academies. You gain their acceptance through deed, patience, or proof that you already understand the Gate's first principle: that restraint costs more than force, and is worth more too.

Each ability is granted when you reach a major story milestone and meet its prerequisites. You cannot advance abilities by rolling advancement marks — only through the fiction.

Heroic Abilities

The Hold Tier 1 | 3 WP | Brawling 14

Activation: When you succeed on a Brawling attack, you may forgo all damage to instead seize and pin the target.

Effect: The target is restrained — they cannot move or take actions until the end of your next turn.

Breaking Free: At the end of your next turn, the target may attempt a contested STR roll against your Brawling. On a success, they break free. On a failure, they remain restrained and may attempt to break free again at the end of each of their subsequent turns. On a Dragon roll in your favour, the target cannot attempt to break free until the start of their next turn.

This ability does not work on monsters that are large or massive.
Read the Pulse Tier 2 | 3 WP | Brawling 16, The Hold

Activation: When you have physical contact with a target — through a hold, a grapple, a strike, or any close moment of touch.

Effect: Roll AWARENESS. On a success, the GM tells you one true thing about the target:

  • What they intend to do next
  • What they are concealing or afraid of
  • Whether something they have said or shown is a lie
  • Their emotional state beneath their surface behaviour

On Failure: The GM tells you something — but decides whether it is accurate. You believe it either way. The GM should seed evidence of any deception into later scenes so the lie eventually unravels through the fiction, not through explicit correction.

On Push: You may ask one follow-up detail.

Once per scene. Works in social encounters as well as combat — a handshake or a steadying grip qualifies as contact.
The Turning Tide Tier 3 (Master Technique) | — | Brawling 18, Read the Pulse, master of exceptional rarity

Type: Passive Reaction (no WP cost)

Activation: When you would take damage from a melee attack, you may roll Brawling as a reaction.

On Success: You redirect the attacker's momentum — you take no damage, and the attacker suffers a bane on all actions until the end of their next turn.

On Dragon Roll: You may immediately initiate a free grapple or Hold against the attacker.

This is a passive ability with no WP cost. It may be used once per round.

Finding a Master

Masters of the Still Gate are defined by what they walked away from. Roll or choose:

D6 Background Personality Tutelage Condition
1 Retired Order soldier Stoic Prove you have shown restraint when force was easier
2 Eldar elder, Mitlands Patient Perform a service that has nothing to do with fighting
3 Wandering healer Generous Agree to teach what you learn to one other person
4 Disgraced inquisitor Just Demonstrate you understand why the Gate exists
5 Former pit fighter Impatient Survive a session of sparring without striking back
6 Hermit, origins unknown Suspicious Find them first — they do not announce themselves
