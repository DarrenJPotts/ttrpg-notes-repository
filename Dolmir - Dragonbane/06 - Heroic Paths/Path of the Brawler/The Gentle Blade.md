Nissail, an Eldar Elve'than swordsman and philosopher, developed the Gentle Blade after witnessing the wars of the First and Second Ages. Its practitioners favour agility over strength, using contact and an opponent's momentum to control a fight without unnecessary injury. The gentle blade is not a weapon, but a body trained to guide rather than cut.

Gentle Blade techniques use **Brawling and unarmed attacks**. Each rank requires the previous rank and a separate Heroic Path advancement. Learning requires a willing teacher and a significant story milestone; mastery requires an exceptional practitioner and a meaningful demonstration of restraint.

#### Dedicated Passive:
**Footwork**
	Once per round during your movement, you may pass through the space of one enemy roughly your size or smaller, even if they attempt to block you. You cannot end your movement in their space. Normal movement costs and free-attack rules still apply. This does not allow you to pass through solid obstacles or move away from a target while maintaining a Hold.

#### Novice:
**Hold**
Willpower: 2 points per Hold
Requirement: Brawling 14

You turn a strike into a controlling grip. After a successful unarmed Brawling attack has overcome the target's defense, you may spend 2 Willpower and forgo all damage to establish a Hold.

While held, the target is restrained and cannot move or take actions, including attacks or active defenses. You cannot move away from it or drag it along. At the end of each of its turns, the target may roll STR or Brawling to escape without taking an action; on a success, the Hold ends. This roll is not opposed.

Maintaining the Hold requires one free hand but no action or additional Willpower. You may attack the held target with a free hand or limb, following the normal action costs and attack rules. The Hold grants no boon to these attacks. You may still defend while maintaining the Hold, but your defensive rolls suffer a bane and retain their normal action costs. You cannot use the hand maintaining the Hold to parry, and any defensive movement that separates you from the target ends the Hold. You cannot use the hand maintaining the Hold to attack or establish another Hold without releasing it. You may release it at any time without an action. The Hold ends if you become unconscious or otherwise unable to maintain your grip, or if you are forcibly separated.

You can only hold one creature at a time. It must be roughly your size or smaller and have a body you can physically grip. Giants, swarms and incorporeal creatures cannot be held with this technique.

#### Journeyman:
**Control**
Willpower: 2 points per use
Requirement: Brawling 16

You feel an opponent's intentions through the movement of their body. Once per round, when you establish a Hold or at the start of your turn while holding someone, you may spend 2 Willpower without an action. Until the start of your next turn, the target's next attempt to escape your Hold suffers a bane.

This benefit ends if the Hold breaks.

Outside combat, you may instead spend an action and 2 Willpower while touching someone and roll Awareness. On a success, the GM reveals a physical sign of tension, fear, pain or readiness to act. On a failure, the signs are inconclusive. This does not reveal thoughts or establish whether someone is lying.

#### Master:
**Redirect**
Willpower: 3 points per use
Requirement: Brawling 18

You turn an enemy's attack into an opportunity to control them. Once per round, immediately after successfully dodging a melee attack from an adjacent opponent, you may spend 3 Willpower to establish a Hold on that opponent or reposition them.

Establishing the Hold requires a free hand but no attack roll or additional Willpower. Apply all the Novice Hold's normal restrictions and escape rules.

If you reposition the opponent instead, move them up to 2 meters to an unoccupied space adjacent to you. You may then move into the space they vacated. Neither movement triggers free attacks from that opponent. You cannot move either creature through an obstacle or into an immediate hazard.

Redirect deals no damage and grants no additional attack. The dodge retains its normal action cost. This technique only works on opponents you could Hold; it cannot redirect ranged attacks, area effects or attacks that cannot normally be dodged.
