Reavers have learned to channel the power of blood, making them terrifying and deadly warriors. The nature of the Reavers means that few truly heroic individuals seek to join their ranks, though desperate times some times push someone to take up the role of Reaver to fight some darker power. Due to its connections to blood magic, becoming a Reaver incurs the risk of being corrupted by demonic forces.

Reaver techniques work with **unarmed attacks and melee weapons**. Each rank requires the previous rank and a separate Heroic Path advancement.

#### Dedicated Passive:
**Blood sense** 
You sense the presence and approximate direction of exposed blood within **10 meters**, including dried traces spilled within the past month. Cleaning away all traces removes the scent.

You can also sense **actively bleeding creatures** within this range, but not blood contained within an uninjured body.

This sense cannot identify an individual, determine their exact location, or remove penalties for attacking an unseen target. Solid barriers block it.

#### Novice:
**Life Siphon**
- Willpower: 3 points to start, 1 per round continued
You can draw an enemy’s life force into you through an attack, healing you even as they weaken. For a number of consecutive turns if maintained with Willpower, you gain Health equal to the amount of damage the target suffers on a successful attack, up to a maximum of 3.

Undead, constructs and creatures without living vitality provide no healing. 

#### Journeyman: 
**Aura of Pain**
- **Willpower Points:** 2 to activate
- **Activation:** One action
- **Duration:** Until the combat ends, you become unconscious, or you dismiss it without an action

When activating the aura, and subsequently **once on each of your turns**, you may unleash a pulse of pain without another action.

For each pulse:

1. Spend **1 additional WP**.
2. Suffer **1D6 damage that ignores armor**.
3. Each enemy within **4 meters** suffers **1D6 damage that ignores armor**. Roll once for all affected enemies.

The pulse needs no attack roll and cannot be dodged or parried. Solid barriers block it.

**Restrictions:**

- You must remain above 0 HP after paying the self-damage. Roll that damage first; if it would reduce you to 0 HP, the pulse fails and you instead fall to **1 HP**.
- The self-damage cannot be reduced, redirected or paid with temporary protection.
- Neither the self-damage nor the aura’s damage triggers Devour Life.
- Maintaining the aura costs no action, but every pulse has its own costs

#### Master:
Death’s Momentum
- **Willpower Points:** 2
- **Activation:** Immediately after your melee attack kills an enemy
The death of your foes lends speed to your step and swiftness to your reflexes. Immediately after you kill a foe in combat, you can make another attack against a different foe in range as a free action. If no foe is in range, you may instead perform a free Move action to put you in range of a new target. If you kill your foe with the free attack, you do not get another free attack or move.
