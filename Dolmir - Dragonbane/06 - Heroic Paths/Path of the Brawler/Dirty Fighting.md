Dirty fighters do not play fair or follow rules of honour. They strike the knee to collapse opponents, the fingers around a weapon and the eyes behind an open visor. Capitalizing on the parts an opponent cannot defend.

Dirty Fighting techniques use **Brawling and unarmed attacks**. Each rank requires the previous rank and a separate Heroic Path advancement. These are proposed homebrew mechanics for playtesting.

#### Dedicated Passive:
**Exploit Weakness**
You recognise visible signs that an opponent is favouring an injured limb or struggling with impaired movement, grip or sight. If such signs are observable, the GM tells you which body area appears affected, but not the creature's HP, injury threshold or hidden vulnerabilities. This does not detect concealed injuries or grant a combat bonus.

#### Novice:
**Dirty Trick**
Willpower: 2 points per targeted attack
Requirement: Brawling 14

Before making an unarmed Brawling attack, spend 2 Willpower and choose a reachable body area. If the attack hits, it strikes that area without an additional hit-location roll or aiming penalty. Resolve damage and defenses normally.

If the target suffers at least 1 HP of damage after armor and other reductions, apply the corresponding effect:

- **Leg Strike:** The target's movement is halved until the end of its next turn.
- **Arm Strike:** The target suffers a bane on its next attack or parry using the struck arm, before the end of its next turn.
- **Head Strike:** The target suffers a bane on its next skill roll relying on sight, before the end of its next turn.
- **Body Strike:** The target suffers a bane on its next dodge roll, before the end of its next turn.

These effects do not stack with themselves; applying the same effect again refreshes its duration. A target can suffer different effects at once. The target must have the relevant anatomy, and you must be able to reach and affect it. A head strike cannot blind an eyeless creature, for example. A miss, successful defense or hit that deals no HP damage produces no effect, but still costs the Willpower.

#### Journeyman:
**Follow Through**
Willpower: 2 additional points per use
Requirement: Brawling 16

Once per round, after your Dirty Trick attack hits and overcomes any defense, but before rolling damage, you may spend 2 additional Willpower to add 1D6 damage to the attack. Resolve the combined damage against armor normally. The additional die is not multiplied by a Dragon result.

If the target suffers at least 1 HP of damage, apply Dirty Trick's normal impairment. You may also move the target up to 2 meters into an unoccupied space adjacent to you, then optionally step into the space it vacated. The target must be roughly your size or smaller; no comparison of damage bonuses is required.

Neither movement triggers free attacks. Normal terrain restrictions apply, and neither creature can be moved through an obstacle or into an immediate hazard. Your optional step is additional movement and does not use your movement allowance.

The impairment remains active for its normal duration; Follow Through does not consume it. The bonus damage can still apply against a target too large to reposition, provided Dirty Trick can affect it.

This technique requires no additional action and grants no additional attack. Its movement replaces any ordinary shove from the same hit; you cannot apply both. The total cost of Dirty Trick with Follow Through is 4 Willpower.

#### Master:
**Combination Strike**
Willpower: 4 points per targeted attack
Requirement: Brawling 18

Once per round, before making an unarmed Brawling attack, spend 4 Willpower and choose two different reachable body areas. On a successful hit, choose one of those areas as the actual hit location and resolve damage against its armor normally. If the target suffers at least 1 HP of damage, apply the Novice effects for both chosen areas.

This represents a rapid combination resolved as one attack. Roll damage only once; it grants no additional attack and does not deal damage twice. Both areas must be anatomically suitable and reachable.

This technique replaces Dirty Trick for that attack; do not pay both activation costs. You cannot use Follow Through on the same attack. The effects keep their normal durations and can create openings for later attacks.

## Using the Body-Part Rules

Inspired by [[Dolmir - Dragonbane/07 - Resources/Heroic Abilities/Rules for Body Parts.pdf]], especially its Precision Strike ability and distinction between hit locations and injuries.

This sub-art uses its own brief tactical effects rather than reproducing the PDF's damage thresholds and longer-lasting temporary injuries. You do not need the full supplement to use it.

If using both systems, attacks made with Dirty Trick or Combination Strike apply the effects above **instead of** the supplement's optional temporary-injury effects. Ordinary HP damage and critical injuries still resolve normally. Untargeted attacks use your usual hit-location and injury rules.

Selecting a body part does not bypass armor, guarantee a critical injury, sever a limb or automatically disable a monster's attacks. A bane affects a roll only; it does not make an automatic monster attack start rolling to hit.

## Playtest Notes

- **Gentle Blade** controls where an opponent can go; **Dirty Fighting** disrupts what they can do next; **Reaver** sustains the attacker through damage.
- Check whether the 2-Willpower Novice effects feel worthwhile without becoming an automatic purchase on every attack.
- Watch head targeting: its broad applicability may make it preferable to arms or torso. Armor, anatomy and the single-roll duration should matter.
- Exploit Weakness is observational utility, not supernatural diagnosis. Its value depends on the GM making visible injuries apparent in descriptions.
