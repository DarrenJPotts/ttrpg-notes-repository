### A Heroic Path for Dragonbane

> *"Fight fast, move, survive."*

---

## The Art

"The Eldar Elve'than swordsman and philosopher Nissail created this form from his observations of the war during the First and Second ages, and his part in it. The style favours speed and agility over strength of blows. The Gentle Blade affords the opponent no time to strike. As Nissail wrote, 'War ravages, blowing winds of despair to enemy and ally. The ideal Swordsman is one that can not be hurt, nor requires the blade to pierce true. The blade should flow, guide and control before death.' This form is fluid and no two wielders use it the same, moulded by both the form itself and the user.

---

## Masters of the Nissail - The Gentle Blade

To learn Nissail - The Gentle Blade, you must find a practitioner willing to teach. They are not found in guilds or academies. You gain their acceptance through deed, patience, or proof that you already understand the Gate's first principle: that restraint costs more than force, and is worth more too.

Each ability is granted when you reach a major story milestone and meet its prerequisites. You cannot advance abilities by rolling advancement marks — only through the fiction.

Each ability takes one shift to learn and must be rolled for with an advancement mark at the end of the session. Roll under your Brawling skill to succeed. You cannot advance to the next ability until you have learned the previous one.

**Master Techniques** (Tier 3) can only be learned after learning both prior abilities. They require a master of exceptional rarity — someone who has walked the Form long enough to have nearly left violence behind altogether.

## Heroic Abilities

### The Blade that Flows, Holds
- **Requirement:** Gentle Blade
- **Willpower Points:** 3

Activate when succeeding on a **Gentle Blade** attack. You forgo all damage to instead maneuverer and pin a target. The target is **restrained** and they cannot move or take actions until the end of your next turn.

At the end of your next turn, the target may attempt a contested **STR** roll against your **Gentle Blade**. On a success, they break free. On a failure, they remain restrained and may attempt to break free again at the end of each of *their* subsequent turns. On a Dragon roll in your favour, the target cannot attempt to break free until the start of their next turn.

*This ability does not work on monsters that are large or massive.*

---

### Read the Pulse
- **Requirement:** Brawling 16, The Hold
- **Willpower Points:** 3

Activate when you have physical contact with a target — through a hold, a grapple, a strike, or any close moment of touch. Roll **AWARENESS**. On a success, the GM tells you one true thing about the target:

- What they intend to do next
- What they are concealing or afraid of
- Whether something they have said or shown is a lie
- Their emotional state beneath their surface behaviour

On a failure, the GM tells you something — but decides whether it is accurate. You believe it either way. *The GM should seed evidence of any deception into later scenes so the lie eventually unravels through the fiction, not through explicit correction.* On a push, you may ask one follow-up detail.

*Once per scene. Works in social encounters as well as combat — a handshake or a steadying grip qualifies as contact.*

## Master Technique

### The Turning Tide
- **Requirement:** Brawling 18, Read the Pulse, master of exceptional rarity
- **Willpower Points:** —

You have learned to move with a target rather than against them. When you would take damage from a melee attack, you may roll **Brawling** as a reaction. On a success, you redirect the attacker's momentum — you take no damage, and the attacker suffers a **bane** on all actions until the end of their next turn. On a Dragon roll, you may immediately initiate a free grapple or Hold against the attacker.

*This is a passive ability with no WP cost. It may be used once per round.*

| D6 | Background | Personality | Tutelage Condition |
|----|-----------|-------------|-------------------|

| 3 | Wandering healer | Generous | Agree to teach what you learn to one other person |
| 4 | Disgraced inquisitor | Just | Demonstrate you understand why the Form exists |
| 5 | Former pit fighter | Impatient | Survive a session of sparring without striking back |
| 6 | Hermit, origins unknown | Suspicious | Find them first — they do not announce themselves |
