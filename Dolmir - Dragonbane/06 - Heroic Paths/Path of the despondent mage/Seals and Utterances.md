Practitioners of Seals and Utterances study how magic is formed rather than committing themselves to a single kind of spell.  Every alteration draws additional Willpower, and an incorrectly formed spell is no safer because its caster understands the theory.

This path modifies **learned spells cast through a magic-school skill**. Each rank requires the previous rank and a separate Heroic Path advancement. Requirements must be met in at least one magic school. These are homebrew mechanics for playtesting.

#### Dedicated Passive:
**Notation**
You can read the structure of an unfamiliar magical inscription well enough to recognise whether it appears to describe a spell, contain a working, or serve as a warning or instruction. The inscription must be visible, intact enough to examine and actually magical in construction.

This does not translate an unknown language, reveal the spell's identity or trigger, detect hidden magic, disarm a ward or teach its spell. Further interpretation still requires appropriate skills, magic or research. You do not need to activate or touch an inscription to examine it.

#### Novice:
**Utterance**
Willpower: 2 additional points per casting
Requirement: Any magic-school skill 13

When casting a spell, before making its normal casting roll, spend 2 additional Willpower and choose one alteration:

- **Range:** Double a numerical range of at least 2 meters. The spell still requires its normal perception and line of effect. Self and touch spells are unaffected; an area does not become larger.
- **Duration:** Double a fixed duration of at least one round and no longer than one stretch. Instantaneous, permanent and concentration-based durations are unaffected. This does not reduce upkeep costs or prevent the spell ending through its normal conditions.
- **Precision:** Exclude one creature you can perceive from an area spell's effects when it resolves. Its space remains in the area, and other creatures there are affected normally. This does not protect against later ordinary fire, falling debris or other secondary hazards caused by the spell.

The alteration requires no separate action or roll. Apply only one alteration, once, to a casting. It does not change the spell's power level, rank, damage, healing or number of targets except for Precision's exclusion.

You must speak the modifying utterance audibly, even if the original spell does not require a Word. All original casting requirements still apply. If the spell fails, the additional Willpower is lost along with its normal cost.

#### Journeyman:
**Seal**
Willpower: 2 additional points, plus the spell's normal cost
Requirement: Any magic-school skill 15

You may prepare one spell in a written seal on a small, non-magical object you can carry and mark. The spell must normally take one action to cast, resolve instantaneously, and not summon a creature, create another stored spell or restore Willpower. Magic tricks cannot be sealed.

Creating the seal takes ten minutes, or the spell's normal casting time if longer. Choose the spell and its power level, fulfil its normal casting requirements and pay its normal Willpower cost plus 2. Consumed ingredients are used now. Do not make the casting roll yet. You may prepare a learned but unreadied spell using its grimoire; you must still have access to the recorded spell during preparation.

Later, spend an action to touch and break the seal. Choose the spell's targets and make its normal magic-school roll, using the power level already paid for. Measure range and area from you, not from an object placed elsewhere. The spell requires its normal line of effect and valid targets.

Releasing the spell requires no additional Willpower and replaces its Word, Gesture, Focus and Ingredient requirements with touching and breaking the seal. It does not remove other restrictions, including the prohibition on casting while equipped with metal. Only you can release your seal, and you must be conscious and able to touch and break it.

You may have one seal at a time. It expires at the start of your next stretch rest, when destroyed or when you begin creating another. It is consumed whether the release roll succeeds or fails. Expiration or failure refunds no Willpower. It is not a proximity trap, a transferable scroll or a way to stockpile magic over several rests.

You cannot apply Utterance when preparing or releasing a Seal. The Master rank below changes this restriction.

#### Master:
**Compound Casting**
Willpower: 3 additional points per casting
Requirement: Any magic-school skill 17

When casting a spell normally, you may spend 3 additional Willpower instead of Utterance's usual 2 to apply two different Utterance alterations to that casting. Make one normal casting roll. You cannot apply the same alteration twice.

Alternatively, when releasing a Seal, you may spend 2 Willpower before its casting roll to apply one Utterance alteration. This requires an audible utterance and follows that alteration's normal restrictions. The seal's preparation costs have already been paid; they are not paid again.

Choose one of these benefits per casting. You cannot apply two alterations to a sealed spell. Compound Casting grants no extra spell, action, power level or casting roll. Failure still consumes the spell's full cost and any seal used.

## Limits and Other Paths

- Choose the spell's power level normally and pay its normal cost before adding these surcharges. Extra Willpower spent here does not increase power level.
- Magic tricks, Slayer Signs, item activations, rituals and the Scelus's Words are not eligible unless separately adapted and agreed with the GM. This path does not turn the Scelus's CON-based Words into ordinary spells.
- Alterations do not remove immunity, invent a valid target, bypass a solid barrier or grant knowledge of a spell.
- If another ability alters the same spell's range or duration, use the greater alteration rather than multiplying them together. Added costs are paid in full and cannot be discounted by another ability.
- Seal's advantage is access and discretion, not action compression: preparing an unreadied spell for later or releasing one without its usual words and gestures. It does not grant another spell preparation slot for ordinary casting.
- The dedicated passive is gained only if this is your dedicated path, following [[Heroic Paths]]. All three active ranks remain available through normal advancement.

## Playtest Notes

Novice broadens a spell rather than increasing its damage. Compare extended-range attacks and selective area spells with what other paths gain for 2 Willpower.

Journeyman should reward foresight without becoming a transferable magic-item factory. Keep the one-seal and next-rest limits explicit.

Master improves efficiency and allows one altered stored spell. If it feels too modest beside other Master techniques, test it before adding free casts, extra targets or instant casting; those multiply existing spell effects much more sharply.
