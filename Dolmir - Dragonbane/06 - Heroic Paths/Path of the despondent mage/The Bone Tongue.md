The Bone Tongue draws power from the traces living creatures leave in their flesh. Bone, hair, teeth and hide become anchors through which an animist calls on memory, instinct and hunger. Each piece adds another voice to the collection; some are harder to silence than others.

For [[Clawbert Danfangle]], this practice is bound to the book's whispers and his growing compulsion to collect. The book encourages him to see creatures as things that can be taken apart and understood. Whether he accepts that lesson remains a choice made in play.

Bone Tongue techniques use **Animism and physical collection pieces**. Each rank requires the previous rank and a separate Heroic Path advancement. The path does not grant Animism, new spells or access to the Scelus's Words. These are homebrew mechanics for playtesting.

#### Dedicated Passive:
**Residual Memory**
By handling a genuine collection piece for one minute, you can distinguish whether its strongest lingering impression is fear, aggression, contentment or distress, if any impression remains. The GM describes a brief sensation, not a complete memory.

This does not identify a creature, reveal how it died or establish whether it is currently alive. Ordinary remains may carry no useful impression. It cannot locate a creature, detect hidden magic or answer questions; those require an active technique or other magic.

#### Novice:
**Invocation**
Willpower: 2 points per use
Requirement: Animism 13, one significant collection piece

Spend an action holding a piece from your collection and roll Animism. Pay Willpower before rolling; failure has no effect. On success, choose one:

- **Senses:** For ten minutes, gain a boon on Awareness rolls that use one sense the source creature possessed, such as hearing or scent. You may also use its natural ability to see in darkness if it had one. This does not grant supernatural detection, sight through walls or perception through the source creature's current body. Choose the sense when activating.
- **Memory:** Ask one question about something the source creature experienced before the piece was separated from it. The GM gives a truthful answer limited by its knowledge and intelligence. Impressions may be visceral or fragmentary, but must contain usable information if the creature knew the answer. Otherwise, it cannot answer. The piece does not learn new information from a still-living source.
- **Harass:** Choose a visible creature within 10 meters. It rolls WIL; on failure, the invoked presence imposes a bane on its next skill roll before the end of its next turn. This does not remove an action, stack with another use of Harass or make an automatic monster attack roll to hit. Mindless creatures and creatures immune to mental influence are unaffected.

A collection piece is not consumed. The spirit or residual echo is not compelled to perform additional services. Senses cannot stack with another Senses invocation; a new use replaces the previous one. Memory cannot be repeated for the same question without new context.

#### Journeyman:
**Harvest**
Willpower: 3 points per use
Requirement: Animism 15, Brawling 13, five significant collection pieces

Once per round, after a successful unarmed Brawling attack has overcome the target's defense, you may spend 3 Willpower and forgo all damage to take a small, accessible physical sample: hair, blood, loose scales or a similar minor piece. No additional Animism roll is required.

The sample becomes a usable collection piece immediately. Until the end of your next turn, the target suffers a bane on its first defensive or resistance roll against one of your Animism spells or Harass invocations. If the effect normally allows no such roll, Harvest does not create one or provide another benefit. The bane is consumed by the first eligible roll and does not stack with another Harvest.

Harvest requires a free hand and physical access. It does not bypass armor or clothing that completely prevents access to the chosen sample. It cannot extract an organ, sever a limb, remove a functional tooth or cause a critical injury. Creatures without physical remains cannot be harvested.

Outside combat, you may take an offered sample from a willing creature or collect one from accessible remains without using this ability or paying Willpower. Those samples can still become collection pieces, but do not impose Harvest's resistance penalty. Ordinary consensual collection does not require an attack roll.

#### Master:
**Embodiment**
Willpower: 4 points per use
Requirement: Animism 17, Journeyman, a major story milestone involving the collection

Spend an action holding one significant collection piece and roll Animism. Pay Willpower before rolling; failure has no effect. On success, embody that piece's instincts for three rounds, including the round of activation. You can use Embodiment once per combat.

While active:
- Gain a boon on Animism casting rolls.
- Reduce the normal cost of each Animism spell by 1 Willpower, to a minimum of 1. This does not reduce the costs of Heroic Path abilities, items or metamagic surcharges.
- Gain Invocation's Senses benefit from the embodied piece, choosing one appropriate sense. This replaces, rather than stacks with, an existing Senses invocation and ends with Embodiment.

All spell requirements, action costs, valid-target rules and the normal restrictions on magic remain in force. Embodiment grants no extra attacks, spells or spell power levels. Invocation and Harvest are path abilities, not Animism spells; they receive neither the casting boon nor the discount.

When Embodiment ends, roll CON. On failure, gain the Exhausted condition, recovering through the normal rules. You remain responsible for your actions throughout; the collection's hunger does not automatically force you to attack allies or surrender control of the character.

## The Collection

A significant piece comes from a distinct creature and has a recorded source or meaningful connection to play. Five hairs from one creature are not five significant pieces. A purchased bone of unknown origin is not automatically a usable focus; establish its provenance through play or examination agreed with the GM.

Track each piece's source, form and any known sensory traits. A piece does not grant the source creature's statistics, spell list, resistances or attacks. Duplicates can replace a lost focus but do not multiply benefits.

Existing collection entries from the previous draft:
- Merry's claw: wolfkin, prized, associated with persistent whispers.
- The hermit's hide, preserved as a hat or cloak.
- The Old God bone: an unusual artifact whose properties must be established separately, not an unrestricted source of divine knowledge.
- Smaller pieces such as teeth, nails, hair and bones; record their sources before using them.

Keeping a focus accessible does not exempt the caster from normal requirements for hands, gestures, words or metal equipment. A lost or destroyed piece becomes unavailable; it does not remove learned ranks or disable the whole collection.

## Hunger and Advancement

The path can awaken when Clawbert learns Animism and deliberately takes a meaningful piece from another creature. Journeyman follows a consequential use of the collection in magic. Mastery requires confronting what the collection has cost him or someone else; it need not predetermine damnation or redemption.

The book can demand predatory acts, reward rationalisations and make keeping the collection increasingly uncomfortable. A cruel harvest should have consequences for the victim and the relationships around it. Consensual collection, scavenging and hunting are not automatically morally identical to mutilating a captive.

Keep whispers and urges vivid, but distinguish them from compulsory mechanics. Do not add indefinite banes, secret corruption thresholds or involuntary party attacks without agreeing on those stakes with the player. Redemption, resistance and further corruption remain possible outcomes, not a script.

## Design and Playtest Notes

- **Novice:** Gain information, borrow a sense or briefly disrupt an opponent. Harass replaces the previous scene-long bane on every action.
- **Journeyman:** Gain a collection piece and create a short opening for your magic. It sacrifices attack damage and cannot automatically maim a target.
- **Master:** Temporarily improve Animism rather than granting universal life detection or taking control away from the player.
- **Spirit Warrior** negotiates with an independent spirit; **Bone Tongue** invokes creatures through their remains. Invocation does not replace a willing spirit pact.
- **Seals and Utterances** changes a spell's structure. Embodiment improves normal Animism casting, but its discount cannot reduce that path's additional costs.
- The passive is gained only when Bone Tongue is the character's dedicated path, following [[Heroic Paths]]. All active ranks can be learned through normal advancement.
- Watch Embodiment with Animism spells that benefit strongly from repeated casting. Three rounds and the normal action economy are deliberate limits.
