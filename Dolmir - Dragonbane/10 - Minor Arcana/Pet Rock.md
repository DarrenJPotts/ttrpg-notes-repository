# Pet Rock & Pocket Pebbles

*companion, harmless, mysterious*

---

## The Pet Rock

A smooth, unremarkable stone about the size of a fist. It looks like any other rock you might find along a path, with a faint warm quality to its touch. Most people don't notice anything unusual about it at all—which suits it just fine.

The stone doesn't move much. Or at least, not when anyone is watching. It will sit in one spot for hours, weeks, sometimes days, before quietly shifting position when no one is looking. No one knows what it eats, or if it eats at all. It seems drawn to companionship more than anything else.

### Stat Block

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Ferocity** | 1 |
| **Size** | Tiny |
| **Movement** | 6 meters/round |
| **Armor** | 4 |
| **HP** | 5 |

### Abilities

**Stiff as Stone:** The pet rock is indistinguishable from a normal stone when motionless. A character must make an **AWARENESS** roll (difficulty 13) to realize it is alive. If the rock remains still for a full stretch of time, treat it as part of the environment.

**Unnoticed Movement:** If no creature is directly observing the pet rock, it may move up to its full movement as a free reaction at the end of another creature's turn. It crawls, rolls, or shifts quietly—gaining position without fanfare.

**Harmless Companion:** The pet rock cannot meaningfully participate in combat. If it takes damage, it stops moving and acts as an ordinary stone for the rest of the encounter. If someone picks it up and encourages it, it will begin moving again after a stretch rest.

### Attacks (D6)

| D6 | Attack | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **1–5** | **Nudge** | The pet rock bumps slowly into a target within 1 meter. Inflicts **1D4 bludgeoning damage** (laughably weak). Target is more amused than hurt. |
| **6** | **Determined Shove** | The pet rock musters unusual effort and rolls against a target with determination. Makes a melee attack against a single target within 2 meters. Inflicts **1D4 bludgeoning damage** and pushes the target **1 meter** back (if size permits). |

## Pocket Pebbles

The smaller cousin of the Pet Rock. A smooth pebble no larger than a fingernail, with the same faint warmth. Pocket Pebbles doesn't move on its own—it's perfectly content to rest in a pocket, pouch, purse, or small container indefinitely.

### Stat Block

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Ferocity** | — |
| **Size** | Microscopic |
| **Movement** | 0 |
| **Armor** | — |
| **HP** | 1 |

### Abilities

**Collectible Companion:** Pocket Pebbles is a tiny, inert companion. It fits in any pocket or container and weighs nothing. It does not participate in combat, cannot attack, and has no mechanical function beyond flavor.

**Mysterious Migrations:** At table, Pocket Pebbles occasionally shifts between different pockets, pouches, and containers without being noticed. Purely aesthetic. The GM can describe someone discovering it in an unexpected place—a gift from someone? A prank? A sign of good luck?

**At the Table (Out of Character):** If a player has Pocket Pebbles at the table, it can move around the table itself between stretches—ending up in surprising places, getting passed between players, or appearing in the GM's dice bag. Pure fun.

## Getting a Pet Rock

**Where do they come from?** Unknown. Perhaps they've always existed in quiet places—along streams, in old gardens, beneath temple steps. Perhaps something magical created them. The rocks themselves are not forthcoming on the matter.

**How to attract one:** Leave a quiet, undisturbed spot with a small offering (bread, herbs, water) for several days. If a Pet Rock decides you're worth following, it will be there next time you look. Don't force it. Rocks move at their own pace.

**Why have one?** Pet Rocks provide no tactical advantage, no magical power, and no practical benefit. They are companions for those who appreciate quiet company, small mysteries, and the comfort of something alive that asks nothing of you. In a campaign full of darkness and dying gods, a Pet Rock simply *is*—asking no questions, judging nothing, content to sit and exist alongside you.

---

## Tone & Use

These companions work best in a **grounded, atmospheric campaign** like the Dying Lands. They provide:

- **Comic relief** in dark moments (absurdity as balance)
- **Character flavor** — what does it say about someone who keeps a Pet Rock?
- **Mystery hooks** — where did it come from? Why does it follow them? Does something else like this exist?
- **Quiet moments** — the Pet Rock isn't demanding or needy; it just is

Use them sparingly. They're most effective when they matter *because* they don't do anything important.
