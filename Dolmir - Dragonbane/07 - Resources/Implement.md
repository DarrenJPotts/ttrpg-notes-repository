 
**Botanist V0.2** Requierments: Healing 12 or Bushcraft 12 WP: 2 You may spend a shift to look for 1d6 herbs. As an action, you may spend 2 WP and 2 units of herbs to create a concoction. The concoctions can be used during a streach rest to alter the effects of a healing roll on an ally. Multiple products can be applied to the same roll but multiple uses of the same concoction don't stack. Products: -_Flower Paste_- Turns the extra d6 healing into 2d4 instead. -_Sweet Leaf_- Turns the regular d6 WP regained into 2d4 instead. -_Rejuvenating Tea_- In addition to the regained hp the treated ally can remove an additional condition if any. -_Bundle of General Herbs_- This item can not be used in medicine but instead be sold for 5 silver. If an alchemist buys the bundle it counts as 2 General Herbs.
