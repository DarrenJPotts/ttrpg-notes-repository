## Core Rule

**If something isn't explicitly stated as prohibited, it's fair game.** — Tomas (Free League)

## Skills

**Can I push a failed push?** No, you can only push once per roll.

**Do multiple Boons/Banes stack?** Yes. Roll an additional D20 for each boon/bane, counting only the lowest/highest. Boons and banes cancel each other 1:1.

**I want to Hide?** Use Sneaking (p34).

**Weapon or Ride skill while mounted?** Use the lowest of Riding and weapon skill. Riding also replaces Evade when on horseback.

**What skill for listening?** Awareness (p33).

**Can I lock a door with Sleight of Hand?** Yes. Why not?

**Ability Check roll?** Roll under the attribute value directly (not untrained skill).

**Does Brawling count as a Weapon skill?** Yes (p35).

**Can I use different weapon skills for shields?** Yes. Any STR-based melee skill (not Knives/Staves) can parry with a shield and gain advancement marks.

**Which skill to follow a trail in a dungeon?** Depends on the player's approach: Hunting & Fishing (tracking), Spot Hidden (clues), Beast Lore (lair signs), Myths & Legends (monster lore). Or suggest a new Secondary Skill: Dungeoneering.

**Can I push a Bushcraft roll for camp/sleep?** Yes, but only one shot. If rest is interrupted, the pushed condition persists.

**Why would a player not Barter?** Risk of a Demon roll (no sale). GM can increase price on failure.

**When are Opposed rolls used?** Only where explicitly called out: Awareness, Persuasion, Sneaking, Topple, Disarm, Grapple, Poison (open), Disease (open), Dominate spell, Chess/Cards.

## Heroic Abilities

**Does Sneak Attack and Backstab damage stack?** No. Backstab *is* a sneak attack with a boon (not a bane), so they don't stack.

**Do Heroic Abilities use an action?** Yes, unless explicitly stated otherwise.

**Does any Weapon skill work for HA requirements?** Yes. If you have 12+ in any weapon skill, you qualify.

**Does HA have no Requirements at character creation?** Correct (p26).

**Does Weasel use an Action/Reaction?** No. It's free.

**Can I use Defensive or Fast Footwork even after using my action?** Yes (p36-37). Both are reactions with no action cost.

**Can Musician maximize effect by waiting to the end of the next turn?** Yes. Your turn moves with the initiative card swap, so all turn-triggered effects move too.

**What does Musician affect?** All rolls (skills and attributes) for allies within 10m when activated. Works outside combat too.

**Can Musician affect Monsters?** Yes, but monsters rarely use skills outside combat. Useful against dodge/parry (base 15) or special monster actions.

**Can the redirected Weasel target defend?** Yes, they can dodge/parry normally.

**Can I combo Heroic Abilities?** Generally yes, unless stated otherwise. Limited only by WP.

**Can I use Defensive, Fast Footwork, and Weasel against the same attack?** No (Errata 2.0). You can't try to dodge/parry the same attack twice. Weasel must be used *before* attempting dodge/parry.

**If a skill drops below 18, do I lose the HA?** No. You keep it and can gain another if the skill reaches 18 again.

**Iron Fist and Power Fist clash?** No longer (Errata 2.0). Iron Fist now adds +1D6 (not increases to 2D6), so it stacks with Power Fist.

**Does Berserker cost an action?** Unclear RAW. Reasonable to interpret activation + first attack as one action, then "keep fighting" as normal turns.

## Combat

**When does a Turn start?** Your turn starts when you move/act. Waiting can be used to shift initiative within the round.

**Can enemy NPCs swap initiative cards?** Yes. Monsters cannot (p41).

**Do Actions replenish at the start of each Round or Turn?** Start of each Round, though not explicitly spelled out.

**Running away from combat?** Free attacks trigger on failure to evade. Surviving foes can pursue next turn. Use opposed movement rolls to catch the fugitive.

**What happens on a Demon parry/dodge?** No rules, but suggest: extra D6 damage, no armor, weapon/item broken, new condition, enemy gets bonus damage die.

**Initiative: Needing More Than 10 Cards?** Group NPCs into initiative groups (p41) or use 13 cards from a standard deck's suit.

**In an Ambush, how do initiative cards work?** Failed ambush rolls get "bottom" cards (#10 down). Sort out low cards equal to number of failed characters, shuffle and deal those.

**Is diagonal movement allowed?** Yes (p44).

**Weapon size restrictions for Mallards/Halflings?** None mentioned. STR requirements are all that matter.

**Weapons with STR range in melee have 2m range?** Yes. Add "2/STR" to melee weapon table for clarity.

**Can I use a round rest outside combat?** Yes. It's not limited to combat situations (though mainly used there).

**Does Draw Weapon let me sheath for free?** Yes. "Put away" includes proper sheathing.

**What's the point of Shields?** 
- High durability (don't break easily)
- Only way to parry ranged attacks (without Deflect Arrow HA)
- Some monster attacks require shields
- Can be used as weapon (D8) if disarmed
- Works with Dual Wield HA

**Are Parry and Dodge opposed rolls?** No. Both attacker and defender spend actions, so it's not opposed (which only costs action for active party).

**Can I push Parry and Dodge rolls?** Yes. Push rules apply to any skill/attribute roll.

**Can a weapon break on armor?** Yes. If damage is completely negated by armor, the weapon takes damage instead (p50).

**Non-lethal attacks?** No rules. Common approach: declare intent, NPC at 0 HP is unconscious, not dead.

**Do I need Throwing Arm HA to throw weapons?** No. You can throw any weapon designed to be thrown. Throwing Arm lets you throw melee weapons.

**Can a prone character attack or free attack?** Yes. Prone doesn't prevent attacks, only makes ranged attacks harder to land.

**Does a prone character get cover bane on ranged attacks?** Not RAW, but suggested to give bane from distance.

**Can I aim with ranged weapons for a boon?** Not RAW, but reasonable to allow one round aiming for boon.

**Using ranged weapons while in cover?** Either bane on roll or "you can't target them." No shooting and keeping cover simultaneously.

**Can I Sneak Attack with ranged weapons?** Yes, but no extra damage die (only Subtle weapons get that).

**Can I sneak attack with a Long weapon from 4m?** Yes (Errata 2.0, p43).

**Can I Sneak Attack during combat?** No. Sneak attacks are pre-combat only. Use Backstab HA during combat.

**Can I peek, shoot, and duck back to cover same turn?** Yes, if you split movement. If attempting a hidden sneak attack after, roll Sneaking with bane to hide again (failure: all see you).

**Does extra Sneak Attack damage die increase damage bonus?** No. The extra die is separate.

**Do I roll for free attacks or auto-hit?** Always roll (p43).

**How many free attacks can I perform?** No limit per opponent, but limit of one free attack per fleeing enemy seems reasonable.

**Do involuntary moves trigger free attacks?** No (Errata 1.3, p43).

**If an NPC is within 2m of two PCs and flees?** Two separate Evade rolls (one vs each PC's free attack threat).

**How many Evades can a player do against free attacks?** No limit.

**Do Long weapons trigger free attacks at 4m?** RAW: No, only melee range (2m). Suggested: Yes, if you control that space.

**If I move but don't act, can I parry/dodge later?** No (Errata 2.0, p42). You must use your action and move on your turn.

**If I do nothing on my turn, can I parry/dodge later?** No (same as above).

## Grapple

**Why is breaking free a free action for the enemy?** Translation issue. It's an action for the grappled enemy, but not for the grappler.

**What's Break?** An unarmed attack with boon that can't be dodged/parried. Counts as normal unarmed attack.

**I'm grappled—can I cast spells with Word requirement?** No ruling. Depends on how much freedom/concentration casting requires.

**Grapple with no actions left—do they go free?** No. Maintaining the grip is a free action.

**Can Find Weak Spot be used for ranged weapons?** No, melee only. (Suggested: two banes on ranged use for cool moments.)

## Helping Others in Combat

Declare assistance before the assisting character's turn and flip their card. Both characters then act with boon on the same initiative card. If either dies before they act, the assist is lost.

## Reactions & Movement

**Using a reaction loses Move?** Yes, you lose movement that round, but dodge/parry grant 2m of free movement.

**Do parry/dodge movements trigger free attacks?** No (p46-47).

**Can parry movement push opponent into danger?** RAW: Yes. Suggested: Declare intent before rolling parry; normal parry wouldn't force danger.

**If a PC dodges and moves 2m away, do remaining attacks miss?** Yes, if now out of range.

**Can ranged crits be parried by shields?** Yes (Errata 2.0, p49).

## Damage, Poison & Death

**Order: Resistance vs Armor?** Armor first (target is under the armor).

**Object AR/HP table:** Chain 9/20, Barrel 3/10, Chest 5-10/20-25, Door 5-6/10-20, Padlock 5/20, Gate 12-25/40-100, Portcullis 10/30.

**Unarmed punch into plate armor?** Suggested: Attacker takes D4 damage.

**Prone + Area of Effect = Extra D6 damage?** Only for melee attacks (p50), not area spells.

**Does armor protect against fire?** Yes, unless the spell/attack explicitly says no (Dragon fire exception).

**Fire damage?** No general rules. Torch = normal melee weapon, can roll to extinguish. Improvised torch = 2D6 fire auto-hit (one-use).

**Poison Antidote stats?** Missing from equipment. No official ruling.

**How does Poison work with open opposed rolls?** Both roll; if tied or target lower, target gets full effect. PC can push their roll.

**How does Disease work with open opposed rolls?** Both roll daily; PC wins if lower/tied. If diseased, roll each day, taking D6 damage per failure. One success = fully cured.

**Lethal Poison doesn't kill?** Wrong. Poison deals 1D6/round until you reach 0 HP, then triggers death rolls.

**Can Monsters be poisoned?** No (p52).

**Cured from Poison after death rolls?** Yes. Poison stops at 0 HP.

**Can a character rally multiple times in combat?** Yes. Death rolls reset each rally.

**Can 0 HP character suffer instant death?** Yes, if damage ≥ max HP.

**Does new damage auto-fail a death roll?** Yes, one failed death roll per damage instance.

**Death rolls reset on 3 successes?** Yes (Errata 2.0, p50).

**Death rolls with Sickly condition?** Bane applies.

**Continue death rolls after combat ends?** Yes, keep rolling in round increments until resolved. Other PCs can help with Healing rolls (no bane outside combat).

## Magic

**How to become a Mage after creation?** Learn Magic Talent HA, find a teacher, study 1 week, CON roll. Success = base INT in that school (p61).

**Mage base skill if not trained?** Mages start with one school as trained (doubled from INT). Other schools = 0 base until trained (p25, p61).

**Learning spells beyond base INT limit?** Grimoire holds INT number of prepared spells. Lose grimoire = lose prepared spells.

**Upper limit to spell power?** Power Levels 1-3 (2-6 WP cost), or 2 WP fixed spells. Magic Tricks = 1 WP, no power level.

**Power from body with 1 WP left?** Yes (Errata 1.3, p58). Roll chosen die for WP gained and take same damage.

**Is Power Level = Rank?** No. Power level is chosen when casting; Rank is just lore tier (p57).

**Can Power from body kill you?** Yes. Damage can exceed HP.

**AOE on gridded maps (Circle/Sphere/Cone)?** Unclear RAW. Sphere 4m = 2 squares each direction. Suggestion: simplicity over accuracy.

**Spell range on 2×2 grid?** 2m = 1 square. 10m = 5 squares (don't count your own).

**Cast Protector on caster?** Yes (Errata 2.0, p62).

**Spells targeting Person—can caster use on self?** Probably, but Protector got explicit errata. No general ruling.

**Protector power 3 = immunity to magic?** No. Magic attacks need 4+ hit dice to affect the target.

**What's a monster magical attack?** Undefined by RAW. Suggest tagging monsters individually (Fire Breath, Curse, Cold Strike, etc.).

**Enchant Weapon + Dragon roll = advancement marks?** Yes, rolls of 1–2 count as dragon rolls (p63).

**Teleport combo—100m up, Slow Fall, unwilling target?** No. Unwilling targets require opposed checks or spells that say they resist.

**Can I Shatter enemy armor?** No. Armor has no durability listed.

**Touching unwilling enemy with spell in combat?** Opposed roll suggested (Brawling vs Evade). Otherwise restricted to out-of-combat use.

**Why no magic self-heal?** Errata'd in 2.0. Treat Wound, Heal Wound, Restoration can now target caster.

**Spells vs disease/poison?** No spells in core box.

**Firestorm prerequisites (comma-separated)?** Yes, both needed. "Fireblast **and** Whirlwind" (Errata 2.0, p68).

**Casting spells while in melee = bane?** No (Fenhorn ruling). Melee bane only applies to ranged weapons.

**Does metal affect spells already cast (like Flight)?** No. Spell already cast is unaffected.

**Enchant Weapon touching metal?** Don't hold the weapon during casting; touch it only at the end when discharging the spell.

**Metal floor/room blocking spells?** No clear rule. GM decides per table style (realistic vs rule-of-cool).

**Magic Trick requirements (words or gestures)?** Gesture (Errata 2.0, p58). GM can require word for stealth detection.

**Magic Trick Flick abusing?** Creates invisible killer potential. Suggested: require audible gesture; allow Awareness rolls to detect.

**Light on objects someone carries?** Only focus items. Light refers to focus, so limited by carried focus count. Chalk counts as focus (suggested: remove chalk from focus list).

**Whisper a Word-requirement spell?** No (Errata 2.0, p58). Cannot whisper.

**Cast spells undetected?** No ruling. Suggestion: spell effects visible/audible; once cast you're exposed.

## Monsters

**Monster skills (Awareness/Sneak)?** Monsters have default 15 for dodge/parry. Sneaking/Awareness range 12–16 or custom by GM (p84).

**Do monsters trigger free attacks when moving away?** Yes. Monsters have Evade 15 (default).

**Do Monsters get Free Attacks?** Yes (Errata 1.3, p43). Perform a monster attack.

**Do Monster Abilities trigger free attacks?** Only if moving *away* (not around or during special abilities). Pouncing/swinging during special movement doesn't trigger.

**Monster size on battle map?** Large dominates 4×4m area (2 squares). Monster occupies the "surface" it controls.

**Ferocity clarification:** Ferocity = multiple initiative cards. Ferocity 2 = draw two cards, one action each. Dodge/parry costs one turn.

**Limits to Ferocity?** No limit. Monster can have Ferocity 1 per PC (−1) without cap.

**Monsters with no Ferocity?** Orks/Goblins function as NPCs mechanically (one init card).

**Do all Monsters do Fear attacks?** No. Fear attacks are listed when they apply.

**Can Monsters move every turn?** Yes (p83, movement per turn not per round).

**When do Monsters roll attacks?** GM rolls or selects at start of monster turn, then moves before/after.

**Monster holding/swallowing PCs + acting?** Yes, holding is free. Monster can still act with Ferocity cards if logically able.

**Darkness and Vision:** Only monsters can see dark (unless stated). Nocturnal = twilight vision, some have Heat Vision (short-range), some have Dark Vision.

## NPCs

**NPC Damage Bonus notation?** "AGL: +D4" means +D4 based on Agility (not AGL + D4).

**Do NPCs rarely make attribute rolls?** Yes. Mainly use CON/WIL vs spells. Calculate attributes only if needed (p106).

**Low NPC skill values (5)?** Correct. NPCs have 5 in untrained skills, double for trained. GM doesn't roll for many actions—decide narratively.

**Poison vs NPC with Robust HA?** Roll against max HP −2 per Robust level (effective CON).

## General Discussion

**Camping & Bushcraft:**
1. Find safe location (GM grants boon for particularly good spot)
2. Divide watches (optional; all sleeping = vulnerable)
3. Each sleeper rolls BUSHCRAFT (bane without gear, boon with tent, normal with bedroll)
4. Success = good sleep; failure = try again next shift
5. 6 hours sleep (any combination) avoids Exhausted

**Opposed Roll balance issues:** RAW favors lower-rolling active party. Design choice for simplicity. If math bothers you: use Pendragon rule (highest under skill), or count difference (higher difference wins). Or embrace it as feature (underdogs get chance).

**Magic and Metal:** Mage can't use magic in direct contact with metal (except tiny items like buttons). Sheathed swords OK. Item in backpack OK. Full metal room blocks casting. **Key ruling:** Touch the metal only at the end of casting to discharge spell (e.g., Enchant Weapon).

**Skill roll spam:** Not inherently wrong but can dilute stakes. Solution: consequence for each roll (time passing, difficulty increasing, risk of Demon roll). Rolls that lack risk (Beast Lore) benefit from all PCs trying; high-stakes rolls (persuasion, lockpicking) should limit attempts.

**Balancing encounters:** 
- Fewer enemies = PCs favored
- Equal numbers = depends on tactics/equipment
- 1.25–1.5× enemies = enemies favored
- 2× enemies = hard fight
- 3×+ enemies = likely TPK
- Use Ferocity as a balance knob

**Slashing vs Piercing:** Piercing ignores armor / gets crits. Slashing breaks weapons / can multi-hit (HA). Bludgeoning knocks out. No inherent "best" damage type, though piercing currently most versatile.

**Selling looted gear:** No official rule. Suggested: damaged/poor quality gear sells for 1/4–1/2 value (half if fixed). Bartering rolls can raise price. Can't carry much, so inventory becomes the limit.

**Free Attack name:** Confusing term. Suggestion: Opportunity Attack, Disengage Attack, or Melee Reaction.

**Parry/Dodge placement:** Should be Reactions, not Actions (p42 confuses).
