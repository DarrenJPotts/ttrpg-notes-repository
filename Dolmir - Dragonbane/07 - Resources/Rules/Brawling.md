# Brawler's Den Rules

Rounds: Both fighters secretly choose one move, then reveal together. Each round is a full exchange of fighting, not one literal punch. Resolve moves, update scores, then resolve crowd influence.

Victory: First to score 3 hits, knock out their opponent, or force a surrender wins. No round limit. Some rounds have no scoring hit.

## Stats
Attack: Agile Brawling / Brawling
Defense: Evade
Saving throw: CON

## Moves
Attack: Roll attack. A winning attack scores 1 hit.

Evade: Roll Evade. Success stops the opponent's move. No scoring hit.

Counter: Roll Evade with BANE against any offensive move, including Trip. Success stops their move and grants one attack roll. A successful counterattack scores 1 hit. Failure lets their move land if their attack roll succeeds. No countering counters.

Stun: Roll attack with BANE. If it wins, no scoring hit, but the opponent must choose Evade next round. They still roll to defend normally.

Trip: Roll attack. No scoring hit. A successful Trip beats a normal Attack even if the Attack rolled lower. If Trip wins, gain BOON on your attack roll next round. Other offensive moves contest Trip normally.

Liver Shot: Roll attack with BANE. If it wins, score 1 hit and the opponent's attack rolls have BANE next round. This is temporary, not the Exhausted condition.

## Move Resolution
Offensive moves: Attack, Stun, Trip, Liver Shot.

| Moves chosen                       | Resolution                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Offensive vs offensive             | One success beats failure. If both succeed, lower roll wins. Only the winning move takes effect. |
| Trip vs normal Attack              | Successful Trip wins regardless of the Attack roll. If Trip fails, a successful Attack scores.   |
| Evade vs offensive                 | Successful Evade stops the move. If Evade fails, a successful offensive roll takes effect.       |
| Counter vs offensive               | Successful Evade with BANE stops the move, then roll one counterattack.                          |
| Evade / Counter vs Evade / Counter | No effect and no score.                                                                          |

Ties or both failing: No effect and no score.

Scoring: Only Attack, Liver Shot, and counterattacks score. Maximum 1 scoring hit per round. Stun and Trip only apply their effects.

Effects: Last for the next round only, then expire even if unused. Repeated copies of the same effect do not stack. Trip's BOON can apply to a counterattack, but not an Evade roll.

## Knockouts
A Dragon on a scoring attack that lands forces the opponent to roll CON with BANE. Failure means knockout; success means the hit scores normally. Dragons on Stun or Trip do not trigger knockouts.

Safety: Scoring hits do not remove HP. A knocked-out fighter wakes after a stretch rest unless the GM rules otherwise.

## Crowd Influence Round
After each combat round, unless the bout has ended, roll D10 on the table below. This replaces the normal crowd influence attempt.

One non-fighting party member describes how they handle the event and rolls an appropriate skill. Use a normal skill roll, not a fixed DC. The GM may apply BOON or BANE for an especially easy or difficult approach. Let different party members take turns.

| D10 | Crowd event | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Opposing fans grow louder. Rally your own supporters. | Success: Party fighter gains BOON. Failure: Opponent gains BOON. |
| 2 | Party opportunity! Describe how you sway the crowd. | GM chooses the skill. Success: Party fighter gains BOON. Failure: No effect. |
| 3 | An opposing fan distracts the referee. Get their attention back. | Success: No effect. Failure: Opponent gains BOON. |
| 4 | A bottle flies towards the party fighter. Intercept it or shout a warning. | Success: No effect. Failure: Party fighter suffers BANE. |
| 5 | Party opportunity! Describe a chant, stunt, or distraction. | GM chooses the skill. Success: Party fighter gains BOON. Failure: No effect. |
| 6 | A fight breaks out between the fan-bases. Settle it or rally your side. | Success: Party fighter gains BOON. Failure: Opponent gains BOON. |
| 7 | A streaker jumps into the ring. Turn the distraction to your advantage. | Success: Opponent suffers BANE. Failure: No effect. |
| 8 | Party opportunity! Describe a clever way to support your fighter. | GM chooses the skill. Success: Party fighter gains BOON. Failure: No effect. |
| 9 | Drums, horns, and chants swell for the opponent. Drown them out. | Success: Party fighter gains BOON. Failure: Opponent gains BOON. |
| 10 | A fan tries to cheat for the opponent. Spot and stop them. | Success: No effect. Failure: Party fighter suffers BANE. |

Effects: Apply to one roll in the next combat round, declared before rolling, then expire. The affected fighter chooses when to use BOON; the opposing side chooses when to impose BANE. Crowd effects do not stack with other crowd effects. Resolve the event before fighters secretly choose their next moves.

## Surrender and Fouls
Surrender ends the bout immediately. Weapons, magic, or direct outside interference risk disqualification. Cheering and crowd influence are allowed.
