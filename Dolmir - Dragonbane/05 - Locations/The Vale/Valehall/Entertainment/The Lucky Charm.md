# The Lucky Charm

## Location

Nestled in the Lower Quarter, the Lucky Charm is the gem of the district and amongst the most reputable of establishments. The street outside is paved with worn stone and a subtle sign of a painted charm hanging from wrought iron, marks the entrance. 

## Description

A two-storey building of pale stone with a dark timber frame. The upper windows are shuttered and private; the lower level opens onto a spacious common room with velvet-deep red walls that seem to drink the lamplight. Smooth wooden banisters trace the edges of the space, burnished to a warm glow. Soft music drifts through the air, a lute player, a singer, sometimes both. The air is thick with incense, wine, and the low murmur of conversation.

The interior is warm and intimate, divided by heavy curtains and cushioned alcoves. Candles flicker in sconces. The rear of the establishment opens onto a private courtyard and back stairs leading to the upper rooms.

## Owner & Proprietress

[[Ariel Enel]], an Eldar Elf of grave beauty with ice-sharp eyes that make your hairs stand on edge. She stands above the common area, looking down at its members. She is as much guardian as proprietor.

## The Rule

A single law governs the Lucky Charm: **No patron or visitor may lay a hand on any woman of the house.** Not a suggestion. Not negotiable. Those who forget this fact learn swiftly from the darker elements of the Lower District—the kind of justice that leaves no evidence for the city guard to find. The Lucky Charm is protected. Its women are protected.

## Menu

- Red wine from the southern valleys (2 sp per glass)
- Spiced mead (1 sp per mug)
- Spirits—various origins (3 sp per measure)
- Dark ale (6 cp per tankard)

### Food
- Roasted lamb with herbs and dried figs (1 gp)
- Spiced chicken pastry with pine nuts (8 sp)
- Bread, cheese, cured meats (5 sp)
- Oysters (when available) (6 sp)
- Sweetcakes and honey (3 sp)

## Notable Staff

**Kess** — A sharp-tongued Halfling woman who manages the kitchen and bar. She knows everything that happens in the Lower Quarter and forgets nothing.

**Marina** — A musician who plays lute most evenings. Halfling. 

**The Watchers** — Several unsmiling individuals who work the edges of the common room. Their role is to *ensure* the Rule is kept. Their methods are discreet and final.

## Notable Patrons

The Lucky Charm attracts a careful mix: wealthy merchants seeking discretion, minor nobility wanting to slum without being recognized, wanderers with coin and stories, and those with business that doesn't bear daylight. Regulars are welcomed and remembered. Strangers are tolerated but watched.

## House Rumors & Whispers

- Ariel Enel came from the Deep Mitland and arrived in Valehall with nothing but a name and unflinching resolve
- Some say she was Eldar nobility once, before the fading; others claim darker things
- A young noble was found drowned in the Silverfond last year—he'd been refused entry to the Lucky Charm the week before
- The Ordo Magi occasionally meets with Ariel in her private rooms; what they discuss, no one knows
- One of her workers disappeared three months ago; no one speaks of it
- The Lucky Charm has never been robbed. Not once. Not because of locks.
