### _The Towering Stone of Umbar_

> _"The walls have burned twice. They are still standing."_

[Valehall], the Towering Stone of Umbar, is the fortress city on the edge of the Vale. Beyond its wide stretched green fields and tall white walls of stone lies a vibrant and diverse city carved out the of mountains of the Vale. 
During the [Second Long Night] the impenetrable stone walls kept safe both Man and Eldar against the [Jotun-born] and again during the [Rite of Purification] as the [Old Order] tried to lay siege when denied entry of the 'Faithful'. The burned scorch marks can still be seen on its walls. 

#### Politics
**Ruled by:** [[House Hathor]], Lords of the Vale of Odellia

**Current Lord:** [[Reindell Hathor]], pragmatic, patient, and presently preparing for war whether he admits it or not. He is not a man of ideology or religion. He is a man of the Vale: its people, its walls, its survival. The Order's burning of Ashveil has made neutrality increasingly difficult to maintain, and the arrival of the Umbarian ambassadors of both princes has complicated things further.

**Political Position:** Valehall has long styled itself a _neutral_ city; one of the few places in the Heartlands where Eldar walk without hoods and the Faith does not set the agenda. The House of Hathor has defended this right since [The Second Age].
- The **Order of the One God** is practiced within its walls however those who break the laws are expelled. Others have gone underground and are currently helping [The Smiling Blade]
- A **Traditionalist ambassador from Umbar** , one [Serevann Dault], has taken residence in the lower city. He is courteous, patient, and has been here six months. His dispatches home have not been seen.
- The **Royalist faction** has quiet support from Hathor himself, though he will not say so publicly and an ambassador shall arrive soon, [Gylas Cholte].
- **Father [Alexios Valon]** directs the Faith's pressure on Valehall from afar, operating out of [Templewall], where he holds considerable influence within the Order's hierarchy. His instrument here is the Smiling Blade, an Inquisitor dispatched to force open what diplomacy could not.

**The War Council:** Hathor has begun summoning his bannermen. Ostensibly it is a review of the Vale's defences. In practice, the lords of the outer settlements know what is coming. The question is which side will move first.

#### Economics
**Primary export:** Wine. The Vale's deep red vineyards produce a vintage well known across the Heartlands — heavy, dark, faintly bitter, aged in the mountain's natural cold. House Hathor taxes its trade and licenses its merchants. The Vaeloriane family held a significant trade agreement with Valehall's wine merchants; that agreement is now in question given the events in Ashveil and the status of House Vaeloriane.

- **Corvin** (the merchant from the gorge, if the party encountered him) trades wine futures out of Valehall. He is not a significant player, but he knows every player.
- The **Jefferson trading house** was Valehall's largest external broker — dealing not just in wine but in information, letters of credit, and access. Jefferson's disappearance has created a hole in the city's commercial web that others are quietly filling.

**Other trade:** Mountain iron from the old mines (now largely exhausted, their depths given over to the dead), preserved meats, pelts from the upper passes, and a small but thriving market in Eldar-made goods — bone-worked tools, woven cloth, medicines — that you cannot find in the Traditionalist cities.

#### Population
Valehall holds roughly **twelve thousand souls**, large for the Heartlands outside the major cities. Its population is unusually mixed by the standards of the Faith's domains:
- Approximately **one in six** residents are Eldar (elves, beastkin, and others) living openly in the lower and middle tiers of the city. Most work in trade, craft, or the quarry. Only few hold formal civic office, though several hold significant informal influence.
- A community of **displaced residents**, perhaps two hundred and growing, have arrived in recent weeks. Most are housed in the lower city's pilgrim quarter, occupying space normally used for seasonal merchant traffic. They are frightened, angry, and beginning to organise. Others are beyond the city wall in a growing informal settlement
- Perhaps **fifty to eighty Order sympathisers** operate quietly within the city, unknown to Hathor's watch. Father Valon manages them carefully.

#### Religion
- The faith of the common people is [The Lady Saint]
- Other faiths practice with the Eldar Gods
- Due to the Mountainous region and old mines, the dead are laid to rest in the catacombs which used to be mines. 
	- However some graveyards exist

##### The Dead
The mountain's old mine shafts have, over generations, become the city's catacombs. The dead are walked in by candlelight and placed in carved niches along the shaft walls, named stones set into the rock above them. It is considered the highest honour — to rest in the mountain itself.

The upper mine shafts were the deepest and are now the oldest, their niches packed tight with generations of the city's dead. The lower shafts are newer. Some shafts are still being expanded.

A small number of surface graveyards exist outside the lower walls for those who could not be carried up — travellers, the destitute, those who died unnamed. They are maintained but not much visited.

#### Crime
Any crime would be hidden behind the various guilds behind closed doors or travel through the underground tunnels but there are no major crime syndicates inside Valehall.

#### Law
**Core principles driving their law:**
- Commerce is sacred. Anything that disrupts trade is the worst offense.
- The walls kept everyone safe. Threatening the city's security is existential.
- Neutrality is policy. Taking sides publicly is dangerous to the city.
**Serious offenses** — Hathor's justice, likely imprisonment in the mountain or exile from the Vale
**Moderate offenses** — fines, short detention, goods confiscated

#### Military 
Hathor commands a **standing garrison of approximately eight hundred soldiers**, enough to hold the walls, patrol the Vale, and remind merchants to pay their taxes. For a war, it is not enough.

Hathor also has roughly sixty to ninety knights in employ under the **Knight Order of the Vale**. As well as a couple hedge knights in employ.
	**Twenty to thirty** within Valehall directly under House Hathor, household knights, a small number of sworn sword nobles from the Vale's minor lordly families
	Another **thirty to fifty** spread across the bannermen holdings in the lower Vale farmlands, where the terrain is flatter and cavalry actually makes sense

**His bannermen can raise roughly three thousand additional troops** across the Vale's settlements — infantry mostly, some cavalry from the lower farmlands, and a small company of Eldar rangers who patrol the mountain passes and owe Hathor a debt of old standing.

#### Geography
The land surrounding the Vale is a mix of open rolling hills with small sections of pine forests. The Valehall itself is built and carved from the Vale mountains and extends outwards into the open fields. 

Mine shafts and underground tunnels exist both in the mountain and under the Valehall. The river of Odellia can be seen from the highest seat of the Valehall.
