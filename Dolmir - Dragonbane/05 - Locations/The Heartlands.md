## Noble Houses
Separated into Eight Duchies of the Heartlands, falling under three categories: 
The Royalists - Loyal to the Umbarian throne
The Traditionalists - Loyal to the throne but the One God above all 
The Neutralists - Loyal to the throne but remain independent of sway. 

### Emblems
- Monarchs have a single colour coat of arms on a white background with their familial crests
- Duchy houses are two fields divided in the centre vertically with their family crests on the left on a background of their choosing and their monarchs crest on the other with the monarchs colours. 
- Free Knights carry their own crests unless elevated to dukedom
