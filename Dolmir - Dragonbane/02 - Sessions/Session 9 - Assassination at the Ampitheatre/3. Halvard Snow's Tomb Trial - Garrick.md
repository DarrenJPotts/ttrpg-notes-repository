
**Halvard's First Words**:
"A Mallard. I haven't seen one of your kind in... how long? Decades. Centuries? The years blur." 

He studies Garrick carefully.

"What's your lineage? Who was your sire? Your line?"

**Garrick's Answer**: Whatever he says, Halvard *listens*.

**Halvard's Testing**:
- "You've come to take the oath. The oath is not simple. It costs everything."
- "What do you know of Drake of the Vale? What do you know of our history?"
- "Why should a dying old man teach a boy who may outlive him by centuries? Give me a reason."

**What Halvard Wants**:
- To know if Garrick is genuine
- To know if he understands what the oath *means*
- To know if he's worthy of the Mallard name (if he carries it)

**What Halvard *Observes***:
- Garrick's bearing, his courage, his willingness
- Garrick *might* be bloodline—there's something familiar
- Garrick represents a *connection to the past* that Halvard thought was lost

---

## PHASE 1 COMPLICATION: HALVARD'S CONCERN

**Halvard Stops the Questioning**:
"Before we go further, you need to know something. I survived the Second Long War. I survived the Rite of Purification. I watched armies clash and fall. I watched good men burn for faith and folly."

He pauses, his voice heavy.

"I died once, you know. By a misguided child with a knife. A child who didn't understand what he was doing. I died, and the magic of this order brought me back. But I've never been the same."

**What He's Warning**:
- The oath is binding and *dangerous*
- It connects you to the history of the order
- That history is *not* glorious—it's tragic
- Taking the oath means carrying the weight of that tragedy

**The Complication**:
"If you take the oath here, in the Tomb of the Vale, you won't just swear words. You'll *see*. The tomb shows you the truth of what we are. Some men break. Some go mad. Some become what we *need* them to be."

He leans forward.

"Are you ready to see the truth? Because once you do, you can't unsee it."

**Garrick's Choice**:
- Accept the trial (go to the tomb)
- Refuse (leave, but he's never truly tested)
- Ask more questions (delay, but eventually must choose)

---

## PHASE 2: THE TOMB OF THE VALE

**The Setup**:
- Halvard leads Garrick deep beneath Silverhome
- Stone passages carved with names, dates, epithets
- The tomb is *cold*. Magic hangs in the air—old magic, sad magic
- They descend for what feels like hours
- Finally, they reach the chamber

**The Chamber**:
- Circular, vast, ceiling lost in shadow
- The remains of great warriors in stone caskets around the perimeter
- In the center: A pool of still water, perfectly black
- [[Drake of the Vale]]'s tomb is the largest, marked with a *massive maul* carved in stone

**Halvard's Final Words**:
"Drink from the pool. Touch the water. Open yourself. The tomb will show you."

**What Happens**:
Garrick touches the water. The tomb *activates*. He sees visions—not dreams, but *memories* impressed into the very stone and magic of the place.

---

## THE VISIONS: WHAT GARRICK SEES

**Vision 1: The Burning of Mallard Kin**
- A vision of a *massacre*
- Young Mallards running, screaming
- Fire. Death. Slaughter.
- A single young Mallard child *survives* by hiding in the undergrowth
- Watching everything burn

**Vision 2: The Protection**
- The young Mallard is running through a caravan
- A *human child* (noble blood, fine clothes) is about to be trampled
- The Mallard *throws* itself in front
- Shields the child. Takes the blow meant for the child.
- Both survive. But the Mallard *loses an eye* in the process
- The Mallard becomes the human child's protector

**Vision 3: The Band of Defenders**
- Years pass. The young Mallard is grown.
- Standing with a band of unknown warriors (human, elf, some beastkin)
- Facing an *enormous* army
- The sky is *filled* with dark wyverns, giants, jotun-born creatures
- Twisted humanoid things moving between the ranks
- Hopeless odds. But they *stand*.

**Vision 4: The Moment of Truth**
- The band makes its last stand
- The Mallard *refuses to break*
- It's *Drake of the Vale* in that vision—or the ancestor Drake fought alongside
- The malice and darkness closing in
- A *choice point*: surrender or burn

**Vision 5: The Reckoning**
- Halvard's death (from Phase 1): A child with a knife, stabbing him
- But now Garrick sees the *context*: the child was brainwashed, terrified, forced
- Halvard dying and yet *forgiving* the child
- Magic surging through the tomb as Halvard's oath binds him *back to life*

**The Core Truth**:
- The Mallard lineage has *always* been protectors
- They have *always* stood against darkness
- They have *always* paid a price (loss, pain, sacrifice)
- The oath is a *continuation* of that lineage
- Taking it means accepting the burden—but also the *purpose*

---

## PHASE 2 COMPLICATION: THE VISION OVERWHELMS

**Option A: Emotional Collapse**
Garrick sees too much. The weight of history, sacrifice, and duty crashes down. He weeps. He's broken by the vision. Coming back to consciousness, he's *changed*.

**Option B: The Vision Traps Him**
Garrick gets *stuck* in the visions. He's living the massacre, the protection, the final stand. Time doesn't work normally. When he finally breaks free, has minutes passed or hours? Days?

**Option C: The Vision Shows Something Else**
In the final moments, the vision shifts. Garrick sees something *not in the others' memories*. Something personal. A *warning* about him specifically. A mark on him. A connection he didn't know he had.

**Option D: Halvard Enters the Vision**
Halvard's consciousness *touches* Garrick in the tomb. They *speak* in the visionary space. Halvard asks one final question: "Do you understand now what the oath means?"

---

## PHASE 3: THE OATH & TRANSFORMATION

**The Setup**:
- Garrick wakes from the vision at the tomb's center
- He's changed by what he's seen
- Halvard is waiting, watching
- The choice is now *clear*: take the oath or walk away

**Halvard's Offer**:
"The oath is this: You swear to protect those who cannot protect themselves. You swear to stand against darkness, no matter the cost. You swear that your strength is not your own—it belongs to those you defend."

"Do you take this oath?"

**The Oath-Taking**:
- Garrick speaks the words (if he chooses)
- The magic of the tomb *responds*
- Light floods the chamber
- The spirits of the fallen knights seem to *acknowledge* him
- He feels the weight of their legacy, their sacrifice, their *purpose* settle on him

**What Changes**:
- Garrick is now a Knight of the Vale (officially)
- He gains access to the **Drake's Oath** (can read the ancient texts, access the order's knowledge)
- He understands his connection to the old Mallard lineage
- He's *bound* by the oath—it's magical, real, constraining and empowering

**What He Learns**:
- **The Second Long War**: The history of it, the stakes, why the Knights exist
- **The Rite of Purification**: What it was, what it cost, why Halvard survived
- **Drake of the Vale**: The legendary founder, his bloodline, his power
- **The Mallard Legacy**: His people were *always* protectors. This isn't new. It's inheritance.
- **The Current Threat**: The oath carries a *warning*—there is *danger coming*. Dark things stirring. The order is needed again.

---

## HALVARD'S FINAL WORDS

**After the oath is taken**, Halvard speaks to Garrick one more time:

"You have taken the oath of the Vale. You are now bound to protect. But know this: the world is darker than it was. The darkness that the Knights stood against in the Long War—it's stirring again. The Rite of Purification was meant to bury it, but burial isn't *death*."

"There are things in the shadows. Orders older than the current Faith. Powers that remember when the gods walked. They're *moving*."

"You have the blood of Drake in you—I felt it in the vision. That means *you* have a role to play in what's coming. Don't shy from it."

**Halvard offers Garrick**:
- Full access to the Knights' archives
- Training in the ancient magic and martial tradition
- A position as a full Knight of the Vale
- A place to belong
- A *purpose*
