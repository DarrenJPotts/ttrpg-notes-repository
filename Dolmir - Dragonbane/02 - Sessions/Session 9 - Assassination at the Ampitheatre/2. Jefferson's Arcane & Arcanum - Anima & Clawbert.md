## ENTERING THE SHOP

Coming back to the Arcana Arcanum after initial discovery, you find the store pretty much like you left it.

**What in particular are you looking for?**

- There is a locked door in the back of the shop
	- To open the lock it will need to be lock picked or broken or the key needs to be found
	- **BROKEN**: The later guard will open it
	- **PICKED**: Can be locked again
	- **KEY**: Can be found in his study with SPOT HIDDEN but if take too long, as they get it they will hear footsteps outside

## THE CELLAR

The now unlocked door opens to a dark cellar that lowers a few feet down. The ever so slight sound of water drops can be heard from below. 

As you descend the ladder and small light illuminates the walls, you see you are in a damp dark hallway of sleek stone. 

The Hallway leads a few metres deeper in before opening up to a dimly lit room filled with boxes and storage. 

**What are you looking for?**

### Items to be Found
**A tome covered in beautiful rich red leather that is aged well, embossed with two coiling dragons or wyverns. Secured by a golden clasp**

Extract 1:
> "First came the dawning children, of star and dust, who would greet all kin in kind. 
> Second the Stone Singers, sons of the Titans, whom climbed into the skies.
> Third the kin of ash, born of the eldest Titan, the Jotun and the unending night began."

Extract 2:
> "On wooden steeds arrived the kin of short-life, unknowing and lost. 
> At Sehan'nain's behest the dawning children gave shelter.
> Wolves given shelter in a den of lamb."

Extract 3:
> "... and with the unending night dusking once more, 
> the wolves of man swallowed the heart of blessing 
> and with it the beings of ash and dawn dwindled into poor reflections..."
