#### Arrival at Silverhome
Garrick, you make your way across the city's Noble District, passing by where you know Lia and Elenessa shall be soon. The city is abuzz with people and excitement for what appears to be a major play.

You pass by the Stone Seat, where previously you had seen Lord [[Reindell Hathor]], you pass the towering stone walls. You cannot help but think that a Mallard such as yourself was one of the reasons this city, these stones, vines and people are even here today as you make your way to Silverhome, the headquarters for the Knights of the Vale.

#### At Silverhome
The Knights of the Vale headquarters, it is a fortress-hall carved into living rock in the Vale Mountains, just like the Stone-seat, and nearly just as fortified. The Home of the order of knights sworn to protect the Vale.

Are the entrance is a large stone archway that leads inwards to the grounds of Silverhome, passed two guardian knights you can see the stone structure of the quarters as well as an opening training ground where some knights train near a large oak tree. 

**Silverhome Reception**:
- The Knights greet him formally but with curiosity
- He's brought before [[Halvard Snow]] in the great hall
- Halvard is in a high-backed chair, wrapped in furs. He's old. He's dying. But his eyes are *sharp*.

#### In the Knight's Hall 
You are brought the the halls of Silverhome, passing portraits of various figures, knights of old or people of import, you pass cooks prepping the knights dinners and knights talking joyously to each other, you hear clashes of nearby soldiers sparring with blunted weapons (preparing for the tournament). 

#### Knights Vow
On the tomb of Drake of the Vale are inscribed the words: 
_
"To stand amongst those worthy to be called knights, I vow to protect the smallfolk above all creeds, duties and loyalties. 
To stand with honour, I vow to be brave in the face of nights long and dark, 
To stand just, I vow to choose what's right for all kin, even when it breaks me,
To stand steadfast, I vow to be the shield against the armies of those who bring ruin,  
I am no solider, I am no hound of war, I am the hand offered to those who need it.
"
