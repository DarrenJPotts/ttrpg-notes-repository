### Scene: Inside The Gate
The carts groan as they roll through the barricade walls of the gorge. The rain and thunder does not let up. The camp spreads before you and the thick mist clumps tight to the lower walls, torchlight smearing gold across the wet rock that seems to tower into nothingness, the smell of woodsmoke, damp wool and mud scents are picked up as the wind howls through the snake-like gorge. Soldiers move between tents huddling under outcroppings of stone to avoid the battering rain. Nobody looks at you directly, the mist obscuring you for now.

The wind howls, almost roaring. 

*What do you do/questions?*
  
----
#### What you see
##### Brother Aemon
A young Order priest stands at the edge of the road just past the entrance, holding a covered lantern against the rain. Brown robes, hood pulled low, slim build. He is ostensibly checking papers. As you enter, he calls you towards with a raised hand.
When the carts draw level he steps forward, not aggressive, almost apologetic.
*"Forgive me. Formality only. Names, and your destination? Order requires."*
- He writes nothing down immediately — just listens
- His eyes move to the distant platform once while he waits for an answer, a habit he seems unaware of
- He has a ledger. A list of names noted, with small check marks and Xs. 
	- If looked closely short descriptions describing the group. 

###### Brother Aemon's Questions
He will ask one or two of the following depending on how the conversation flows. He does not push hard. He seems tired of pushing.
1. *"You've come from the south road, did you pass through the Ashveil?"*
2. *"Have you encountered any travelling groups? Eldar, particularly. We have standing orders. A human lady, two Eldar elves, and a... child. Accompanied by two others... Eldar too."*  He says this without enthusiasm and without disrespect that would usually be given to beast kin.
3. *"There is another matter of an Inquisitor. Templar rank. We were awaiting word but her riders are yet to come. You wouldn't have seen them either?"* — He watches their faces when he asks this, not their mouths

###### Reading Brother Aemon
- He does not believe everything the Order tells him about the Runekeeper
- He has read the iron plate on the cage door a hundred times and it still doesn't sit right with him
- He will not help the party directly, not here, not tonight — but he will not obstruct them if they answer cleanly
- He will recognise Elenessa by description of Brother Calem. His expression doesn't change but he takes slightly longer than necessary to step back from the cart and will rush them along with *Be quick, do not be seen... I'm sorry, Calem always spoke high of you*.
	- *Hook: Aemon is the sympathetic thread inside the Order. Whether the party pulls it depends entirely on how this conversation goes*

##### The Guard Tents
A row of canvas tents lines the eastern wall of the gorge, grey and utilitarian with the insignia of the Old Order. Weapons are stacked at the entrance of each, spears leaning together, shields propped against tent poles. Off-duty soldiers move in and out pulling on cloaks or ducking out of the rain under small outcroppings of stone.
- The tents are close enough to the road that passing carts will be visible to anyone stepping outside if lingering too long
- A whetstone sound comes from the largest tent, someone sharpening a blade in the dark
- A hand-drawn notice is pinned to the central tent pole facing the road. A rough description of several individuals. No names. Rough physical descriptions. One reads *"Eldar, multiple, travelling together."*
- Directly across the Runekeepers Stockade. 

##### The Runekeeper's Cage
Set apart from the guard tents, a reinforced stockade platform sits in the mud. Rain pelting down on the hunched body of a man. Iron shackles, a single torch bracket mounted on the side. A man sits with his back hunched facing towards the tents. 
Late middle age, lean, dressed in travelling clothes that were once fine. He does not look up when the carts pass.
- His body is slumped, very still.
- A small iron plate is bolted to the stockade. It reads: *MAGI HERETIC.*
- If anyone makes direct eye contact he holds it for a long moment before looking deliberately toward a sleeping guard. Then back towards the cart.

"The lock is a single bar latch. The guard to my left, Robert... actually a nice guy considering,  has not moved in an hour. Toss me the key. I can help. I'll uhh meet you out front, after I collect my things. "

- He will not say what things. He will not say where he is going inside the camp. He will not explain himself further unless the party refuses — at which point he looks up for the first time and says simply: _"Then you will want to remember the name Altes Lesym of the Ordro Magi. For later. And if I die... well it's a good name for your first born I suppose."
- If the party unlocks the cage he does not move immediately. He waits until the carts have cleared the area before he acts. What he does between the cage and the gorge exit is not visible to the party  but he arrives. Quietly, on foot, from a direction that doesn't quite make sense given where the cage was.
- Hook: What he went to collect, and how he moved through a camp full of soldiers without being seen, are questions he will not answer until he trusts them. All he will say is *Proof. Proof of their deeds.*

### Scene: The Gorge — Corvin's Broken Cart
The tents and guards fall behind you. The gorge mouth bends and swallows the carts one at a time and the walls narrow. Rock walls rise on either side, slick with rain, torchlight from the brackets above casting everything in long shifting shadows. The road is old stone here, uneven, worn smooth in the centre by years of trade and travel. The sound of the camp fades. What replaces it is the gorge itself, wind moving through the channel louder than before, water running somewhere below the road aided by the rains bashing against the walls feeling a bit like they are trying to drown you, the echo of your own wheels doubled back at you off the stone.

Ahead, partially visible through the dark and rain, something is wrong with the road.

*What do you do/questions?*

----

#### What you see
##### The Blocked Road
A merchant cart sits listing heavily to the left, one rear wheel cracked clean through at the axle. The cart has drifted toward the gorge wall leaving a gap on the right, wide enough for a person on foot, not wide enough for a loaded cart to pass without scraping stone or risk being stuck as well. Canvas covers the merchant's goods, tied down but bulging unevenly. Several crates have shifted with the list, one half-wedged against the broken wheel.

- The gap cannot be passed with loaded carts without first moving something — either Corvin's cart shifts or the party's carts are lightened
- Every minute spent here is a minute the camp has to notice the gorge has gone quiet

##### Corvin
A man in his fifties crouches at the broken wheel, turning it over in his hands with the focused desperation of someone who already knows the answer. Heavy travelling coat, soaked through, a lantern hanging from the cart frame throwing light across his face in pieces. He looks up when the party's carts approach — relief and apology in the same expression.
  
*"Thank the road you've come. Axle's gone clean through. I can't move her. If you could help me shift the load to the wall, even a little — just enough to widen the gap —"*

He talks too much when he is nervous. He is very nervous.
- Not Order. Not a threat. A merchant who has been alone in a dark gorge for an hour, acutely aware that soldiers are behind him
- Will help enthusiastically and clumsily — creates noise, drops things, narrates his own efforts
- If directed with calm purpose he quiets down and works efficiently
- Has travelled this road a dozen times. Knows the Hathor lands, which settlements ask fewer questions, what lies ahead
- *If rushed or panicked he raises his voice without meaning to*

##### The Overhang Guard
A soldier posted on a natural rock shelf above the road, elevated enough to see the length of the gorge in both directions. Tonight the rain sheets off the overhang above him, his hood is pulled to his eyebrows, and his chin keeps dropping toward his chest.
- Fighting sleep and losing
- Noise — raised voices, crates dropped, wheels grinding stone — wakes him
- If he wakes and looks down he sees the blockage first, then makes a decision about whether the people around it are suspicious

##### The Crinwin Markings
Midway along the gorge wall, level with Corvin's cart, a dark discolouration spreads down the rock face. Not moss. Not water damage. A thick liquid mixes with the rain and flows down. Your eyes follow the length of it to it's origin. Small markings and crude sticks tied together hang and blow in the wind.
- The horses slow before the party sees it, ears back, reluctant to pass
- Corvin has been facing away from that section of wall since his cart broke. He does not explain why.
	- If pressed the he will say *"I swear on my ol ma that I saw some' dark and tall move up there. Tossed a rock right at my cart.*
- A soldier lies dead in a crevice above.

----

#### How The Scene Runs — Clock Escalation
The longer the party takes with Corvin's cart the more the clock drives what happens next. The scene has three distinct phases depending on how many segments remain when the blockage becomes a problem.

---
##### Segments 6–4 — Corvin Tries To Help
The delay is noticed only by Corvin himself. He is grateful, cooperative, and working alongside the party to shift the load and widen the gap. The problem is that in doing so he moves around both carts — checking wheels, pushing from the back, peering under the canvas to check clearance.
At some point he leans close to the second cart and goes still.
A shape. A sound. Something that shouldn't be there.
He straightens slowly and looks at Elenessa. His voice drops to almost nothing.
*"There is someone in your cart."*
Not an accusation yet. A statement. He is waiting to be told a reason he can accept.
- If given a convincing answer — a sick relative, someone injured on the road, a servant who can't walk — he accepts it and says nothing more. He is not looking for trouble.
- If the answer is weak or the party looks wrong he takes a half step back toward the camp end of the gorge and his voice wavers — *"I should — I ought to let the men back there know about this —"*
- The party has a moment to stop that from happening. How they stop it is their choice.
- *A bribe lands cleanly at this segment level. Corvin has debts and no love for the Order.*

---
##### Segments 3–2 — Voices From The Camp

The overhang guard woke up at some point. Or Corvin's delay was noticed. Either way a voice carries through the gorge from the camp end — a soldier, not yet moving, calling ahead.
*"Oi! What's the holdup in there?"*
Elenessa or whoever is visible must answer. The gap is still not clear. The party is standing in the middle of the gorge with loaded carts and a merchant who is looking at them with wide eyes.
- The soldier hasn't moved yet — he is waiting for a response before he decides to walk in
- A clean answer buys two minutes at most before he comes to check regardless
- Corvin looks between the party and the camp end of the gorge. He has just understood that the people helping him may be the people the soldiers are looking for.
- He does not run. He does not shout. He goes very quiet and moves to the far side of his cart and crouches down, making himself small.
- The party must clear the gap and move — now, not carefully

---
##### Segments 1–0 — Abandon The Carts
The voice has become boots on stone. Two soldiers are walking into the gorge from the camp end, torches in hand, and they can already see the outline of the blockage ahead. There is no time to clear it cleanly.
*The carts have to be left.*
Whatever is inside them — whoever is inside them — must come out now and move on foot. The gap between Corvin's cart and the gorge wall is wide enough for people. It is not wide enough for a loaded cart.
- Garrick and Clawbert moving on foot through the dark gorge is still a risk but it is a manageable one
- Anyone still hidden in a cart when the soldiers arrive is found
- Corvin stays crouched behind his cart. He does not look up as the party moves past him. He will later tell the soldiers he saw nothing.
