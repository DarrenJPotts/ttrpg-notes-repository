#### If They Escaped Through The Gates Cleanly
**The Rest Stop — The Wayfarers Stone**
- An old roadside shelter, pre-Umbarian, built into a natural overhang of rock
- Not a building exactly — three walls of stacked stone and a low ceiling that keeps the rain off
- Corvin knows it — if he escaped with them or ahead of them he is already there, fire going, looking sheepish and grateful in equal measure
- This is where the first real conversation with Altes happens — firelight, relative warmth, the Vale behind them and the unknown ahead
- Tone is a debrief. What just happened, what does Altes know, what does his presence mean going forward

### The Altes Conversation — Regardless of Which Path

This conversation is the bridge between Session 4 and the wider world. It should do three things.

**First — establish what he knows**
- He was in the Vale to head to the Abbey of St Odellia however was waylaid by the Order and spent a few days on the run. His suspicion was right that they are indeed making bold moves.
- He is researching what the Ordo Magi has called the Blight-born at first he thought it was like the old stories of the Jotun-born but he suspects this is more home grown
- He hid his notes ahead before he was captured.
- What he collected from the camp — _proof of their deeds_ — is physical evidence of something the Order has been doing quietly in the Vale. He shows it only when he trusts them. That may be this conversation or the next one.

**The Ordo Magi**
- He is part of the Ordo Magi, a group of once human mages and researchers who specialise in Arcana. Not all magic users but some. 
- He can act as a teacher of Symbolism. 

**Second — point them toward Valehall**
- Valehall is the seat of House Hathor, Lord of the Vale of Odellia
- Altes has a contact there — not a friend exactly, a person who owes him something
- More importantly, Valehall is the only place within reach that has walls, a roof, and people who are not actively hunting them
- He frames it practically — _"You need somewhere to sleep that isn't a ditch. So do I. Valehall is two days north. I know a way in that doesn't require explaining yourselves at another gate."_

**Third — plant the hook for the Vale corruption arc**
- Whatever is spreading beneath the Abbey didn't start in the gorge
- It started deeper in the Vale, closer to Ashveil
- The burning of Ashveil by the Order wasn't random, the Order was in the Vale for a reason, and that reason connects to what Altes was documenting.
- He wants to know why Anima and the group are so wanted
- He doesn't say this directly. He says something like _"Your village was in the wrong place. Which means it was in exactly the right place for something else."_

### The Road To Valehall — What Fills The Gap

They won't reach Valehall this session but the journey north should feel like the world opening up after the claustrophobia of the gorge. A few things to seed on the road.

**The Hathor Lands Feel Different**
- Greener, older, less marked by the Order's presence
- The road is maintained but not militarised — merchant traffic, farming settlements, the ordinary texture of a land not yet at war with itself
- People look at Garrick and Clawbert with curiosity rather than suspicion — Hathor is Royalist leaning, the Order's reach should be  thin here
- Anima and Lia can move without hoods for the first time in days

**A Traveller Going The Other Way**
- Someone heading south toward the Vale pass, ordinary enough on the surface

**The First Night Properly**

- Wherever they stop for the night — inn, farmstead, open field — this is the first night since Ashveil that isn't immediate crisis
- Space for character moments the session didn't have room for
- Elenessa and what the Vaeloriane name means now that the house is gone
- Lia and what passing as human costs her over time
- Garrick and Clawbert existing in a world that is suddenly slightly less hostile
- Altes sitting apart from the group, watching, deciding something about them

### Session 5 Opens At Valehall's Outskirts

However they travelled, Session 5 begins with the walls of Valehall visible ahead — a proper fortified town, House Hathor's seat, the first real civilisation they've encountered since Ashveil burned.

**The complication waiting for them there**

- Altes's contact inside Valehall is not expecting him — and is going to be deeply unhappy to see him
- House Hathor is officially neutral in the conflict between the Order and the Eldar but that neutrality is under pressure
- There is already someone in Valehall who knows the party is coming. Not the Order. Someone else.
- The party walks in carrying the name Vaeloriane, a freed Ordo Magi prisoner, captured evidence of Order crimes, and the knowledge of a corruption spreading beneath the Vale
- Valehall is not the safe harbour it looks like from the outside
