The thunder roars overhead, your carts roll over the stone and dirt road. Brazier fire light through the mist of the Vale. It took all but one week for each and every of your lives to change. From monstrous beings in darkened abbeys, to the char and fire on ones loved. First kills have been taken, the lives of those slipping to the beyond. No name, no bodies, forgotten to the wind. And an adventure has begun, one more task before the world opens up to you, lands, rivers and sea that you have never seen. 
*"In an orderly line!* a man bellows from ahead, obscured by the mist.

### Scene: Walking up to the gate
Thunder and rain roil as the carts pull into line, the wind whistling through the gorge tunnel tempting to blow your hoods back. You wait your turn, instilling in your mind the plan, the confidence and pushing back the doubt. Two guards stand at attention with the larger of the two, with firm stance, gut out, talking to the cart owner. Another, smaller with thin frame inspects the cart before motioning the cart forward through the gates.

Time passes, a merchant cart is pulled to the side, inspected and words are exchanged too hard to hear through the rain and eventually passes through the large barricade walls that close the gap of the Vale Pass. One way in, one way out. 

#### What you see
##### The Eldar
Two your right, a group of men and woman huddle together in the rain, incoherent words are shouted in their direction as another Witch Hunter pulls the hood off an Eldari elf man with slender features. The mist giving the silhouette a female figure. 
All the figures are Eldar, one eyes the river edge. 

##### The Camps
Further back in the mist the light of campfires pierces the façade. Tents and wagons set up to battle the rain and gather warmth. 
A group of soldiers sit around the fire. The loudest of the bunch is a lady, scarred face and mead in hand bellowing about the exploits in the Vale... of Ashveil. The Vaeloraines, of Inquisitor Templar Sige and the burning of the town. Particularly of a blacksmith, how they stoked the exterior smithy until the flames wandered out their confines. A group of armed individuals listen begrudgingly.
- Two mercenary guards of a merchant sit around the fire 
- The merchants daughter fetches them mead. 
- If the group walks up, Sergeant Mirrin is intoxicated so she does not notice them easily but will ask their story. 

##### The Patrol
East of the road, two guards patrol. Holding the collars of their cloaks to shelter themselves against the storm. One crouches inspecting a pile of rocks just off the road. 
 - Small scratchings of crude symbols are etched into rocks
 - Memory will reveal the folk tales of [[Crinwin]]. Creatures of the First Age whom once roamed the West Stormlands of [[Dolmir]]. 

### Scene: The Checkpoint
Give her a branching interrogation. Each question she handles well keeps the clock safe. Each deflection or weak answer risks a segment.

**Opening Questions — Easy**
- _"State your name and business"_
- _"Where are you coming from?"_

**Middle Questions — Harder**
- _"We have orders to check all cargo passing north. Step aside while we search"_
- _"There were survivors from Ashveil. You wouldn't know anything about that?"_

**The Curveball**
- The gate officer pulls out the ledger and shows her a sketch — a rough description of the party circulated after Ashveil
- _"Inquisitor Sige was meant to pass through here. Have you seen her on the road?"_

### Tension Mechanics for the Carts

**The Inspection Moment**
- Almost guaranteed a guard will want to look at the carts
- Elenessa can try to redirect — offer the wine, name drop her house, create urgency
- If she fails, the guard moves toward Cart Two first — give players a beat to decide if anyone acts

**The Noise Problem**
- Garrick especially — build in a moment where something almost gives them away
- A sneeze, a cart wheel hitting a rut hard, Clawbert shifting weight
- Let Elenessa react in the moment — her improvisation either saves it or burns a segment

**The Bribed Guard**
- One of the gate guards could be the doubting soldier
- He processes her papers slower than necessary — not out of suspicion but quietly buying her time
- A small signal if players catch it — he doesn't check the second cart at all
