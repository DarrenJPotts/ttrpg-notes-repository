The gorge bends once more. Corvin's cart falls behind. Opening to a straightened road, the light bleeding through the mist and walls breath wider, enough that the air moves differently and the strong winds once more howl at your necks. The worst of it feels like it is behind you.
It isn't.

*What do you do/questions?*

#### How This Scene Runs — Three Outcomes
This scene has no fixed shape. What happens here depends entirely on how many segments remain when the party rounds the bend. The road itself is the same in each version — what changes is what is waiting on it.

---
##### Segments 4+ — The Quiet Exit
The road ahead is empty. The gorge exit is visible , a pale gap in the rock where the walls finally release the path and the land of Hathor opens beyond. No soldiers. No torches moving. Just the rain and the wind and the last stretch of old stone road.

The carts are still with them. Whatever they carried through the gate, through the camp, through Corvin's blockage — it is still intact.

This is the version where the session ends on a breath released rather than a heart still hammering.

**What happens in the quiet**
- The party has a moment — the first real one — to check on each other properly
- Elenessa sits with what she just did. Used her family name to bluff past the people who burned her home.
- If Altes Lesym was freed he arrives here quietly, on foot, from a direction that doesn't quite make sense. He says nothing about where he came from. He says only: *"Keep moving. I'll explain when there's road between us and that camp."*
- The Hathor border stone is twenty minutes north. They reach it without incident.

---
##### Segments 2–3 — Leave Quietly, Now
The road ahead is clear — but not for long. Behind them, back through the gorge, the sounds of the camp have shifted. Not alarm. Not yet. But something has changed in the rhythm of it — a voice that wasn't there before, a torch moving where it wasn't moving.

The carts can still make it. But only if the party moves without stopping, without looking back, without giving whoever is stirring behind them a reason to send someone through the gorge to check.

**The pressure**
- The party cannot stop, cannot adjust cargo, cannot deal with anything that requires standing still
- If Altes was freed he is already ahead of them somehow — a silhouette in the dark at the gorge exit, waiting without waiting, the kind of stillness that doesn't look like patience, it looks like someone who has done this before
- A clean pace gets them out. Any hesitation and it becomes the next outcome.

---
##### Segments 0–1 — Blocked. Abandon Everything.

They round the bend and stop.
A group of soldiers is coming the other way — northbound patrol returning through the gorge from the Hathor side, torches in hand, moving at pace. Not running. They don't know yet. But they will see the carts in seconds and there is nowhere on this stretch of road to hide a loaded cart, a horse, or anything larger than a person pressed flat against the gorge wall.
At the same moment, behind them, boots echo off the stone from the camp end.
They are between two groups with loaded carts and no time.

*The carts have to go.*

**What they can take**
- Whatever a person can carry in their arms right now, in the dark, in the rain, while moving fast
- Altes — if freed he is already gone from sight, already through somehow, already on the other side of the problem

**The Crevice**
Set into the gorge wall on the western side, half-hidden behind an outcropping of rock that juts into the road — a crack in the stone, floor to ceiling, wide enough for a person turned sideways. Narrow enough that Clawbert will have to exhale to fit. The darkness inside is complete.

It was not made by the Order. It was not made recently. The edges of it are worn smooth in a way that stone does not wear smooth from weather. Something has been moving through it for a very long time.

- The crevice opens thirty feet in onto a passage that runs parallel to the gorge road before curving north and emerging above the Hathor border on the far side of the wall — outside the Order's reach entirely
- The party does not know this going in. They know only that it is dark and narrow and the alternative is being found
- Garrick fits. Clawbert barely. Anyone carrying Sige will need the person behind them to push
- The horses cannot follow. The carts cannot follow. Whatever was in them stays.


**What the soldiers find**
Two abandoned carts on the road. Cargo undisturbed. Horses still hitched. No people. By the time they search the gorge wall with torches the crevice is just a crack in the rock and the dark inside shows nothing.

They will not find it tonight. But they will remember it is there.

**Moving through the crevice**
The passage is not long — maybe two hundred feet — but it does not feel short. The walls press in. The sound changes, the gorge noise cutting out entirely and replacing itself with something older, something that moves through stone rather than air. The Crinwin markings from the gorge wall continue inside — fresher here, more deliberate, covering both walls at shoulder height. They are not decorative.

Anima and Lia know, without knowing how they know, that the things that made these markings are aware the passage is being used.

Nothing moves. Nothing appears. But the awareness is there, heavy and patient, and it follows them all the way to the far end.

**Emerging on the other side**
They come out onto a rocky slope above the gorge exit. The Hathor border stone is visible below them in the dark, pale and worn. The Order camp is behind the rock wall, out of sight, torches moving small and distant.

Cold air. No torches. Nobody looking at them.

Whatever they left in those carts is gone. Whatever was inside them — supplies, hidden cargo, Sige if they couldn't move her in time — now belongs to the Order.

The road north is below them. Altes Lesym is already standing at the border stone, hands in his pockets, looking south. He does not ask what happened in the crevice. He says only:

*"The markings are older than the pass. Older than the Order. Older than most things that still have a name."*

A beat.

*"They let you through. That means something. I don't know what yet."*

Then he turns north and walks.
