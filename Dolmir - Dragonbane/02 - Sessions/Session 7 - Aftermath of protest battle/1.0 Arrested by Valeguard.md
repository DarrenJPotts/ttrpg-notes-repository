## Reindell Hathor (Scene Start)

The entryway to the palace is grandiose in design, simple, sleek stone that towers each of you. It is a wonder if it was placed or carved straight from the rocky mountain itself. The steps are solid beneath your feet as you slowly rise higher towards the Stone Seat, where [[Reindell Hathor]] sits and awaits.  

[[Reindell Hathor]]'s audience chamber is large, austere, *lived in*. Maps of Dolmir on the walls and tapestries of men and woman you dont recognise. 

He's a man in his fifties, worn but sharp and He's not alone.

The man with shoulder length, slicked back hair stands near the window angry, gesturing towards Lord Hathor. Elenessa recognises him.

[[Reindell Hathor]] is red-faced, voice raised: "You shall have no place in vale or the support of its people"

Hushed words are said by the man with long hair.

"The acts in Ashveil are known, Ambassador. You have *played your hand*, and I shall see it severed wrist from palm. If the Faith thinks it can operate inside my vale without consequence, it is *sorely* mistaken. A lesson learned once before..."

The Ambassador opens his mouth to respond.

Hathor: "You're dismissed. And send word to Templewall: the next operative I find in my city will be hanged from the wall. Do not test my patience."

The Ambassador leaves, furious and humiliated.
As he leaves he gives a piercing look at the group.
