## Iskandor's Elixir Shop

**The Shop**
Iskandor runs a small apothecary in the Vintage Dsitrict. The shop is warm, smells of herbs and boiling water, lined with shelves of bottles, dried plants, and strange compounds.
[[Iskandor]] himself is an older man, Eldar, with shaky hands and narrow eyes. His hands are stained with plant matter. 

---

## The Quest: Gather Two Herbs

Iskandor gives Anima two options, or presents both as necessary.

### Option 1: Cloudmoss Tonic (Outside the City)

**The Location:**
- Tall grass fields outside Valehall's walls, perhaps an hour's walk north
- Cool, temperate area—morning mist, damp ground
- The moss grows on rocks and fallen logs in shaded areas

**The Herb:**
- **Cloudmoss** appears as soft, pale grey-white growth on stone and wood
- Looks almost luminescent in the morning light
- Must be harvested carefully—if it's damaged, it loses potency
- Anima's herbalist knowledge makes this easier (HERBALISM check to harvest without damage)
- **Effect:** Restores D6 WP and cures EXHAUSTION instantly

**Complications:**

**The Oder (Order of the Inquisitors):**
- A patrol of Oder members is nearby, searching for something (or someone)
- They're tense, alert, watching for heresy
- If they see Anima:
  - They recognize her as an Eldar
  - They demand to know what she's doing outside the city
  - They're suspicious of herb-gathering (witchcraft? Alchemy?)
- Anima can:
  - Avoid them (STEALTH or AWARENESS to notice them first)
  - Talk her way out (claim she's gathering for Iskandor, a legitimate herbalist)
  - Fight (not ideal—she's weakened by the hex)
  - Run (back to the city, leaving the moss)

**Success:**
- She collects enough Cloudmoss for Iskandor's purposes
- She returns to the city with the herb
- Iskandor pays her coin and gives her the first elixir (restores D6 WP, cures EXHAUSTION)

---

### Option 2: Mirenna Berry (In the Catacombs)

**The Location:**
- Deep in the catacombs beneath Valehall (connected to or separate from the Underlanes)
- A cold, mountain-like chamber where rare plants grow in darkness
- The air is thin, the stone is slick with moisture
- Small luminescent plants glow faintly, providing eerie light
- The inner catacombs is lined with tombs, indistinguishable figures wrapped in linens with sweet scented dried flowers laid in some

**The Herb:**
- **Mirenna Berries** are small, deep blue-black, and clustered on low shrubs that grow in the coolest parts of the mountain
- They grow only in very specific conditions: cold, dark, high altitude (or the catacombs simulate this)
- Extremely rare and hard to find without knowing exactly where to look
- **Effect:** Heals 10 HP instantly when prepared

**Complications:**

**Getting Lost:**
- The catacombs are labyrinthine
- Anima needs a map, a guide, or exceptional AWARENESS to navigate
- Dead ends and wrong turns waste time and resources

**Crinwin Encounter:**
- Crinwin is a [[Jotun-born]] creature
- Crinwin is not hostile outright but will feast on dead flesh
- But Crinwin's idea of "help" is chaotic:
  - Crinwin offers to show Anima the berries
  - Then deliberately leads her the wrong way, "playfully" trying to get her lost
  - Crinwin finds it hilarious that Anima is confused
  
**What Anima can do about Crinwin:**
- Charm them (music, kindness, understanding of fey nature)
- Bribe them (offer something interesting—coin, a story, an item)
- Outwit them (beat them in a riddle or game)
- Fight them (dangerous—Crinwin is small but quick, and the catacombs are cramped)
- Ignore them and push deeper (risk getting truly lost)

**Success:**
- She finds the Mirenna Berries and harvests them
- She returns to Iskandor
- Iskandor prepares them into a healing tonic (heals 10 HP instantly)

## Iskandor's Repayment

Once Anima completes one or both tasks:

**Ongoing Relationship:**
- Iskandor offers Anima regular work in exchange for learning under him
- She becomes a gatherer/component collector for his elixirs
- This is a **mentor role** for Anima—she can learn herbalism, alchemy, and the craft of elixir-making from him
- It also gives her a place to work and a purpose beyond just survival

**What Iskandor Wants From Her:**
- Rare herbs and components (regular jobs, increasingly dangerous)

## COMPOUNDING MEDICINAL HERBS

Compounding involves combining multiple herbs to create potent mixtures with enhanced effects. This process requires proficiency in ALCHEMY and access to specialized tools like a mortar and pestle or an alchemist’s kit. Successful compounding rolls allow for the creation of advanced healing potions, antidotes, or poisons with increased potency. For example:

1. Healing mixtures may restore additional health points or cure specific ailments.
    
2. Antidotes can neutralize stronger toxins.
    
3. Poisons crafted through compounding may have heightened virulence or secondary effects (e.g., paralysis combined with slow damage).
    

Compounding rolls may be influenced by the rarity of ingredients (same BOONS or BANES based on rarity to find) and environmental conditions (e.g., fresh versus dried herbs). Failure risks wasting materials or creating unstable mixtures that could backfire.

The Compounding process follows these rules:

### 1. Requirements:

♦ A successful Bushcraft roll is required to compound herbs.  
♦ The character must have access to appropriate tools, such as a mortar and pestle or an alchemist’s kit.  
♦ Ingredients must be suitable for the desired mixture (e.g., healing herbs for potions, toxic plants for poisons).

### 2. Effects of Compounding:

♦ Healing Mixtures: Increase the amount of HP restored or add secondary effects, such as curing specific conditions (e.g., EXHAUSTED or DAZED).  
♦ Antidotes: Neutralize poisons with higher potency or broader effects.  
♦ Poisons: Enhance virulence (increase potency rating) or add secondary effects, such as combining paralysis with ongoing damage.

### 3. Potency and Difficulty:

♦ The potency of the resulting mixture determines the difficulty of the compounding roll:  
♦ Weak (Potency 9): Standard roll.  
♦ Moderate (Potency 12): Roll with one BANE.  
♦ Strong (Potency 15+): Roll with two BANEs.

♦ The quality of ingredients affects the roll:  
♦ Fresh ingredients provide a BOON.  
♦ Dried or low-quality ingredients impose a BANE.

---

### 4. Failure Consequences:

♦ On a failed roll, all materials are wasted.  
♦ If the roll fails critically (a DEMON), the mixture becomes unstable and may backfire. For example:  
♦ Healing mixtures could cause EXHAUSTION.  
♦ Antidotes might worsen poison effects.  
♦ Poisons could harm the user instead of the target.

---

### 5. Time and Resources:

♦ Compounding takes one shift per attempt.  
♦ Each attempt consumes a specific quantity of herbs based on the desired potency:  
♦ Weak: 1 unit of herbs.  
♦ Moderate: 2 units of herbs.  
♦ Strong: 3 units of herbs.

---

### 6. Environmental Factors:

♦ Environmental conditions influence the compounding process:  
♦ Working in a controlled environment (e.g., an alchemist’s lab) provides a BOON.  
♦ Harsh conditions (e.g., outdoors in bad weather) impose a BANE.

---

### 7. Stabilizing Mixtures:

♦ Advanced mixtures, such as poisons applied to weapons, require stabilization to prevent degradation over time. This requires an additional successful BUSHCRAFT roll.
