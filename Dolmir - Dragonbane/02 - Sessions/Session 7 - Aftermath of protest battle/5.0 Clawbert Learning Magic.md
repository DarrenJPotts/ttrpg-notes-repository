Clawbert, you sit there within the confines of the Ordo Magi base, your new temporary home. One of many that you have had. You have put the time in, learned the words, deciphered obscured texts and delve into the world of the Arcane.
It was not easy, your mind tends to pull from books to "Greater" cravings but you focused your mind and now believe it is time to attempt...

You set what is needed in place and begin to mutter words that feel foreign to your tongue. You feel a draw, your veins burn and your blood tingles, as if your body is in the midst of falling asleep and the pins and needles kick in. It feels oddly good... and then it doesnt. Your vision tunnels and a drip is felt at the end of your snout... blood.

Trying again, you settle your mind and begin the incarnation once more. The same sensation runs over you and your vision beings to narrow once more, but it does not feel as sickening as the first time. Instead your veins tingle and burn to a point where it begins to overwhelm. Something shifts. You feel warm and alive, in your veins, your surroundings. You don't just feel *power*. You feel **Dolmir** and you burn with a spark of potential
 
But the cost is to be paid. A personal. 

*Who are you?* - a voice whispers.

**After the second**: Merry appears laying asleep in the bed. The same one back in **Mountside**. He lay there asleep. Your mind... it desires, it rumbles with a quiet hunger.

*Who are you?* - it booms once more, you cannot decipher what the voice is, it is understanding. It just is. 

Scene after scene, each item in your collection, flesh, bone, hair, piece by piece. Each face a distinct memory unforgotten. Image after image.  Every claw. Every tooth. Every lock of hair from a person you've met. Every piece you severed from the living whole.

*What are you?* - it whispers once more

You stand before a large tree, with an indistinguishable carved face in it. Your mind touches something vast living yet dying at the same time. Warm as a hearth but cold as ice. New and old. Death and rebirth. And it can *feel* you.

Each spell you cast, you must hold that knowing: Hunger. Deep hunger.
