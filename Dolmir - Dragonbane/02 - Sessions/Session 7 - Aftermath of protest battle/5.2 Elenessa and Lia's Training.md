
## Setup

Lia and Elenessa are called in by [[Ariel Enel]] following the ambush with [[Lyra Lovecraft]]. The Lucky Charm is quiet but tense. Blood has been spilled. Questions are being asked. Ariel sees opportunity in crisis — both characters need grounding, guidance, and a path forward.

## Elenessa: The Still Gate

[[Ariel Enel]] and **Serilye** (the Eldar woman from the initial Lucky Charm scene) recognize something in Elenessa

**What they teach:**
- Nissail - The Gentle Blade path begins immediately. Elenessa has already witnessed Serilye's restraint and control.
- **Tier 1: The Hold** becomes available as Elenessa begins training. When she's ready to learn it (shifts + successful roll), the mechanics unlock.
- Training focuses on precision, leverage, and reading an opponent's body — themes Elenessa already understands from her childhood spent studying people.
- Serilye becomes her ongoing mentor: patient, Eldar, principled. She teaches through sparring and problem-solving, not lecture.

**Narrative gate for Tier 2:**
- Elenessa must work with monster components (harvest something from a slain creature) and use it deliberately in a hold/control situation
- Or she must face a moment where restraint costs her more than force, and she chooses restraint anyway

**Note on pacing:**
- Elenessa's Brawling skill determines when she can unlock The Hold (Brawling 14 required)
- She's just beginning. Tier 1 discovery is this session's gift if the narrative supports it. Higher tiers come later.

## Lia & Lily: The Twin Accord

[[Ariel Enel]] pulls Lia aside after the ambush. 

"Five people dead. Although deserved in a sense. There is lack of hesitation. You kill because it is easy."

*Is this what you want the bond to become?*

**What she teaches:**
- The Twin Accord begins in play, unlocked through moments of true coordination under pressure.
- Lia and Lily have already demonstrated synchronization. The path is recognizing what's already there and deepening it.
- **Tier 1: The Opening** becomes available as they recognize their ability to create opportunities for each other in combat.
- Training focuses on *controlled* coordination — using their bond tactically without losing themselves to it.

**The tension:**
- Lia is cold to death. Hardened. Detached from the killing. She says: *"I will kill to protect those I love."*
- [[Ariel Enel]] recognizes this as dangerous — not because killing is wrong, but because Lia is using it to distance herself from Lily.
- The Twin Accord teaches synchronization, but it also creates vulnerability: separation becomes a tactical threat, a wound to both.
- Ariel will **warn** Lia that this path demands presence, not detachment. That using Lily as a tool rather than a partner will eventually break something.

**The critical warning:**
- Ariel takes Lia aside privately. *"Your companion is fragile. Not weak — fragile. A changeling's form is not fixed. It is shaped by what they become, who they are treated as, what they accept about themselves."*
- If Lia continues to treat Lily as a weapon, a tool, or a means to an end — if she distances herself and uses Lily's bond as leverage rather than reciprocating genuine presence — Lily will begin to drift.
- *"She will become less person and more beast. Like the dopplers of old stories — beautiful on the surface, hollow underneath. She wants to be human, Lia. But if you make her into something you use, she will stop trying."*
- Conversely: if Lia chooses genuine reciprocity, presence, and partnership — if she allows Lily to matter equally in the bond — Lily's form will stabilize and strengthen. She will become more herself, not less.
- This is not the path's mechanic. This is the cost of walking it poorly.

**Narrative gate for Tier 2:**
- Lia must harvest monster components (work with Lily to bring something down and deliberately use its parts)
- Or she must face a moment where protecting Lily costs her something real, and she pays it willingly

**On Lily's form:**
- Ariel will note that Lily's changeling nature is compatible with the Twin Accord — the bond can help anchor her when her form wavers
- But Lily must want this bond. Lia cannot force synchronization, only invite it.

## GM Guidance

- **Both paths unlock through play, not through NPC gates.** Ariel teaches the philosophy. The players demonstrate mastery through their choices and actions.
- **Elenessa's path is about restraint.** Victories where she *doesn't* kill. Moments where control is harder than force, and she chooses it anyway.
- **Lia's path is about presence.** She must learn to fight *with* Lily, not through her. The bond is a strength only if both are fully engaged.
- **Tier 1 abilities can be available this session** if the narrative supports it (successful training roll + clear character motivation).
- **Higher tiers come later**, gated by story moments and challenges that deepen the characters' understanding of the paths.
