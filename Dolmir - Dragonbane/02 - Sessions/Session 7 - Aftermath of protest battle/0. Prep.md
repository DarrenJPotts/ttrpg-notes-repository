Bells ring through the air, echoing brief cuts in the screams of protest. Shouts pour in from all angles... Smoke billows from nearby streets. Panic, rage, chaos. Pure chaos. Not just here but throughout the streets of the Valehall... the smell of fire burns at your nostrils, adrenaline dumping from your bodies as the fight ends while the chaos ensues. 

"Oi You there! Halt!", a gruff voice shouts from nearby. 

You see Valeguard flooding in from the streets around you, choking out pathways in and out. The guard charges and spreads forth, you see those who attacked but fled get caught, some tackled and some slammed to prevent espace. 
The Valeguard who shouted, a well built man with a large frame and gruff beard, pushes forward to your group. 

The way in and out is blocked, those who ran or attacked have been apprehended. 

What do you do?

If look around: 
The battle that occurred moments ago had not fully registered in your minds. You reacted on instinct, in defence, for survival... or some of you did. The scene unfolds before you, painted on a tapestry of dirt and viscera, throats cut and their blood bubbles out, last breaths escaping, arrows pierce and maim and bones crushed under the weight of great hammers.  The blood pools into puddles, mixing with dirt and water. In the near distance you hear the cries of a child, lost in the chaos looking for their parent, maybe they lie amongst the dead. And you, although the intent ended up deadly. You can only count three whom had weapons of any import, three who stand out amongst the rest.

If oppose: 
You attempt to oppose the Valeguard, getting off a swift kick knocking one off balance, a punch that cracks against another's jaw and you weave past the grasp of another and you succeed in the futility of it all before you feel a hard knock at the base of your skull where head meats neck as vision blurs.

If not: 
You raise your hands in compliance as they pull your arms behind your back, pushing you to your knees or kicking them out from you. 

You hear, *"Round em up, we will deal with this lot soon enough"* before you begin to fade.
