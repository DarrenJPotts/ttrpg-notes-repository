#### Hunting for Merchant Jefferson 

**The Missing Merchant:**
- [[Aldous Jefferson]] disappeared while searching the Underlanes for mysterious creatures called the **Crin/Crinwin**
- He was hired [[Cilius Val]] and the Shamblers to guide him through the Underlanes
- Believed the Crinwin's existence is tied to Anima's curse

**Pip Giles (The Apprentice):**
- Jefferson's young apprentice (13 years old) went searching for him in the Underlanes
- Pip is now **missing and dying somewhere in the catacombs**
- Left a note and box at Jefferson's home
- Box contains **2 fingers and a ransom demand** (signed "C" — Shamblers underlings, not [[Cilius Val]] himself)

**The Betrayal:**
- [[Cilius Val]] and the Shamblers work with [[The Smiling Blade]] (Old Order Inquisitor)
- Jefferson was **captured and betrayed** deep in the Underlanes
- The Shamblers are a criminal faction controlling most of the Underlanes

**What's Happening:**
- Party needs to navigate the **Underlanes/Catacombs** to find both Jefferson and Pip
- Map available: [[Underlanes Map]]
- Cilius Val won't care if his men die, but the ransom suggests they want payment or leverage

#### Iskandor's Quests

**The Alchemist:**
- [[Iskandor]] is an older Eldar herbalist running an apothecary in the Vintage District
- He's become a **mentor figure to Anima** for the Green Wyrd/herbalist craft

**Herb-Gathering Missions:**
- **Cloudmoss (Outside City):** Tall grass fields north of Valehall, cures exhaustion + restores WP
  - Complication: Old Order Inquisitors patrolling the area
- **Mirenna Berries (Catacombs):** Deep mountain chambers, heals 10 HP when prepared
  - Complication: Labyrinthine paths, risk of getting lost, potential encounter with [[Crinwin]] (Jotun-born, chaotic)

**Iskandor's Offer:**
- Regular work in exchange for learning alchemy and herbalism
- Becomes a **gathering/component collector** for his elixirs
- Provides ongoing mentorship and a place to work

**Status:**
- [[Anima Vossel]] has received work from Iskandor to retrieve herbs
- Connection to her Green Wyrd heroic path unlocking

#### The woman of the Lucky Charm

**The Location & Owner:**
- [[The Lucky Charm]] — tavern and house in Lower Quarter
- Run by [[Ariel Enel]], an Eldar woman of mysterious origin (possibly former nobility)
- Neutral ground with strict rules: **No one lays a hand on any woman of the house**

**Work/Jobs at the Charm:**
- [[Elenessa Vaeloriane]] and [[Lia]] have been tasked to work there under Ariel
- Possible jobs include: protecting patrons, information gathering, attending the Guild house play at Triumph Amphitheatre

**Key NPCs:**
- **Ariel Enel:** Owner, mysterious, protective of her staff
- **Kess:** Fighter/companion, trains others, knows the lower quarter
- **Marta:** Manager — owns/runs the establishment
- **Islas:** Refugee worker, knows about the Blight spreading

**Information & Hooks:**
- Sells intelligence: faction activity, NPC locations, dangerous knowledge (25-50+ coin)
- Neutral meeting ground for various factions
- Connection to **Brawlers Den** through Kess
- Safe haven if party builds relationships

**Current Events:**
- Play moved to **Triumph Amphitheatre tonight** — Ariel wants eyes and ears there
- Staff will be working the venue
- Party expected to "pass" by attending or gathering intel
- Potential assassination plot involving ambassadors
