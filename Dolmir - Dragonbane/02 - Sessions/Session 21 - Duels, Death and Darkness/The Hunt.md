A mock hunting contest is held within the nearby woodlands.
Contestants are given a quarry and must track, locate and successfully hunt or capture it before returning to the tournament grounds.
The quarry may be a released animal, planted trail or marked target.

Contestants are free to employ whatever methods they believe will succeed.

Possible approaches include:
The Hunt
✦ Bushcraft to follow tracks
✦ Spot Hidden to locate signs
✦ Awareness to predict the quarry’s movements
✦ Bows or Crossbows to make the final shot
✦ Traps
✦ Dogs
✦ Ambushes
✦ Climbing for a better vantage point
✦ Following or misleading rival contestants

A Hunt May Become More
The woods surrounding the tournament grounds are
not controlled as easily as the Lists.
Competitors may discover tracks that were never
planted, animals that were never released, or some
thing within the forest that has been hunting them in
return.

The judges consider speed, skill and method when deciding the final placements.

The Hunts
- Bear - Will take a few shots, will charge and attack
- Deer - Spooks when closing the distance and runs
- Fox - Will mislead and hide
- Goat - Up high on a rock face
- Wild Boar - Will charge, on success run away
- Wolf - Will be a pack ambush

##### GM Rolls
**Rival Rolls**
d6	Result
1	Danger: Roll on the casualty table below.
2	Setback: Lose their trail, equipment or time.
3–4	Progress: Find tracks or close on their quarry.
5	Opportunity: Reach their quarry; next turn, capture it on 3+.
6	Success: Secure their quarry.

**Casualties**
d6	Consequence
1	Equipment destroyed or animal escapes; no injury.
2	Minor injury; can continue.
3	Serious injury; abandons the hunt.
4	Incapacitated; needs rescue.
5	Dying; prompt treatment can save them.
6	Killed.

**Lyra Target**
d4	Target

3	Houndmaster - DEAD

**Lyra Kill**
d6	Result
1–2	Cannot find an isolated target.
3	Shadows a rival; leaves evidence someone could notice; Attacking them in a battle or they are attacked and escape, roll on targets for who they run into 
4	Assassinates Rival

##### Player Turns
Each player in a row must find the Quarry, Close the distance and take the prize

| Round                  | Player decision                                                          | What changes                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Find the trail**     | Follow tracks, scout, use animals or shadow a rival                      | They discover where their quarry went—and evidence of other hunters  |
| **Close the distance** | Take a shortcut, set an ambush, pursue extra prey or investigate trouble | Rival plans and Lyra’s interference become apparent                  |
| **Take the prize**     | Capture, kill, rescue someone or abandon the pursuit                     | Their choices determine placement and what comes back from the woods |
##### Complications
Use Round 1: Track → Round 2: Approach → Round 3: Capture/Kill. 

| Quarry        | Round 1 — Track               | Round 2 — Approach                              | Round 3 — Finish                                                          |
| ------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bear**      | Find obvious tracks.          | Get into shooting position.                     | Survives the first shot and charges; resolve combat.                      |
| **Deer**      | Follow its trail.             | Sneak closer; failure spooks it.                | Take the shot. If spooked, spend this round catching up instead.          |
| **Fox**       | Untangle doubled-back tracks. | Locate its hiding place.                        | Cover its escape route or it escapes, requiring another approach.         |
| **Goat**      | Tracks lead to a cliff.       | Climb or lure it down.                          | Secure it; a failed approach leaves it out of reach this round.           |
| **Wild Boar** | Follow churned earth.         | It spots the hunters and charges.               | If its charge hits, it flees: track again. Otherwise, attempt the finish. |
| **Wolf**      | Follow a lone wolf’s tracks.  | Notice the circling pack or enter their ambush. | Resolve the fight—or escape without a quarry.                             |
