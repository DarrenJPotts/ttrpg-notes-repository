A wooden fortification has been constructed upon the tournament grounds.
Two teams compete: Attackers and Defenders.
The attackers must breach the fortification and capture the banner flying from its centre.
To succeed they must:
1. Carry ladders to the walls.
2. Cross the defensive ditch.
3. Reach or breach the walls.
4. Enter the fort.
5. Capture the banner.

The defenders are permitted to use:
✦ Sacks of flour
✦ Padded stones
✦ Buckets of water
✦ Blunted weapons
✦ Shields
✦ Wooden barricades

The event is intended to simulate the chaos of a real siege without the slaughter.

**Distinction in the Siege** 
Golden Marks are not automatically awarded to every member of the winning team.
Instead, the tournament judges select the three contestants who distinguished themselves most during the battle.
They may come from either side.

Distinction

Third Distinction - 1 

A defender who holds the gate alone may
earn greater honour than an attacker who
simply happens to be on the victorious side

#### Defenders
**Defender Roles**
- Gate Guard
- Wall Defenders 
- Flag defenders

Each Defender team gets a budget of 3 Defence points

#### Attackers
**Equipment**
- Two Ladders
- One Ram
- Padded weapons and Shields

#### How It Plays
- **Before the horn:** Defenders spend points and explain placements. Attackers get a brief planning discussion.
- **During play:** Use normal combat turns. Defenders choose between holding positions, triggering obstacles and making sorties.
- **Taking the banner:** Requires an action and one free hand. A dropped banner stays where it falls—no automatic reset.
