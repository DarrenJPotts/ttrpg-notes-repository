### Day 1: The Jousts
The first day of the tournament starts with the Jousts. Here Knights of all standings participate against each other for the honour and boon of House Hathor. 
- Delegations of [[Vaelor II]] and [[Halthor III]] compete to win over neutral houses and display their strength 
- Hedge Knights compete to show their worth in hopes to earn a spot within a Great House's employ. 
- Other compete for honour or riches.

#### Winners
Match 1: [[Halvard Snow|Sir Halvard Snow]] (Neutral) vs. Sir Gregor Vorn (Vaelor's Flag)
**Outcome:** Halvard wins 2-1 (spectacular joust, Halvard's superior horsemanship on display)]]

Match 2: Sir Thadden the Yellow (Halthor's Banner) vs. Sir Eirik Stoneheart (Great House — House Mirenhall, neutral leaning Halthor)
**Outcome:** Thadden wins 2-0 (brutal, efficient, shows training)

Match 3: Sir Bowen of Templewall (Vaelor-aligned mercenary) vs. Sir Gawain of The Green Hedge (Halthor Knight)
**Outcome:** Sir Gawain wins 2-0 (brutal, efficient, shows training)

Match 4: Sir Aldrick of Crossfen (Great House — House Haganor, neutral) vs. Sir Petyr of Millbrook (Halthor's squire-knight)

Match 5: Sir Caeran the Black vs. Sir Thadden the Yellow
Outcome:** Sir Thadden the Yellow wins 3-0 (brutal, efficient, shows training)

Match 6: Sir Thadden the Yellow (Halthor's flag from earlier) vs. Sir Halvard Snow
Outcome: Undecided.
