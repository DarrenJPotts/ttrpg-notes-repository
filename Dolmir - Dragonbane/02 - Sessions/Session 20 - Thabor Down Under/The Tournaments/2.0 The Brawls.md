## Single-Elimination Bracket
**Four entrants:** Elenessa, Pella, Borun, and Dorrik. Dorrik fights alone; Neris and Saelith below are reserve opponents or optional exhibitions, not extra bracket entrants.

> “One defeat ends your run for the title! Semi-final losers return for the bronze bout. Honour the yield, and let the best brawler stand!”

| Bout | Round       | Fighters             | Winner | Result |
| ---- | ----------- | -------------------- | ------ | ------ |
| A    | Semi-final  | Elenessa vs Pella    | E      | KO     |
| B    | Semi-final  | Borun vs Dorrik      | B      | KO     |
| C    | Third place | Loser A vs Loser B   |        |        |
| D    | Final       | Winner A vs Winner B | E      | Hits   |

- **Advance:** Semi-final winners enter the final. Losers cannot win the title but fight for third place.
- **Marks:** Final winner gets **3**, runner-up **2**, third-place winner **1**, fourth place **0**.
- **Between bouts:** Allow a stretch rest; clear temporary bout effects and reset scores to 0–0. Once-per-bout tricks and abilities refresh.
- **NPC bouts:** Resolve offscreen with the linked rules and record the result. No predetermined winner.
- **Safety:** Yield ends the bout immediately. A disqualified fighter loses their bout. If a fighter cannot continue, their next opponent advances by walkover; no eliminated fighter re-enters the title bracket.
- **Changes:** Substitute reserves or additional PCs before the first bout and announce a revised bracket. Neris and Dorrik's tag-team exhibition is separate from this individual competition and awards no extra marks.

## At a Glance
- **Win:** First to 3 scoring hits, knockout, or surrender. No HP damage.
- **Each round:** Secretly choose moves, reveal together, resolve, then resolve crowd influence if the bout continues.
- **Tricks:** Once per bout per fighter. Show the tell before move selection. No extra actions or hits; use normal BOON/BANE handling.
- **Counterplay:** Let players prevent a trick through a sensible response; roll a relevant skill only if uncertain. Preventing it spends its use. Exposed cheats risk a warning or disqualification; legitimate edges are legal.
- **Timing:** Activate tricks after moves are revealed but before rolls, except Saelith's preparation, which follows a successful Evade. Never change a committed move.

### Move Key
| Move | Effect if it wins |
| --- | --- |
| Attack | Score 1 hit. |
| Evade | Stop an offensive move. |
| Counter | Evade with BANE; success stops the move and allows one scoring attack. |
| Trip | No score. Beats normal Attack on success; BOON on next round's attack. |
| Liver Shot | Attack with BANE; score 1 hit and give enemy attacks BANE next round. |
| Stun | Attack with BANE; no score, but enemy must choose Evade next round. |

Use the linked rules for contests, ties, knockouts, and crowd effects. Maximum 1 scoring hit per round. Next-round effects expire even if unused.

## 1. Pella "Anklebiter" Reed
**Halfling drover.** Cheerful, quick, and determined to humble tall people.

> "You can laugh now. Harder with the wind knocked out of you."

**Stats:** Agile Brawling 12 | Brawling 10 | Evade 11 | CON 10

| Move        | Use                         |
| ----------- | --------------------------- |
| Body Blow   | Attack — Agile Brawling     |
| Low Tackle  | Trip — Brawling             |
| Duck Away   | Evade                       |
| Windbreaker | Liver Shot — Agile Brawling |

- **Plan:** Low Tackle → Body Blow with the BOON. Use Windbreaker against persistent attackers.
- **Tell:** Looks at knees before tackling; bounces on her toes before striking or retreating.
- **Weakness:** A failed Low Tackle roll gives her opponent BOON on one attack next round. A successful roll that loses the contest does not count.
- **Beat her:** Counter the tackle; punish a failed attempt.
- **Afterwards:** Praises clever winners; mocks brute force.

### Cheat: Grit in the Eyes
- **Effect:** Once per bout, with Low Tackle, throw dust: opponent's Evade roll has BANE this exchange, including Counter's Evade. No effect against offensive moves.
- **Tell:** Scoops dust and keeps one fist closed.
- **Stop it:** Demand a hand inspection, avoid the dusty hand, or alert the referee.

## 2. Borun "The Anvil" Kegborn
**Dwarf quarry worker.** Beard tucked into his belt; waits for opponents to come to him.

> "Take your time. I'm paid by the fight."

**Stats:** Brawling 14 | Evade 12 | CON 15

| Move         | Use                              |
| ------------ | -------------------------------- |
| Hammer Punch | Attack — Brawling                |
| Closed Guard | Counter — Brawling counterattack |
| Brace        | Evade                            |
| Anvil Blow   | Stun — Brawling                  |

- **Plan:** Defend first; Counter frequent attacks. Swing when opponents hesitate. Follow Stun with Hammer Punch.
- **Tell:** Raised forearms mean defence; a drawn-back fist means a heavy blow.
- **Weakness:** A failed Hammer Punch roll gives his next round's Evade BANE, including Counter's Evade. A blocked successful attack does not count.
- **Beat him:** Bait a missed swing, then attack. Trip beats Hammer Punch, but Closed Guard can stop Trip.
- **Afterwards:** Respects restraint and repays mercy.

### Upper Hand: Quarry Footing
- **Effect:** Once per bout, Brace against Trip gains BOON on Evade. Does not apply to Closed Guard.
- **Tell:** Spreads his boots and settles onto his rear heel.
- **Stop it:** Draw him off his footing. If he commits the edge and you choose anything other than Trip, it is wasted.

## 3. Neris and Dorrik — "Silk and Stone"
**Elf fencing tutor and dwarf debt collector.** Affectionate bickering; rehearsed teamwork.

> **Neris:** "Try not to embarrass us."
> **Dorrik:** "Which of us?"

| Fighter | Attack | Evade | CON |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neris | Agile Brawling 14 | 13 | 10 |
| Dorrik | Brawling 13 | 10 | 14 |

### Tag-Team Rules
- Share one score and choose **one move per round**, not one each.
- Announce the leader before secretly choosing moves. Only that fighter acts, defends, and rolls CON against knockout.
- Either fighter's knockout or surrender ends the bout for both.
- Trip, Stun, Liver Shot, and crowd effects carry over to the team even when the leader changes.
- **Solo Dorrik:** Use only his moves, stats, weakness, and cheat; no tag-team benefits.

| Fighter | Move | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neris | Quick Jab | Attack — Agile Brawling |
| Neris | Slip Away | Evade |
| Neris | Bait and Strike | Counter — Agile Brawling counterattack |
| Neris | Precision Blow | Liver Shot — Agile Brawling |
| Dorrik | Heavy Fist | Attack — Brawling |
| Dorrik | Pin Against the Post | Trip — Brawling |
| Dorrik | Block the Route | Evade |
| Dorrik | Bell Ringer | Stun — Brawling |

- **Plan:** Dorrik Trips → Neris attacks with the BOON. Occasionally break the pattern.
- **Tells:** Dorrik spreads his hands before a pin; Neris studies breathing before Precision Blow.
- **Weakness:** A failed Dorrik Trip roll gives the opponent BOON on one attack next round.
- **Beat them:** Counter Dorrik's setup; expect attacks or Counter when Neris leads.
- **Afterwards:** Protective of each other, even in defeat.

### Neris's Upper Hand: The Tutor's Feint
- **Effect:** Once per bout, Quick Jab gives the opponent's Evade BANE, including Counter's Evade. No effect against offensive moves.
- **Tell:** Offers her chin while watching the opponent's hips.
- **Stop it:** Watch her centre, warn your fighter, or choose an offensive move.

### Dorrik's Cheat: The Hidden Heel
- **Effect:** Once per bout, gain BOON on Pin Against the Post by hooking a heel and illegally grabbing clothing. Neris takes no extra action.
- **Tell:** Reaches for a belt or loose hem.
- **Stop it:** Keep clothing out of reach or tell the referee to watch his hand.

## 4. Saelith "The Last Bell"
**Elf bodyguard.** Removes her former employer's signet before entering; insists on clean fighting.

> "You've proved you can hurt people. Now show me you can fight."

**Stats:** Agile Brawling 16 | Evade 15 | CON 13

| Move | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Measured Strike | Attack — Agile Brawling |
| Read and Retreat | Evade |
| Decisive Counter | Counter — Agile Brawling counterattack; once per bout |
| Break Their Rhythm | Trip — Agile Brawling |
| Temple Strike | Stun — Agile Brawling |

- **Plan:** Open with Evade. Trip repeated attacks; save Counter for a likely offensive move. Follow Stun with Measured Strike.
- **Tells:** Watches shoulders before defending; lowers her stance before Trip.
- **Read the Habit:** Warn before choices that she is watching for a repeat. If the opponent repeats their previous round's offensive move, her Evade against it gains BOON, including Counter's Evade. No benefit if she chooses an offensive move. Different narration does not count as a different move.
- **Weakness:** A failed Decisive Counter attack roll gives her opponent BOON on one attack next round.
- **Beat her:** Vary moves; bait her one Counter and exploit a missed counterattack.
- **Afterwards:** Stops immediately at surrender.

### Upper Hand: A Bodyguard's Patience
- **Effect:** Once per bout, after Read and Retreat stops an offensive move, prepare BOON on next round's Measured Strike. Announce it before move selection; choosing anything else wastes it.
- **Tell:** Holds her ground and watches the shoulder that just struck.
- **Stop it:** Trip her prepared Attack, or defend against it. She gains no immediate counterattack.
