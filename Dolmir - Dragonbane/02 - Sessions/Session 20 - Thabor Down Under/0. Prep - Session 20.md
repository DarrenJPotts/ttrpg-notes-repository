## Session Info
**Where we left off:** 
The Veil had opened... The Gates of the Reverie poured forth with rain, thunder and spirit. While Lia and Elenessa ran their way to Eldarmoore, Anima and Clawbert sought answers.
Lia you ran ahead of Elenessa as you knew the time was close before the Exaltation Army would begin handing out tainted supplies. You ran through mud and muck to stop them just in time, convincing them to better judgement and preventing anymore needless deaths. While Elenessa you were driven by pure instinct, instinct of who needed your help, and you found them in the Centre. Over-crowded and underhoused. Where Lester Gallbeck ruled this small plot of land as a landlord gone wrong. He had some words of course, but you spoke louder with action, breaking his nose and convincing those to follow you instead.
The first time you have taken the reigns of leadership. 

But that victory was short-lived as Burning Corpses befell on the camp, attacking your group and Lia's group as you two were separated. Both managing to survive. 

While Clawbert and Anima dove through notes to find answers, discovering what laid behind the tear in the sky, known as the Reverie - the Bridge to distant shores. Coming to the conclusion that they might need more help than they realise, with the one person Anima did not want to ask; Lady Ariel Enel and after a brief discussion a meeting was called within the groups home.

Here, it was decided to climb the towering building of the Stone Seat and perform a ritual at the pinnacle of the Stone Prow. Altes acting as a source of power while Lady Enel acting as an anchor for the barrier. Waves of enemies began to descend on the party while Clawbert spoke ancient words of power to close the tear. 

The battle was hard fought and just when success was thought to be had, a new being descended, an Arcane Horror. A magical creature of hunger and darkness and so one more battle ensued until eventually Anima delivered the final blow with Lia's ornamental dagger destroying the creature. Victory, the demon defeated and the Veil closed... but the world forever changed. 

We shall see what is to come....
