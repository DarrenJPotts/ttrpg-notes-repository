Altes stops in front of a building that does not invite attention. That is the point. It sits in the lower district on a lane that smells of tallow candles and old wood, wedged between a cooper's storage yard and whatever the Lucky Charm is — the sign above its door depicts a horseshoe and a woman's silhouette and the sounds from within suggest a lively evening trade. The safehouse by contrast is dark, its lower windows boarded, its door swollen in its frame and wearing the general expression of a building that has given up.

Altes produces a key that should not fit the lock. It fits the lock.

_"Mind the step,"_ he says, and goes in.

**What They Find**

The door opens into a narrow front room that sells the abandoned story convincingly. Broken furniture. A collapsed shelf. Plaster off one wall in a long stripe. Dust on every surface that has been there long enough to settle into something permanent. A fireplace that has not been used in years, to look at it.

Altes crosses to the back wall and presses a specific stone at waist height. A section of the facade wall swings inward — not dramatically, just enough — revealing the actual room beyond.

It is small but it is functional. A table with two chairs. A hearth that has been used recently, ash still warm if anyone checks. A shelf of books wrapped in oilcloth. Basic provisions — dried meat, hard bread, a stoppered bottle of something that turns out to be surprisingly good wine. A second shelf holds equipment the party cannot immediately identify: small tools, glass vessels, a locked iron box the size of a fist that Altes moves to his side of the table without comment.

The ceiling is low. The single window looks onto a blank wall. But it is dry and it locks from both sides and nobody on this lane is the kind of person who asks questions about their neighbours.

_"Home,"_ Altes says, in the tone of a man who means it loosely.

**Secrets and Clues**

**The Space Itself**
- The facade room is the cover. If anyone forces the front door they find an abandoned building and leave satisfied. The partition wall from the inside looks like plaster and timber. From the outside it is indistinguishable from the rest of the structure
    - Altes explains this once and does not repeat it: _"The front room is what anyone who comes looking finds. Everything that matters is behind the wall. If you hear someone at the door you close the partition and you do not breathe loudly."_
- The building is owned on paper by a trading concern that dissolved twelve years ago. Altes does not explain how he knows this or how he obtained the key
- The Lucky Charm opposite is useful. The lane sees enough evening traffic that people coming and going from the safehouse do not stand out. Altes has clearly factored this into the location's appeal and says so without embarrassment

**The Money Problem**

- The safehouse has provisions for perhaps a week. Beyond that the party will need coin
- Altes has resources but they are not liquid — his assets are in favours, information and materials, none of which spend easily at a bread stall
    - _"The Warden's Register is two streets north. Mehira posts contracts that do not require lengthy explanation of who you are or where you came from. I would suggest starting there."_
- He will not send them on jobs blind. Before they take anything he wants to know what it is and who posted it. He has reasons for this that he does not fully explain yet
- The odd jobs available in the lower district have a pattern to them that Altes has noticed and has not mentioned. Several of them concern the same area of the southern lower district. The same area where the militia patrol went missing

**Anima's Curse — The Investigation**

- Once the party has settled Altes clears the table and looks at Anima directly for the first time since they entered
    - _"What you told me at camp. I want to hear it again here, properly, with nothing missing."_
- He listens without interrupting. When she finishes he is quiet for a long moment. Then he opens one of the oilcloth books and begins looking for something
    - He does not tell them what he is looking for. He does not tell them what he suspects. He will say only: _"This is not common. That is either very bad or very interesting. Possibly both."_
- The investigation will take time and materials he does not currently have. He gives Anima a short list written in his cramped hand
    - The list contains three items. Two are obtainable in Valehall's market district without difficulty. The third is listed simply as _Kingswood resin, unblighted_ with a small question mark beside it as though he added it hoping the problem would solve itself
- The locked iron box on the shelf contains something relevant to Anima's condition. Altes knows this. He is not ready to open it yet because opening it means committing to a theory he has not finished testing
    - If someone asks about the box he picks it up, considers it, and puts it back
    - _"Soon,"_ he says. _"When I am sure."_

**The Wall Behind the Wall**

- Behind the investigation table, barely visible in the low light, the inner wall has markings on it. Not decoration. Working notes in the same script as Altes's journal, diagrams that mean nothing to the party yet, and in the centre a rough map of something that takes a moment to identify
    - It is the Kingswood. Marked with locations, annotations, and in one corner a word written and then crossed out and written again: _alive?_
    - Altes has not pointed this out. He may not have intended them to see it yet

**The Lucky Charm — Across the Lane**

Not a threat. Not immediately a hook. But a presence worth establishing:

- The proprietor is a woman named **Orla** who has been on this lane longer than any other business and intends to remain so
- She noticed the safehouse light the first night and has said nothing because in this lane noticing things and saying nothing is the social contract
- She hears things. Her clientele includes militia, merchants, and at least one person who works in the Hathor administrative offices. She does not sell what she hears but she is not above trading it for the right thing
- She will acknowledge the party if they acknowledge her. She will not be the first to speak
