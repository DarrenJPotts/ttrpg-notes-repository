### Event- Ashveil Refugees
Around a bend in the road, a small camp sits just off the verge. Two carts, canvas lean-tos strung between them, a fire that has been burning long enough to settle into embers. An older woman is folding a blanket with the focused care of someone who has very little left to fold carefully. 
A child sits on a cart wheel watching the road. When the party passes, the woman looks up. Her eyes move across the group and stop.
She knows where they are from. 
She says nothing, but she nods, putting her hand softly on her chest before going back to her folding.
