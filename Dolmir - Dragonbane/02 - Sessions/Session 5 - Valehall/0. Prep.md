### Strong Start
Sleep swept over the camp in quick succession due to the exertion and adrenaline of the day before. You had left the Vale, leaving behind its rolling hills you farmed in, the river you played in and the beds you laid your heads to rest. What followed was The Vale Gorge which seemed an insurmountable task. To walk through the encampment of those whom caused such destruction and upheaval to your lives. To hide your faces, ears and kin and to make it out of the other side feels like it was ripped straight out of a folk tale.
Plans were made, sec d the journey began.
And as you rest your heads on soft ground of your camp, the skies clear and the stars shine bright, swirling in a mix of golds and whites. Your eyes close, dreams follow and soon the golden light of the sun crests the East.

You awake to hear ruffling around the campfire, brought to life with a bit more warmth than the previous night. [[Altes Lesym]] crouches over the fire, prodding the mornings breakfast.
