The gates creek open and you entre through the arch of the gatehouse, entering into the city and it is overwhelming, sounds hit your ears with the constant noise of people, voice layered over each other, town criers shouting news, cart wheels on stone, children running, dogs, cattle, arguments. Twelve thousand people fill this city on a normal day. 

You smell woodsmoke, fresh baked bread. You smell animals that pass you by. People move with purposeful indifference, some bump past, around you, past you. You have to step to the side else be knocked by a hurried cart. 

An old man sits on the steps of a building doing nothing outside of smoking his pipe. 

And above you the mountains high, passed the walls, past the people, the Stone Seat of [[Reindell Hathor]].

It is lively, it is bustling, it is full. 

### Ordo Magi Path
[[Altes Lesym]] will tell them he will meet up with them at the memorial plaza before sundown. He has some business to attend
	- *"I have things to attend to before sundown. There is a memorial plaza, you will find it if you follow the main road north and take the second left after the tannery smell fades. You cannot miss it. There is a fountain that no longer works and a very old tree that still does."*
	- *"Be there before sundown."

Then he is gone, moving into the crowd with the unhurried efficiency of a man who knows exactly where he is going and very much does not want company getting there.

#### Secrets and Clues
- The memorial plaza is a quiet square in the middle district, built around a old fountain and a broad-trunked oak. Stone plaques line the surrounding walls, names of the valiant heroes who died in the [[Second Long War]] and [[Rite of Purification]], in its centre The Saint Odellia
- Altes is late. Not by much, but enough to notice. When he arrives he is moving faster than his usual pace. His shoulder has some ash on it.
    - He does not explain either thing
- He leads them through the lower district without a word, taking turns that feel deliberate, never the shortest route, always with a sightline back the way they came
- The building he brings them to is in the Lower Quarter, wedged between a cooperage and a grain store. It looks abandoned convincingly, boards on the lower windows, door swollen in its frame. Altes produces a key that should not fit the lock and opens it without difficulty
    - Inside it is not abandoned. A table, chairs, a hearth that has been used recently, a shelf of books wrapped in oilcloth, basic provisions. Someone has been here before them and left it ready
    - _"The Ordo Magi have maintained a presence in Valehall for a long time,"_ he says, setting his bag on the table. _"Mostly gone. But this will do."_
- He will answer two questions about the room and then stop answering questions about the room
- The ash on his sleeve came from a document he burned. He will not say where or what it contained. He will say, if pressed, that it was something the Order should not find. He says this the way people say things they have already decided not to elaborate on
- Now that they are part of the order, he will mention the reason he is here. 
	- *The Ordo Magi have long since been a thorn in the side of The Old Order. We are researchers, record keepers, explorers of the Arcanum. And heretics if you go by their point of view.*
	- *The Order are up to something, and the current Ordo Magi are spread thin... too thin. I am trying to bring to light their deeds. Not the burnings... no offense, that is light work compared to their goal. I believe they mean to plunge the world into dark... although unintentionally I think*

### The Military Path
[[Rewyn Fosley]] directs a few new recruits and the group into the city. A different guard stamps something in a ledger without looking up. Clay militia tokens are pressed into each palm — small, stamped with the Hathor mark, still warm from whoever had them last.

_"Keep those on you,"_ Voss says. _"Anyone asks, you are auxiliary intake, registered this morning. You report to the garrison house in the Bulwark district before the evening bell. Do not be late for that."_

He is already looking past them at the next group in the queue.

#### Secrets and Clues
- The garrison house is a wide, low building near the eastern wall, functional and cheerless. A duty sergeant takes their names at the door, properly this time, written in a real ledger with real ink, and assigns them a shared billet in the upper floor
    - The billet is four walls and straw mattresses but it is dry.
    - There are three other auxiliaries already billeted there. They are quiet in the way people are quiet when they are listening
- The evening briefing is run by a junior officer named **Calla Mourne** a young, precise, visibly tired woman. She outlines the week's patrol rotations without enthusiasm and then pauses at the end
    - _"There is one additional matter. Lower district, south side. Third night running we have had a patrol come back short a man. No bodies recovered. Command has assessed it as desertion."_
    - She says the word _desertion_ with the flat tone of someone repeating an assessment she does not believe
    - *Next we have a few new recruits. Introduce yourselves... please. Tomorrow you start training and assignments.*
- The militia token grants movement through most of the city.
- [[Rewyn Fosley]] appears once more before the end of the first day. He finds whoever in the party seemed most capable and speaks to them quietly
	- *Just wanted to wish you all luck... you will need it.*


### The Vaeloraine Path 
The senior officer does not give his name. He walks them through the gate with the brisk efficiency of a man managing something delicate and says very little until they are clear of the gatehouse arch and into the square beyond. Then he stops and turns.

_"There is an inn near the vinter district road called the The Press. Rooms have been arranged. Stay there, stay quiet, and do not announce yourself further until Lord Reindell sends word. He will send word."_

_"Your house was respected here ma'am. It still is."_

Then he is gone back through the arch before she can answer.

#### Secrets and Clues
- The Press is a respectable inn in the Vinter district — clean, quiet, the kind of establishment that does not ask questions because its clientele pays well enough to make questions impolite
    - The rooms arranged are on the upper floor, away from the common room. Whoever made this arrangement did so before the party arrived, which means word travels faster inside Valehall than it should
    - The innkeeper, a broad woman named **Sess**, hands over the keys without comment. She has been given instructions and she is following them. She will not say by whom
- Lord Reindell's word arrives sooner than expected — before the party has fully settled. A young page in Hathor livery knocks on the door and delivers a folded note sealed in grey wax
    - The note is brief. An address in the upper district, a time two hours hence, and a single line at the bottom: _Come without the Runekeeper._
    - Whether they comply with that last instruction is their choice
- The address is not the Hathor seat. It is a private residence — well-maintained, unremarkable from the outside, the kind of building that belongs to someone who has money and would prefer you did not think about where it came from
    - Lord Reindell receives them in a small study. He is older than expected, unhurried, with the stillness of a man who has been having difficult conversations for a very long time
    - He offers wine before he says anything of substance. The wine is from Ashveil. He watches their faces when he pours it
- What he wants is information. What happened in Ashveil, in their own words, from people who were there. He already knows the broad shape of it — he has sources — but he wants the detail and he wants to understand who these people are while he gets it
    - He will not make promises in this meeting. He will ask questions and listen and at the end he will say only: _"House Hathor has not forgotten what House Vaeloraine built. Nor what was done to it."_
    - What that means in practical terms he leaves unspoken. For now
