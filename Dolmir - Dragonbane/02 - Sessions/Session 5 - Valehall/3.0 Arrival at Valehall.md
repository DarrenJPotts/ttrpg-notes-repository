[[Valehall]] approaches. Tall walls of white stone tower against the high mountains of the Vale. The three tiered city of stone gives reason to why it is nicknamed the Towering Stone of Umbar.  It's tall walls are only outshone by the mountain itself and in its centre, the palace of House Hathor sits high against the mountain wall.

The stone road ahead is worn deep by generations of cart wheels and the weight of the trade. This city is unlike any other, and for some it is the first time seeing something of this grand magnitude.

The Towering Stone lies ahead.

---

### Scene: The Gates of Valehall
The closer you get the more you see. Figures on the walls. Torchlight behind arrow slits. A queue of both people are carts. And as the treeline opens you are greeted with a large camp to the east, man and Eldar kin shuffle between tents busy with whatever tasks present. 
Ahead of you lies the large walls of the Valehall, towering over you as you close in the distance. A guard in Hathor's colours talks to a man covered in plate as you come up from behind. 

#### The Smiling Blade
- A quite conversation happens between the guard before being turned around and the Smiling Blade walks up to the groups path
- Stops ahead of them, blocking carts and stares quietly for a while 
- There is an immense pressure that feels as if its choking your lungs
- Before courteously bowing and leaving on his way with a smile

#### The Gate Guard
- Asks what the official business of the group is 
- The city is closed due to capacity, currently cannot take more refugees but there is the encampment if they need a place to stay while under their protection
- If you use #Vaeloraine name, he will dismiss them at first with the *Yeah and I am the Lord of this here city*

### Scene: Getting into the city
There are 3 options they group knows of: 
- [[Rewyn Fosley]] the militia recruitment 
- [[Altes Lesym]] as part of Ordo Magi business
- #Vaeloraine name

#### Path One — The Ordo Magi

_Altes Lesym steps forward and offers a solution but it might put more crosshairs on their back. He is presently at task but one that is hush. He can get them in but word travels quick, and association with the Ordo Magi can be bad._

**Secrets and Clues**
- Altes presents a small iron seal to the gate guard — the mark of the Ordo Magi. The guard examines it longer than necessary, then steps aside without a word
    - The Ordo Magi have standing access to Valehall by longstanding arrangement with House Hathor. The guard knows this even if he does not like it
- The party enters as Altes's associates. No names are taken beyond his own. This is deliberate on Altes's part
    - _"Names in ledgers have a way of travelling faster than the people they belong to."_
- Inside the gate a second guard pulls Altes aside briefly and speaks to him in a low voice. Altes listens, nods once, and rejoins the party
    - He will not say immediately what was said. Later, if pressed: someone inside the city has been asking whether a Runekeeper was expected. He does not know who
- **Cost:** The party is now associated with the Ordo Magi in the eyes of anyone watching the gate. Order sympathisers inside the city will note it. It also means Altes has a claim on their time until his business is concluded
    - What that business is remains his to reveal when he chooses

**GM Note:** This is the lowest friction entry but the highest long-term entanglement. Altes is not a threat to the party but he has his own agenda and it will eventually intersect with theirs in ways he has not yet disclosed.

#### Path Two — The Vaeloraine Name

_Elenessa steps forward. What happens next depends entirely on how she carries herself._

**Secrets and Clues**
- Elenessa can present the insignia of #Vaeloraine. He excuses himself and disappears inside the gatehouse for several minutes
    - The party stands at the front of the queue. People behind them begin to notice
- He returns with a senior officer — older, grey at the temples, a Hathor badge on his chest. The officer looks at Elenessa carefully, as though checking her face against something he was told
    - _"House Vaeloraine."_ He says it quietly. Not a question. He steps aside and gestures them through without further examination
    - [[Valehall]] does not know what occurred yet but know of some arrangement.
- Inside the gate the officer leans close and speaks in a low voice
    - _"Lord Reindell will want to know you are here. I would suggest you find somewhere to stay before that word reaches people you would rather it didn't."_
- **Cost:** Elenessa is now visible. The name that opens the gate is the same name that the Order has reason to remember. Whatever protection House Hathor offers inside the walls does not extend beyond them, and the Smiling Blade is still camped outside
- [[Altes Lesym]] will go his separate ways for now

#### Path Three — Military Recruitment

_[[Rewyn Fosley]] walks through the encampment recruiting for the militia._

**Secrets and Clues**
- [[Rewyn Fosley]], thin and gentle. 
    - _"You look capable. City's short on capable right now. There's a posting available if you want inside today and coin at the end of the week."_
- The terms are deliberately vague. Duration of service is open-ended. Assignment is at the discretion of the militia command. He glosses over both of these things if asked
    - A party member who pushes back on the terms gets a slightly more honest answer: patrols have thinned out because of the state of the Heartlands. 
- They are processed through a side gate, faster than the main queue, and handed a temporary militia token each — a small clay disc with a stamped H
    - The token grants movement within the city but marks them as militia auxiliaries. Other factions will read that accordingly
- Within the first hour inside the city someone from militia command will find them with their first assigned task. It will seem minor. It will not be
- **Cost:** Someone owns their time and they have not yet met that someone. The open-ended terms mean the obligation can be called in at any moment, at the worst possible time. The militia token also makes them visible in a way the other paths do not — they are on record, by name, in a city where the Order has sympathisers
