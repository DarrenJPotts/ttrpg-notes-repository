# Knights of the Vale Tournament — Day 1: The Jousts

**Date:** Earth Day (Tournament opens)  
**Location:** The Vale's tournament grounds (three stands: Lord's Stand, Guild Stand, Common Stand)  
**Atmosphere:** Pageantry masking tension. Banners clash with each other. The crowd feels it.

---

## The Stands & Viewing

**Lord's Stand** (Best view, faction signals)
- [[Reindell Hathor]] presides (neutral, wary)
- [[Vaelor II]]'s ambassador + knights camp on the left
- [[Halthor III]]'s ambassador + knights camp on the right
- The Faith representatives (Cardinals, Inquisitors) sit front and center
- [[Lady Ariel Enel]] and her court occupy a private box

**Guild Stand** (Merchants, crafters, intelligencers)
- [[Altes Lesym]] sits here, watching everyone
- Ordo Magi affiliates scattered throughout
- Spies from both factions

**Common Stand** (Townsfolk, refugees, servants)
- Where the party likely sits if not with a patron
- Cheapest, most chaotic, best eavesdropping

---

## The Joust Bracket: Three Rounds

**Structure:** Single elimination, best of three lances to unhorse or force yield. Whoever wins the most rounds advances to the **Final Event** (a 7-man melee).

### **ROUND 1: Opening Matches**

#### **Match 1: [[Sir Halvard Snow]] (Neutral) vs. Sir Gregor Vorn (Vaelor's Flag)**
- **Halvard's colors:** Black and silver (House Snow — independent)
- **Gregor's colors:** Gold and white (Faith / Vaelor's faction)
- **Outcome:** Halvard wins 2-1 (spectacular joust, Halvard's superior horsemanship on display)
- **What the crowd sees:** Vaelor's champion is *good* but not unbeatable. Murmurs ripple.
- **What [[Altes Lesym]] sees:** Halvard is still neutrality incarnate. But he chose to wear his house colors openly. Not hiding.

---

#### **Match 2: Sir Thadden Kale (Halthor's Banner) vs. Sir Eirik Stoneheart (Great House — House Mirenhall, neutral leaning Halthor)**
- **Thadden's colors:** Purple and black (House Kale, Halthor's sworn)
- **Eirik's colors:** Silver and green (House Mirenhall)
- **Outcome:** Thadden wins 2-0 (brutal, efficient, shows training)
- **What the crowd sees:** Halthor's knights *train*. This one is dangerous.
- **Opportunity for party:** Eirik is thrown from his horse hard. The crowd goes silent. A healer is called. He'll live but is out of the tournament.

---

#### **Match 3: Sir Bowen (Vaelor-aligned mercenary) vs. [[The Smiling Blade]] (Inquisitor, Old Order)**
- **Bowen's colors:** Gold and red (mercenary, working for Faith gold)
- **The Smiling Blade's colors:** Black and crimson (Old Order — no house banner, just faith iconography)
- **Outcome:** Smiling Blade wins 2-1 (methodical, ritualistic, unnerving to watch)
- **What the crowd sees:** The Inquisitor fights like he's *judging* the field, not just competing. The Faith has its own power here.
- **What [[Reindell Hathor]] sees:** The Old Order is flexing. This is a *statement*.

---

### **ROUND 2: The Factional Clash**

#### **Match 4: Sir Aldrick Thorne (Great House — House Thorne, neutral) vs. Sir Petyr of Millbrook (Halthor's squire-knight)**
- **Aldrick's colors:** Bronze and brown (House Thorne — old neutral family)
- **Petyr's colors:** Purple and black
- **Outcome:** Aldrick wins 2-0 (a house knight proving his worth, earning favor)
- **The crowd sees:** A neutral house *beats* a Halthor knight. The room shifts. Vaelor's faction cheers.
- **Opportunity for party:** A servant from House Thorne approaches the party afterward, looking for information on who Altes backs. They're trying to position themselves.

---

#### **Match 5: Sir Caeran the Black (Vaelor's heavy cavalry) vs. [[The Smiling Blade]] (rematch from earlier rounds)**
- **Caeran's colors:** Gold, white, and black (Vaelor's elite war knight)
- **Smiling Blade:** (same as before)
- **Outcome:** Smiling Blade wins 2-1 (a grueling match; Caeran is unhorsed, stays down)
- **What the crowd sees:** The Inquisitor just beat Vaelor's elite. The Faith is *not* aligned with either king — they're independent power.
- **What bothers [[Altes Lesym]]:** The Smiling Blade is proving something. This joust isn't just about the kings.

---

#### **Match 6: Sir Thadden Kale (Halthor's flag from earlier) vs. Sir Halvard Snow**
- **Thadden:** Purple and black
- **Halvard:** Black and silver
- **Outcome:** **Halvard wins 2-1** (the crowd rises; this is the match everyone wanted to see)
- **What the crowd sees:** The neutral knight is *the strongest*. Halvard Snow could side with either king and tip the scales.
- **Crucial detail:** As Halvard raises his lance in victory, he makes deliberate eye contact with [[Reindell Hathor]] and nods. *A signal.*

---

### **FINAL EVENT: The Seven-Knight Melee**

**Rules:** Last knight standing wins. Swords, not lances. No killing, but injuries are expected.

**Competitors who advance:**
1. [[Sir Halvard Snow]]
2. [[The Smiling Blade]]
3. Sir Aldrick Thorne (House Thorne)
4. Sir Thadden Kale (Halthor's knight)
5. Sir Gregor Vorn (Vaelor's knight)
6. Sir Belinor (wild card — entered late, unknown allegiance)
7. One more to be determined (mercenary or house knight your GM needs)

---

#### **The Melee Unfolds**

**Phase 1 (Early chaos):**
- Aldrick and Thadden square off immediately. House pride.
- Belinor hangs back, watching.
- Gregor and Thadden *gang up* on Halvard (Vaelor's faction trying to remove the neutral threat).

**Phase 2 (The Moment):**
- Halvard is surrounded. Bleeding. The crowd gasps.
- **[[The Smiling Blade]] breaks formation and shields Halvard**, fighting back Thadden and Gregor.
- The Inquisitor is *protecting* the neutral knight.
- The crowd is confused. So are the ambassadors.

**Phase 3 (The Tragedy):**
- As Halvard gets his footing back, one of Vaelor's supporters in the crowd throws something (a dagger? poisoned blade? GM's choice).
- Halvard is struck. 
- **[[The Smiling Blade]] moves to catch him as he falls.**
- Chaos erupts. Is it assassination? Foul play? The refs freeze, unsure.
- **Halvard dies in The Smiling Blade's arms.**

**The Smiling Blade stands, looking directly at [[Reindell Hathor]], then at the ambassadors.**  
*"Justice in the Vale. Always."*

## Party Interaction Opportunities

### **Before the Final Melee:**
- A faction representative approaches them: "Who do you serve?" (Vaelor, Halthor, Faith, Eldar, neutral?)
- A noble they've helped asks them to place a bet on Halvard.
- [[Pip Giles]] is in the crowd somewhere, watching. If the party finds him, he's acting *very strange* (the Shelob curse progressing).

### **During the Melee:**
- If the party tries to warn Halvard or intervene, the refs eject them or worse.
- If they're particularly astute, they might spot the weapon before it's thrown.
- They might see **[[Lyra Lovecraft]] moving through the crowd** with purpose.

### **After Halvard Falls:**
- Chaos. Guards move in.
- **[[Lyra Lovecraft]] appears and creates a distraction** (exactly what, the GM decides, but it prevents Lia's execution if she's blamed or accused).
- [[Reindell Hathor]] is visibly shaken. He calls for order.
- The ambassadors from both kings exchange looks. Neither ordered this.
- Altes is *gone* — disappeared into the crowd. Where'd he go?

## GM Notes: Running This

**Keep it Lazy:**
- Don't narrate every lance strike. Describe the highlights.
- Let the crowd's reaction tell you what matters.
- If a player wants to investigate something, let them. Fail-forward into new complications.

**The Stakes:**
- This isn't just spectacle. Halvard's death removes the neutral knight everyone was betting on.
- It signals that the Faith (via The Smiling Blade) is making its own play.
- It destabilizes [[Reindell Hathor]]'s authority and draws the kings' full attention.

**After This:**
- The tournament *continues* (the Faith demands order be maintained).
- But the political calculation has shifted. Both kings now know someone is playing the board independent of them.
- Altes will be working on damage control / opportunity assessment.
- [[Lyra Lovecraft]] reveals herself to Lia that night (per your prep notes).
