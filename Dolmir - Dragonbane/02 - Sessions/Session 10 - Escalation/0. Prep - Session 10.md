##### The demand
**His voice carries across the fields, amplified. It echoes off the walls.**
***"I have been... most patient. Waiting outside these walls while your Lord plays at neutrality. But the Order does not forget those who shelter heresy."***

He steps closer, unhurried. His eyes scan the walls before meeting your direction...

***"We have but two humble demands... First we wish only to take the girl."***

He pauses for a while...

**"The one who assassinated our representative and the ambassador of Prince Vaelor, second of his name."**

He takes a step forward. His men don't move.

***"Second, a man who has taken something from us... a merchant I hear, Aldous Jefferson. I believe he owns a store within your walls.***

***"These are our demands we lay forth for a deal. The betrayer stands amongst your ranks. I know you stand there in your pitiful guise... You know my word to be bound and true. Accept this with honour. Give me the girl."***

Silence. Just wind and distant screams from the refugees.

***Well... do we have an accord?***

> You have a chance to talk amongst yourselves, you see Atles and Ariel have appeared close to your group


##### If refused
He doesn't move. Doesn't raise his voice. Instead, figures are pulled forward from his ranks — bound, gagged, terrified. You recognize them.

Members of Ashveil society. Perhaps people the party encountered. Perhaps people they know.

**"Our dear Lady Sige was defiled and left to rot beyond the Vale walls."**

His smile never wavers.

**"Your last chance now includes those responsible for her death. I will say it once more — you know my word is binding, betrayer."**

He gestures. The prisoners are held visibly, uncomfortably. One of the Witch Hunters draws a blade slowly, lets it catch the light.

The threat is unmistakable.

##### If They Still Refuse
**"Then you have refused and called for greater action as duty demands."**

He steps closer to the gates. His voice drops, but it reaches everyone on the walls.

**"We are in your city, Lord Hathor. We are in your walls, your homes. Your friends could very well be on our side too."**

He points, not at the gates, but at the city behind them.

**"It will be your head, Lord Hathor. Not in glorious battle. In the dark. In your own hall."**

##### The Hold
The Black Templars don't advance. The Witch Hunters don't draw weapons. They simply... wait.

Because there's still an ambassador from one of the princes _inside_ Valehall.

If the Blade attacks now, it becomes an act of war. It shatters the Faith's claim to neutrality. The other prince's ambassador will flee and report. Every neutral city in the realm will rise against the Faith for violating sanctuary.
The Blade knows this.
So does Reindell.
So does everyone on those walls.
**He can threaten. He can demand. He can terrorize.**
**But he cannot strike. Not yet.**
