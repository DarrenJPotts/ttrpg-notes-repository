## Scene: Garrick & The Smithies — Tournament Information

**Context:**
The Knights of the Vale are hosting a **recruitment tournament in 2-3 days**. It's an open call—any fighter in Valehall can enter to showcase their abilities. The tournament is partly spectacle (drawing crowds), partly recruitment (finding new sword-arms), and partly a show of strength from [[Reindell Hathor]].

Garrick learns this by visiting local smithies and talking to the owners, who have connections to the fighters and mercenary community.

---

## The Smithies

### **Option 1: Steelwife's Armoury**
**Owner:** Magda Steelwife (gruff, no-nonsense, former fighter herself)

**Location:** Lower Quarter, near the market

**What She Knows:**
- The tournament is 2 days out at the Knights' grounds (outside the city walls)
- **Open call:** Anyone can enter, no buy-in fee, but you have to make it past initial rounds
- Prize pool is decent (coin for winners, but more valuable is **recognition from Reindell Hathor**)
- She's skeptical of Garrick at first (mallard-kin are rare, unfamiliar) but respects anyone serious about proving themselves
- *"The Vale needs bodies. Doesn't care where they come from, long as they're good with a blade"*
- She mentions other competitors she's heard about (local toughs, mercenaries from outlying settlements, one or two trained soldiers)

**Her Take on the Politics:**
- Hathor's using the tournament to flex power during the succession crisis (two kings claim the throne)
- The ambassadors (Templewall and Umbar) will likely attend—it's a neutral show of strength
- *"Politics, but with swords. Watch yourself."*

**Hook for Garrick:**
- If Garrick seems serious, Magda offers to let him train at her space for a day or two before the tournament (*"Need to loosen up, get a feel for real fighters"*)
- She doesn't give pity or easy sparring—she hits hard and expects him to hit back

### **Option 2: The Silver Steel Smithy**
**Owner:** Cilius Val (enigmatic, well-connected, runs underground betting/favors)

**Location:** Merchant Quarter, more upscale

**What He Knows:**
- Same basic facts about the tournament
- More insider info: There's already a **favorite** (a sellsword named Kael, known for dirty tactics)
- Some fighters are being **quietly backed** by noble houses or factions (betting and reputation stakes)
- He's heard the **Brawlers Den** is considering sending someone (the underground fighting ring has its own reputation to defend)
- The atmosphere will be tense because of the ambassadors and the succession crisis—high stakes beyond just combat

**His Take on the Politics:**
- Cilius is more cagey about deeper conspiracies
- He hints that the tournament is a *"good place to be seen"* and a *"bad place to make enemies"*
- If asked directly about assassination or Primrose Court: *"Things happen at crowded events. Be sharp."*

**Hook for Garrick:**
- If Garrick's interesting enough, Cilius offers to *"put word out"* that he's entering—gives him street credibility
- He might ask Garrick to *"keep an eye on certain fighters"* during the tournament (light intelligence gathering, nothing dangerous)
- Cilius hints at connections to larger forces but doesn't spell them out

---

## Scene Structure (Freeform)

**Garrick can visit one smithy or both.** Each visit should feel:

1. **Short (5-10 minutes of play time):** Information gathering, not extended roleplay (unless player wants it)
2. **Atmospheric:** Let the smithy itself set tone (clang of hammers, smell of hot metal, the vibe of craftspeople who know fighting)
3. **Character-revealing:** What does the smithy owner say? What's their opinion of Garrick? Does he earn respect or seem naive?
4. **Non-committal:** The smithies don't force Garrick's participation—they inform him and let him decide

**Possible Extensions:**
- **Training montage:** If Garrick wants to train with Magda before the tournament, a brief montage (1-2 rounds of sparring) shows his skill level and builds confidence
- **Betting conversation:** If Garrick asks about wagering, Cilius can explain odds and the betting economy around the tournament
- **Other fighters:** Either smithy can introduce Garrick to 1-2 other tournament contenders (rival, ally, or neutral)

## GM Notes

**How to Play:**
- Let Garrick ask questions; the smithy owners are knowledgeable and willing to talk (they want good fighters in the tournament)
- Don't force plot exposition—if Garrick doesn't ask about ambassadors or politics, the smithies won't over-explain
- Use descriptions: the heat of the smithy, the sound of work, the weathered faces of people who've fought for a living
- If Garrick trains with Magda, keep it brief (one combat exchange, narratively resolved) to show his skill and her respect

**Tone:**
- Garrick is finding his footing in Valehall. These visits are him *discovering* the city's fighting culture
- The smithy owners see him as an unknown (rare beastkin, new face) but treat him as serious if he acts serious
- The tournament is a legitimate opportunity—not a trap, not a setup—but it's clearly tied to larger political currents

**What Doesn't Happen Here:**
- No combat (unless he trains with Magda, which is narratively light)
- No revelation of deeper conspiracies (smithy owners keep those close)
- No forced choice or consequence—Garrick can attend the tournament or not; the scene is information/opportunity, not pressure

**Follow-up:**
- If Garrick enters, the tournament will be a side story (not happening this session, but next one or later)
- The smithy owners become recurring contacts (equipment, information, training, reputation)
