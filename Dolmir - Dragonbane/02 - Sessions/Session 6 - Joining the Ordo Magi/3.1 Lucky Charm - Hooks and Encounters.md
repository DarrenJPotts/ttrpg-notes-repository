## The Lucky Charm — Hooks, NPCs, Events

The **Lucky Charm** is a tavern and house of assignation in the lower quarter. It serves wine, ale, food, and companionship. The clientele is mixed: merchants, refugees, labourers, desperate nobility, spies, and lonely wanderers.

**Atmosphere:**
- Warm lamplight, heavy with wine and perfume
- Sound of laughter, low music, clinking glasses
- A sense of refuge—people come here to escape the dying city outside
- Staff are watchful, professional, and more dangerous than they appear
- Prices are fair but not cheap; no one takes advantage here

---

## NPCs & Staff

### **Kess** (The Fighter)
**Role:** Worker at the Lucky Charm; trained fighter

**What She Does:**
- Works as a companion/dancer
- Defends patrons and staff from trouble (non-lethally, usually)
- Knows everyone in the lower quarter
- Trains fighters on the side (connection to Brawlers Den)

**Hooks:**
- **For Elenessa:** If the mugging happens, Kess intervenes, teaches jiu-jitsu, offers mentorship to the Brawlers Den
- **For others:** If they're friendly, Kess is a source of street information and lower-quarter connections
- **Potential quest:** Kess might ask the party to help with a problem (protecting someone, retrieving something, dealing with a troublemaker)

**Personality:** Gruff, direct, observant. Doesn't judge. Respects competence and sincerity.

---

### **Marta** (Owner/Manager)
**Role:** Runs the Lucky Charm with an iron will

**What She Does:**
- Owns the place, hired most of the staff
- Keeps the peace through reputation and swift consequences
- Protective of her people (staff are valued, not disposable)
- Has connections to various factions (neutral ground)

**Hooks:**
- **Information:** Marta hears everything. Party can buy information from her (price varies)
  - *"You want to know about the ambassadors? 50 coin. About Jefferson? 75. About who's hiring?"*
- **Refuge:** If party is in trouble, Marta might offer them a safe place (for a price or favor)
- **Warning:** She warns them about danger if she knows it's coming (sympathiser raids, police crackdowns)

**Personality:** Business-like, sharp-eyed, motherly to her staff but ruthless to troublemakers.

---

### **Islas** (A Refugee)
**Role:** New arrival from the Heartlands; desperate

**What She Does:**
- Arrived in Valehall 2 weeks ago, fleeing the Blight
- Works washing dishes, cleaning, running errands
- Desperate for work, scared, observant

**Hooks:**
- **Information (Desperate):** Will talk about what she saw fleeing the Blight
  - *"The crops are dead. The soil is black. People are getting sick, just... sick. No cure. They rot from the inside."*
- **Quest Hook:** Her family is still out there (trapped between Valehall and the Heartlands). She needs help, has little to pay
- **Emotional Beat:** She's a reminder of the dying world—not abstract, but real

**Personality:** Young, frightened, but tough underneath. Grateful for kindness, suspicious of cruelty.

---

### **Thorne** (A Spymaster's Agent)
**Role:** Patron, ostensibly a wealthy merchant

**What He Does:**
- Sits in a corner booth most evenings
- Watches people, takes notes, asks subtle questions
- Connected to one of the political factions (GM's choice: Primrose Court, Old Order, Ordo Magi, or independent)
- Is NOT immediately obvious as a spy—just seems like a wealthy loner

**Hooks:**
- **If party is interesting:** Thorne watches them, might approach with a job offer
- **Information:** If party buys him a drink, he drops hints about plots (assassination, blight, conspiracies)
- **Warning:** If party is being hunted or under suspicion, Thorne might warn them (for a price or future favor)
- **Danger:** If party becomes a threat to his faction, he reports them

**Personality:** Smooth, cultured, dangerous. Seems friendly but is always calculating.

---

### **The Drunk Noble** (Rotating NPC)
**Role:** Local nobility, inebriated and complicated

**What He/She Does:**
- Different person each time, but always a minor noble hiding from responsibilities
- Drinks, flirts, complains about the succession crisis
- Sometimes generous, sometimes cruel, depending on mood
- Easy mark for theft or manipulation, but also connected

**Hooks:**
- **Information (Drunk):** Will spill secrets about their house, the ambassadors, scandals
- **Quest:** Might hire the party for something foolish (retrieve a love letter, win a bet, etc.)
- **Danger:** Their guards might cause trouble if the party gets too familiar
- **Romance:** Might flirt with party members

**Personality:** Self-pitying, dramatic, occasionally insightful between the wine.

## Random Encounter Events

**When the party enters or settles in, roll d6 for an event:**

| d6 | Event | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **A Drunk Argument** | Two patrons (merchants? soldiers?) get loud over money or insults. Threatens to spill into violence. Marta or Kess will step in unless party intervenes. |
| **2** | **A Lover's Reunion** | Two people who haven't seen each other in years recognize each other across the tavern. Emotional moment—laughter, tears, hugging. Doesn't involve party unless they get curious. |
| **3** | **Sympathiser Recruitment** | A One God sympathiser sits near the bar, quietly pitching to locals: *"Join us. The Eldar are poison. The One God will purify the city."* Kess ejects them if they get pushy. |
| **4** | **Bad News Arrives** | A traveler or messenger brings word from outside the city: *"The Blight is spreading faster. Three villages gone in a week."* Tavern goes quiet. Islas looks terrified. |
| **5** | **A Quiet Transaction** | Thorne (or another spy) meets someone in a corner booth. Exchange of coin or papers. If party is watching, they might see it and wonder. |
| **6** | **Music & Dancing** | A musician arrives with instruments. Music starts. Staff and some patrons dance or sing. Warm, brief moment of normalcy before the reality outside sets back in. |

## Information for Sale

**Marta (or Kess) can sell information. Price varies by sensitivity:**

| Information | Price | Who Knows |
|---|---|---|
| **General City Gossip** | 5-10 coin | What's the mood? What are people talking about? |
| **Faction Activity** | 15-25 coin | Where are the sympathisers meeting? Are the guards on edge? |
| **NPC Locations** | 20-30 coin | Where was [NPC] last seen? Who do they meet? |
| **Dangerous Knowledge** | 50+ coin | Who's hiring assassins? Which noble is betraying which? Is the ambassador really here to negotiate? |
| **Personal Secrets** | Variable | Blackmail, affairs, debts, crimes. High price. High danger. |

**Key Information Available:**
- Lyra was spotted in the upper quarter (if party asks) — 25 coin
- Jefferson went toward the Underlight — 20 coin
- Sympathisers are planning something (vague) — 30 coin
- The tournament is a power play by Hathor — 15 coin
- Brawlers Den is looking for good fighters — 10 coin

## Possible Quests/Jobs

**Marta or Kess might offer work:**

### **Job 1: Protect a Patron** (Escort/Bodyguard)
- A refugee or minor merchant needs escorting across the lower quarter to somewhere else
- Risk: Sympathisers, gangs, or bandits
- Payment: 20-40 coin, depending on danger
- Complication: The person might be hiding something

### **Job 2: Find Someone** (Investigation)
- Marta had a regular customer who hasn't shown up in days
- Worried he's in trouble or dead
- Party tracks him down (in Underlanes? With a gang? Dead?)
- Payment: 30-50 coin + whatever the customer rewards for rescue

### **Job 3: Break Up a Conflict** (Politics)
- Two factions (sympathisers vs Eldar supporters? Rival merchants?) are using the Lucky Charm as neutral ground for a meeting
- Meeting is getting heated; Marta wants them separated before violence
- Party must diplomatically or forcibly split them up
- Payment: 25 coin + favors from whichever faction they help

### **Job 4: Retrieve Stolen Goods** (Theft/Recovery)
- Something valuable was taken from the Lucky Charm
- Party tracks the thief (minor criminal, street kid, desperate person)
- Recovery means either buying it back, stealing it back, or dealing with the thief
- Payment: 40-60 coin

## Atmosphere & Details to Enhance

**Visual:**
- Worn wooden tables, mismatched chairs
- Oil lamps casting amber light
- A bar with bottles of wine, ale, spirits (prices on a board)
- A small stage in the corner (for musicians)
- Curtained booths for privacy
- Herb bundles hanging from the ceiling (perfume, protection?)

**Sounds:**
- Low murmur of conversation
- Occasional laughter or argument
- Clink of glasses
- Music (if musician is present)
- The sound of the city outside (distant, muffled)

**Smells:**
- Wine, ale, perfume
- Tobacco smoke
- Food (bread, stew, meat)
- Incense (trying to mask the smell of sickness from outside)

**Social Rules (GM guide):**
- Marta keeps the peace; violence gets you kicked out or worse
- No weapons drawn indoors (unless necessary self-defense)
- Staff are off-limits (respect them or leave)
- Thievery is tolerated (part of the lower quarter) but violence over it gets you ejected
- Sympathisers are unwelcome but tolerated (free speech, sort of)
- Eldar are welcome (Marta doesn't discriminate)

## Connecting to Larger Plot

**Information the Lucky Charm reveals:**
- **Lyra sighting:** Kess or Islas might mention seeing an Eldar woman handing out parcels (if party asks about suspicious activity)
- **Blight spreading:** News arrives of more sickness, more refugees, more death
- **Political tension:** Drunk nobles gossip about the succession, the ambassadors, the tournament
- **Sympathiser activity:** Constant low-level recruitment and agitation
- **Jefferson:** Information on where he went (Underlight, catacombs)
- **Brawlers Den:** Connection to Kess, lead to underground fighting

**The Lucky Charm as a Hub:**
- Party may return here multiple times during the session
- Information updates as the world changes (by evening, more refugees, more sickness reported)
- Can become a safe place if party builds relationships with Marta/Kess
- Enemies might also use it (spies watching, sympathisers recruiting, assassins scouting)

## GM Quick Reference

**Key NPCs:**
- **Kess:** Fighter, Brawlers Den contact, mentor to Elenessa
- **Marta:** Owner, information broker, neutral arbiter
- **Islas:** Refugee, emotional anchor to the Blight, potential quest hook
- **Thorne:** Spy, mysterious, potential danger or ally
- **Drunk Noble:** Gossip, quests, information

**Why Party Might Be Here:**
- Elenessa's mugging (triggers here)
- Information gathering (Marta sells it)
- Social encounters (downtime, relationships)
- Quests (Marta offers work)
- Faction connection (Spies, sympathisers, others use it)
- Refugees/NPCs need help (Islas, others)

**What to Emphasize:**
- It's a refuge from the dying world outside
- But the dying world intrudes (news, refugees, sickness)
- Danger and opportunity coexist
- People here are complex (not all good or bad)
