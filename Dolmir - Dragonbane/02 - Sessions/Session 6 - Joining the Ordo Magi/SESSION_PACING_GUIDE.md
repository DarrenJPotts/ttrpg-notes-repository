## Session 6 Pacing Guide — Freeform Framework

**Philosophy:** This is NOT a rigid timeline. Use these phases as **loose guideposts** based on party action, not as a schedule to force. Follow the party's lead, check the clock between scenes.

---

## Phases of the Session (In-Game & Out-of-Game)

### **Phase 1: Morning (Safehouse)**
**Duration:** 30-45 minutes (out-of-game)

**What's Happening:**
- Party wakes at the Ordo Magi safehouse
- **Anima's memory sequence** (strong start)
- Runekeeper Lesym briefs everyone on the hex situation
- Party discusses what to do / where to go next
- **This phase sets tone:** Personal, introspective, establishing the dying world's weight

**Triggers to End Phase:**
- Party decides to leave the safehouse
- Everyone's taken care of their immediate concerns (Anima's processing, group questions answered)

**Scenes Possible Here:**
- Anima's hex aftermath
- Clawbert approaches Lesym about the book (they discuss magic)
- Party equips/prepares

**Don't Rush:** Let Anima's moment breathe. This opening shouldn't feel hurried.

---

### **Phase 2: City Exploration (Mid-Morning to Afternoon)**
**Duration:** 60-90 minutes (out-of-game)

**What's Happening:**
Party disperses into Valehall to pursue their individual hooks. Multiple scenes run in parallel depending on who goes where.

**Possible Threads (Party Chooses Order):**

**A) Garrick's Smithy Visits**
- Garrick goes to Steelwife's Armoury or Silver Steel to learn about the tournament
- **Duration:** 10-15 minutes per smithy
- **Tone:** Exploration, information gathering, potential training

**B) Elenessa at Lucky Charm**
- Elenessa heads to the tavern/house of ladies
- Mugging happens, Kess intervention, hook planted
- **Duration:** 20-30 minutes
- **Tone:** Action-lite, character introduction, mentor hook

**C) Clawbert's Magic Initiation**
- Clawbert works with Runekeeper Lesym on accessing magic through the book
- **Duration:** 20-30 minutes
- **Tone:** Personal, horror-tinged, cost of magic revealed

**D) Lia/Lily Exploration**
- Lia and Lily walk the city, maybe gather information, social encounters
- Could encounter sympathiser protests (ambient flavor)
- Could stumble into the plaza where Lyra is sighted (triggers Phase 3 early)
- **Duration:** Variable (10-30 minutes)
- **Tone:** Exploration, building toward discovery

**E) Anima's Recovery**
- If Anima wants downtime, she rests and processes
- Could explore the lower quarter, talk to Lesym, ask questions about her past
- **Duration:** 10-20 minutes
- **Tone:** Quiet, contemplative

**How to Run This Phase:**
1. Ask party: *"What do you do?"*
2. Let them split or stay together—follow their choice
3. If they split: Alternate between groups quickly (10-15 min on Elenessa, then switch to Garrick, etc.)
4. Use **ambient flavor moments** between scenes (market bustle, guard patrols, refugees, weather changes)
5. Check on **ticking situations** (Pip getting anxious? Sympathisers growing restless?)

**Transition Out:**
- A scene naturally concludes (Elenessa leaves Lucky Charm, Garrick finishes at smithy)
- OR party heads toward a location where multiple hooks converge
- OR it's late afternoon, urgency builds

---

### **Phase 3: Lyra Sighting & Chase (Afternoon)**
**Duration:** 45-90 minutes (out-of-game, depending on how party engages)

**What's Happening:**
- Lyra spotted in the plaza/market distributing parcels
- Lia recognizes her mother
- Chase begins (3 skill checks)
- Sympathiser ambush initiates
- **Combat: Lyra's Ambush** (Protestor Group vs Party)
- Lyra escapes via rooftop
- Party is left bloodied, emotional (especially Lia)

**Scene Breakdown:**
- **Sighting & Recognition:** 5 min (emotional beat)
- **Chase (3 skill checks):** 10-15 min
- **Ambush setup:** 5 min
- **Combat:** 20-40 min (depends on party effectiveness and casualties)
- **Rooftop escape & aftermath:** 10 min

**Tone:** Urgent, emotional, chaotic street fight

**When to Trigger:**
- When Lia/Lily are in the plaza (party exploration naturally leads here)
- OR around 60-75 minutes into the session (pacing check)
- Do NOT force it; it should feel like bad timing/chance encounter

**Combat Notes:**
- Use your Foundry map for terrain
- Initiative 20 Events table (d12) at end of round 2 & 4
- Track innocent bystanders
- Remember: Lyra's hidden intervention if Lia/Lily reach critical Condition

---

### **Phase 4: Evening & Fallout (Final 20-30 minutes)**
**Duration:** 20-30 minutes (out-of-game)

**What's Happening:**
- Party returns to safehouse (or elsewhere)
- Wounds tended, emotions processed
- **Pip approaches** (if not yet done) with the box containing two fingers + Primrose
- Lesym checks on party, mentions the charm will be ready in a few days
- Time passes—evening in Valehall
- **Ticking Situations update:** GM narrates what's happening in the city (spreads of blight? Guard patrols? Sympathiser movements?)

**Possible Scenes:**
- Party tends wounds, gathers intel on what they learned
- Anima discusses her memory with Lia
- Lily's form concern surfaces (if not already)
- Pip's delivery (box with fingers)
- Lesym provides guidance on what's next
- **Ambient:** Church bells toll (death count for the day), refugees spotted, frost in the evening air

**Tone:** Reflective, ominous (world continues to deteriorate)

**Session Wrap:**
- End on a quiet note or a mystery (Pip's box, Lesym's warning, sighting of the spreading sickness)
- Don't resolve everything—leave threads for next session

## Pacing Checklist

**Use these to sense-check where you are:**

- [ ] **15 min in:** Anima's memory complete, safehouse discussion ongoing
- [ ] **30 min in:** Party leaving safehouse OR beginning exploration
- [ ] **45-60 min in:** Individual scenes (Garrick smithy, Elenessa Lucky Charm, Clawbert magic) underway
- [ ] **75-90 min in:** At least one major scene concluded; Lyra sighting possible (don't force)
- [ ] **90-120 min in:** Lyra combat finished OR still underway (depends on party approach)
- [ ] **120+ min in:** Fallout, Pip moment, Lesym updates, end-of-session narrative

**If You're Running Long:**
- Shorten combat (enemies break morale faster, fewer reinforcements)
- Skip one ambient moment (keep 1-2, not all)
- Hurry the Lyra chase (reduce skill checks to 2 instead of 3)
- Don't do the full Initiative 20 event cascade—pick 1 key event instead

**If You're Running Short:**
- Skip Garrick's smithy visits (handle off-screen)
- Combine scenes (Elenessa mugging happens en route to somewhere else)
- Extend one scene (let a conversation breathe, add NPC depth)
- Lyra combat is the heaviest scene; it'll take what time you have

---

## Ticking Situations to Narrate Between Scenes

**Use these to fill transitions and remind party the world is moving:**

**Early Session (Morning):**
- Church bells start tolling (death count from yesterday)
- Frost visible on street despite being past winter
- Refugee families huddled near the walls

**Mid Session (Afternoon):**
- One God sympathiser patrols noticeable
- Market prices have spiked (grain, medicines)
- Rumors of sickness in the lower quarter

**Late Session (Evening):**
- Blight spreading—people report illness
- Guard presence increased (tension with ambassadors)
- Sense of urgency in the city

---

## Scene Priority (If Short on Time)

**MUST DO:**
- Anima's memory (strong start)
- Lyra sighting + combat (emotional climax, mechanically prepared)

**SHOULD DO:**
- Elenessa at Lucky Charm (quick, clear hook)
- Clawbert's magic (personal moment, thematic)

**NICE TO HAVE:**
- Garrick's smithy visits (can happen next session or off-screen)
- Extended ambient moments
- Pip's box delivery (can happen early next session)

---

## Running Non-Linear

**If party does something unexpected:**
1. **Check:** Is it tied to a prepared hook? (Yes → run that scene)
2. **If not:** Improvise using NPCs/consequences from the ticking situations list
3. **Keep the pressure:** Remind them the world is moving (bells toll, sympathisers protest, sickness spreads)
4. **Return to threads:** Don't abandon prepared scenes; work them in when party shows interest

**Example:** Party wants to investigate the Underlanes instead of going to Lucky Charm first.
- Let them explore Underlanes (find hints of Jefferson, catacombs)
- While they're down there, sickness begins spreading above
- They resurface into chaos
- Lyra sighting comes later (evening instead of afternoon)

---

## Session End

**Good endpoints:**
- Lyra disappears on rooftop (emotional beat, mystery)
- Pip delivers the box (plot hook for investigation)
- Lesym warns of spreading sickness (world escalation)
- Party at rest, reflecting on what they've learned

**Don't end on:**
- Mid-combat (finish the fight)
- Mid-conversation (let NPCs have their say)
- A cliffhanger that needs immediate resolution

Let the session end with **questions, not answers.** What's in the box? Why was Lyra here? What's the sickness? Who saved us in the fight?
