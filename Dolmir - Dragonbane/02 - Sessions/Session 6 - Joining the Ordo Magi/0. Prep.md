### 2. Strong Start

The smell of iron rises from the pool of blood on the table in the centre of the room. *Drip* - It hits the floor in periodic drips like a faucet that was left slightly open but that faucet is your friend. The Eastern sun of the morning crests the walls of the Valehall, reaching you last in the lower quarter. 

Anima, before the morning light woke you and your dear friend Lia explained what had transpired. After the what felt like hours had passed and [[Altes Lesym]] had been pushed out of the void of your mind and soul... you sat there alone. And it comes to you in parts after Lia had spoken to you, your mind flickers back into that world of dark. The roots and star of your core. It comes in waves and splashes. 
The dark void, the drips of water... then you stand next to an familiar bed, gilded stone and wooden walls around you. Vines growing on pillars and in the distance a tree. Large and looming with golden leaves.

*Ani, come on... we're going to be late... again*

She tugs at your hand leading you through open walkways of stone with loggia outlooking a central garden. The air is sweet and the autumn breeze fresh. 

*What do you do Ani?*

You follow. Your small feet skip-step to match her pace as you wind through the loggia. Other Eldar pass in robes of deep green and silver. They nod to Maya, with a smile but look of 'you got to get moving', you understand that much. The Great Tree's roots run beneath these stones. You can feel them. Even then, you could feel them.

The air grows cooler as you approach the heart of the Conclave. Incense smoke curls through archways, something sweet and old with an earthly hint. The others gather as you walk. There's a formal procession beginning, robed figures moving toward the inner sanctum where the Great Tree's canopy opens to the sky. The faces are a blur, like your memory or perception of them are slightly off but they feel friendly. 

Then Maya *stops*.

Her hand tightens on yours. You feel her attention shift as her breath catches.

Across the courtyard, near one of the pillar archways, stands a young man. Human. His features blur when you try to focus, dark hair you can tell that much and what feels like a pause in time, a long distant stare. Through Maya... Through you... before the blurred face turns back.

She whispers a name. But another blur to you. Indecipherable

Maya's lips tighten. Her jaw works silently. For a moment, you think she might say something, but instead she pulls you forward. When she finally looks away, there's something hard in her expression but that quickly fades once more to her sun filled smile.

*Come*, she says quietly. *We.. are... definitely... late.*

The inner sanctum opens before you. The Great Tree rises, impossibly vast, its trunk wider than houses. Golden leaves shimmer in the dappled autumn light. Other Eldar stand in concentric circles around it, palms open, voices beginning a low harmonic chant.

Maya guides you into the circle. Your small hand still in hers. Its warm, comforting even. The roots hum beneath your feet. The chant swells.

And then—

Lia you notice the glazed over look in Ani's eyes of a person who is lost in thought. She has not responded for some time. What do you do

The smell of iron is still there. On the table. The blood from the extraction.

Lia sits beside you, concern creasing her features.

### 7. Fail-Forward Notes

_For each key beat: if they fail, what complicates instead of stops?_

**Jefferson's Disappearance**
- *If Pip isn't found by midday:* Pip acts alone, ventures into the Underlanes looking for Jefferson without backup. By evening, he's captured by sympathisers or gets trapped in catacombs. Party now rescuing Pip becomes the session goal instead of finding Jefferson.

**God Order Sympathiser Protest**
- *If party initiates violence:* Guards arrest them or sympathisers take hostages. Faith pressure on Lord [[Reindell Hathor]] increases; he's forced to choose sides publicly.
- *If party avoids/talks through protest:* Sympathisers don't disperse, they escalate, moving to the Merchant Quarter to target "heretics." An innocent is killed before dispersing or arrested

**Ambassador Assassination (Primrose Court)**
- *If assassination succeeds:* Blame falls on Umbar's supporters. Lord Hathor pressured to arrest prominent Umbarian faction members. Party must decide if they help cover it up (compromising Ordo Magi) or expose Primrose Court (making dangerous enemies).
- *If party discovers the plot but warns the wrong person:* Assassins learn party knows. They either abort (loses momentum) or accelerate (kills ambassador earlier), forcing party into reactive crisis management.

**Clawbert's Magic Access & Old God Contact**
- *If Clawbert doesn't pursue Runekeeper's offer:* He accesses magic without supervision. Roll a Mishap with an Old God — could be minor (strange dreams) or severe (possession attempt, drawing unwanted attention from Dolmir itself).
- *If Clawbert accidentally taps into something forbidden:* He hears/sees something about the Kingswood wound that Runekeeper was trying to shield him from. Knowledge becomes dangerous; now Inquisitors want to interrogate him.

**Lily's Form Slipping**
- *If Lia doesn't notice and Lily doesn't tell:* Form destabilizes at the worst possible moment — mid-combat, mid-infiltration, mid-conversation with an NPC. Party exposed, Lily traumatized, Lia feels betrayed.
- *If Lily confesses to Lia but not the party:* Creates internal tension. Lia is keeping a secret, which complicates trust. Other PCs may wonder what's going on between them.

### 9. Ambient City Flavor

_Quick atmospheric moments to drop during exploration. No mechanics, just texture._

**Lower Quarter (Safehouse, Markets, Taverns)**
- A street preacher ranting about the "infection from above" while passersby hurry past with their heads down.
- Merchants closing their stalls early; someone whispers about prices rising again—nobody can afford winter.
- A drunk bard outside the Shambles Tavern, singing old songs about kings that "won't come back no more."
- Children playing with sticks, pretending to be Faith patrols. One child plays "the sick one" and lies still while others "burn" them.
- A woman at a well collecting water, muttering prayers to both the One God and something older—fear in her eyes.

**Merchant Quarter (Shops, Streets, Ambience)**
- A shop window displaying supplies: salt, herbs, charms against sickness. The owner watches from the door, arms crossed, wary. On inspection it is a snake oil salesman selling fake 'Eldar' relics
- A child with a cloth wrapped around their arm, fever-bright eyes, being led home by an older sibling. People give them wide berth.

**Upper Quarter (Nobles, Ambassadors, Order)**
- Servants carrying supplies and messages between estates. One drops a bundle; another servant helps, glancing around nervously.
- An Old Order Inquisitor walking alone through the streets, robes pristine, eyes cold. Citizens cross to the other side.
- A young noble laughing loudly with friends about the "rustic refugees" camped outside the walls—oblivious to the tension around them.
- Candlelight in windows of grand houses; music and clinking glasses while the lower city struggles.

**Transitions & Atmosphere**
- Morning fog rolling through the streets, muffling sound. Makes the city feel smaller, more claustrophobic.
- A beggar asking for coin "for the sickness come tomorrow." Most ignore; one person gives reluctantly.
- The church bells toll irregularly—one for each death in the city this week. The count is rising.
- Refugees from outlying villages sitting by gates with what they could carry, watching the city guard turn new arrivals away.
- The smell of earth and decay drifting in from the direction of the fields—blight-tainted crops.
