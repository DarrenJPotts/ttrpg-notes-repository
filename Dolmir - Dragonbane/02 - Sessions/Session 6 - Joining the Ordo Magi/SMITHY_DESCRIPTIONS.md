## The Two Smithies of Valehall

---

## **Steelwife's Armoury**

**Location:** Lower Quarter, near the market, worn wooden storefront with a painted sign of a woman wielding a hammer

**Exterior:**
- Heavy iron door, scarred from use and age
- Windows reinforced and somewhat grimy
- Smell of hot metal and smoke drifting from around the sides
- Street outside is busy: merchants, refugees, lower-class workers
- A few people always loitering (fighters, mercenaries, people waiting for work)

**Interior:**
- **Loud.** The sound of hammering echoes constantly—smithy is attached to the shop
- Walls lined with weapons and armor in various states of completion
- Worn leather aprons hanging on hooks
- Racks of spears, swords, shields, axes—practical, well-made, not fancy
- A water trough in one corner for cooling metal
- Scorch marks on the stone floor; small dents and dings everywhere (sign of hard use)
- A counter at the front covered with tools, measurements, scraps of leather

**Atmosphere:**
- Hot. The forge is always burning
- Gritty. Smoke, coal dust, metal shavings everywhere
- **Alive.** People coming and going, commissions being discussed, metal singing under the hammer
- No pretense. This is a working smithy, not a showroom

**Magda Steelwife (Owner):**
- Early 60s, broad-shouldered, scarred hands and forearms
- Grey-streaked hair pulled back in a tight braid
- One eye has a long scar running down from eyebrow to cheekbone
- Always wears leather apron stained with soot and old blood
- Moves with the confidence of someone who's fought and survived
- Speaks in a raspy voice, direct, no time for flattery
- Has a grin that's more threat than welcome (but not unkind)

**What She Sells:**
- **Practical armor:** Leather, reinforced cloth, some chainmail
- **Working weapons:** Swords, spears, axes, clubs—functional, not decorative
- **Repairs:** Fixes broken gear, re-fits armor
- **Custom commissions:** If you have coin and time, she'll make what you need
- **No fancy work:** If you want gold inlay or jeweled hilts, go somewhere else

**The Vibe:**
- Workshops are for people who fight *to survive*, not for glory
- Magda respects skill and sincerity
- Dislikes pretense, nobility, or people who waste her time
- Will spar with you if you're serious about proving yourself
- Her workspace is *her* space—treat it with respect or get out

**Sounds:**
- CLANG-CLANG-CLANG of hammer on anvil (constant)
- Hiss of hot metal in water
- Crackling of forge fire
- Magda cursing under her breath at a difficult commission
- Leather creaking as she moves

**Smells:**
- Smoke, coal, hot metal
- Sweat and leather
- Burnt hair (from the forge)
- Iron and oil

## **The Silver Steel Smithy**

**Location:** Merchant Quarter, elegant stone storefront with polished brass fixtures and a painted sign of a sword with a star

**Exterior:**
- Large glass windows showing displays of fine weapons and armor
- Clean, well-maintained façade
- Subtle but obvious wealth: polished hinges, fine woodwork on the door
- Street outside is quieter, more upscale—merchants, minor nobility, well-dressed folk
- Guards sometimes visible nearby (Cilius Val's connections)

**Interior:**
- **Clean. Orderly. Quiet.** Only the sound of careful metalwork
- Weapons and armor displayed like art—on velvet cushions, mounted on stands
- Polished wood counters with decorative inlay
- A separate workshop in the back (hidden behind a curtain)
- Fine furniture: chairs for clients, a small table for discussing commissions
- Shelves with leather-bound ledgers, samples of materials, sketches of designs
- Soft lamplight (candles, not crude oil lamps)
- A faint smell of fine oil and leather

**Atmosphere:**
- Refined. This is a shop for people with coin
- **Mysterious.** What happens in the back room? Who does Cilius really work for?
- Quiet power. Less obvious than Steelwife's, but present
- Feels like a place where deals are made (beyond just weapon-selling)

**Cilius Val (Owner):**
- Mid-40s, lean and graceful in movement
- Fine clothes: silk shirt, good-quality vest, tailored trousers
- Dark hair streaked with silver, worn long past the shoulder
- Hands are scarred (he does smithy work) but manicured
- Eyes are sharp, intelligent, always calculating
- Smile is pleasant but reveals nothing
- Speaks in a smooth voice, cultured, but with an edge underneath
- Wears a ring on his left hand—some kind of sigil (faction marker?)

**What He Sells:**
- **Fine weapons:** Swords, daggers, spears—well-crafted, sometimes ornate
- **Elegant armor:** Leather, chainmail, plate—designed for function and appearance
- **Bespoke commissions:** If you have coin, he'll make something custom and beautiful
- **Special work:** Hints that he can do things others can't (armor that hides things? Weapons with hidden properties?)
- **Information:** Less openly, but he knows people and can connect buyers with goods (or sellers with buyers)

**The Vibe:**
- This is a shop for mercenaries with coin, minor nobles, and people with secrets
- Cilius is smooth, charming, but dangerous
- He asks questions—who are you? What's the weapon for? Who sent you?
- Respects confidence and frankness
- Dislikes neediness or desperation
- Hints at deeper connections without revealing them

**Sounds:**
- Soft hammer work from the back room (skilled, precise)
- Quiet conversation
- The clink of coins being exchanged
- Pages turning in ledgers
- Wind chimes or bells (aesthetic, not functional)

**Smells:**
- Fine oil, leather, metal
- Incense (masking the forge smell with something more pleasant)
- Subtle perfume (possibly Cilius's)
- No coal smoke—the workshop is separate and ventilated

## **Quick Comparison**

| Aspect | Steelwife's | The Silver Steel |
|---|---|---|
| **Class** | Working-class, practical | Merchant-class, refined |
| **Clientele** | Fighters, soldiers, survivors | Mercenaries with coin, nobles, spies |
| **Quality** | Functional, reliable | Beautiful, sophisticated, hints of mystery |
| **Owner Vibe** | Gruff, direct, respects skill | Smooth, calculating, hints at secrets |
| **Cost** | Reasonable, based on materials | Higher, based on craftsmanship and connections |
| **Information** | Smithy gossip, lower-quarter news | Faction intelligence, political rumors |
| **Training** | Magda will spar, teach discipline | Cilius might connect you to others, or offer jobs |
| **Danger Level** | Physical (if you disrespect her) | Political (if you get on her bad side) |
| **Atmosphere** | Hot, loud, alive | Cool, quiet, calculating |

## **Why Visit Each**

**Garrick Would Visit Steelwife's if:**
- He wants honest advice about the tournament
- He respects practical fighting and wants training/sparring
- He wants to meet other fighters
- He needs good, reliable gear at fair prices

**Garrick Would Visit The Silver Steel if:**
- He wants finer connections and information
- He's curious about the deeper game (factions, politics)
- He wants to make an impression with better gear
- He wants to be *seen* as more than a common fighter

**Both Can Provide:**
- Information about the tournament
- Intro to other fighters
- Training opportunities
- Gear/weapons
- But in very different ways
