## Combat: Lyra's Ambush — Dragonbane Mechanics

This is a **multi-stage boss fight** using Dragonbane rules:
- **Inciters** = Individual bosses (trained fighters)
- **Protestor Groups** (2-3) = Coordinated mini-bosses with separate initiative & attack tables
- **Individual Protestors** = Minions (scattered fighters, low threat alone)
- **Innocents** = Environmental complications

---

## Initiative & Turn Order

**Roll d12 + Vigilance for:**
- Each **Party Member** (individually)
- Each **Inciter** (3 individuals, roll separately) — bosses
- Each **Protestor Group** (2-3 groups, roll separately) — mini-bosses
- Individual **Protestor Minions** (optional, only if they're acting independently)
- **Innocent Bystanders** (minimal—act last if at all)

**Sample Turn Order:**
1. Clawbert (d12+2)
2. Inciter #1 — Leader (d12+3)
3. **Protestor Group A** (d12+0)
4. Lia (d12+1)
5. Elenessa (d12+2)
6. Inciter #2 (d12+2)
7. **Protestor Group B** (d12+1)
8. Garrick (d12+2)
9. Anima (d12+1)
10. Inciter #3 (d12+2)

## Inciter Stat Blocks

**Inciter — Trained Fanatic Leader**

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Condition** | 15 each |
| **Armor** | Leather (+1) |
| **Weapons** | Short sword (d8+1) or club (d6+1) |
| **Combat Skill** | d10 (trained) |
| **Tactics** | d8 (street fighting) |

**Inciter Actions (per turn):**
- **Attack:** Roll Combat d10 vs party member's Defense. On hit: weapon die + stat, minus armor
- **Rally Group:** Yell order to a Protestor Group (that group gains +2 to next action this round)
- **Grapple/Disarm:** Tactics d8 (opposed by party member's Combat)
- **Move:** Reposition tactically
- **Retreat:** If badly wounded or leader falls

**Inciter Condition:**
- Each hit reduces Condition
- At 0 Condition: down (dying/unconscious)
- **Leader falls (0 Condition):** Other Inciters roll Morale d12. Failure = flee or fight recklessly
- **2+ Inciters down:** Remaining flee immediately

## Protestor Group Mechanics

**2-3 Protestor Groups** act as independent entities. Each has its own initiative and attack table.

### **Protestor Group Stats**

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **Composition** | 4-5 protestors per group |
| **Group Condition** | 12-16 (per group) |
| **Armor** | None |
| **Weapons** | Improvised (d4 damage, unarmed d6) |
| **Combat Skill** | d8 (untrained, fight in mob) |

### **Individual Protestor Minions** (Scattered fighters)

If a Protestor Group breaks apart or individuals are separated:
- **Condition:** 4-6 per minion
- **Combat:** d6
- **Damage:** d4 (unarmed) or d4 (improvised weapon)
- Each minion acts on their own (not grouped)
- **Much weaker than groups** — easy to subdue or intimidate away

## Morale System (Protestor Groups)

**Each Protestor Group starts with 2-3 Morale independently.**

**Morale decreases when:**
- Group takes heavy damage (reduced to 50% Condition): -1 Morale
- An Inciter is hit: -1 Morale (affects all groups)
- An Inciter falls: -2 Morale (affects all groups, potentially catastrophic)

**At 0 Morale:**
- Group breaks formation
- Becomes 2-3 individual minions (d6 Combat, d4 damage)
- No longer uses attack table — acts as weak individuals
- Many flee

**Rally Action (Inciter):**
- Inciter yells at a specific group: "+1 Morale restored" (once per group per round, max)
- But Inciter loses their attack that turn

## Combat Flow (Round-by-Round)

**Round 1:**
- Party acts in initiative
- **Inciter A acts:** Attacks or yells rally
- **Group A rolls d6:** Picks their action (swarm, grab innocent, rally, scatter)
- Remaining inciters act
- **Group B rolls d6:** Picks their action (different from Group A)
- Remaining party acts
- **Group C rolls d6 (if present):** Picks their action
- **After initiative:** Check for innocent movement

**Mid-Combat (Round 3-4):**
- **Morale checks:** Has anyone fallen? Groups become demoralized
- **Group A breaking?:** At 0 Morale, they become minions
- **Reinforcements?** (GM call) 1-2 more protestors arrive
- Combat gets messier as groups splinter

**End Combat When:**
- All Inciters are at 0 Condition (down)
- All Protestor Groups at 0 Morale (scattered/fleeing)
- Party flees/surrenders
- Lyra appears on rooftop (party may disengage)

## Innocent Bystanders

**Each Innocent has their own initiative (acts last):**
- **Round 1-2:** Each moves away (Vigilance d12 vs difficulty 8 to escape cleanly)
  - Success: Out of combat
  - Failure: Still in field, in danger
- **If grabbed:** Party can make **Combat roll** (opposed) to free them
- **If hit by stray attack:** Party gets -2 ongoing (guilt/distraction); innocent takes damage

**Innocent Safety:**
- If killed: Party killer gets **Guilt** condition, reputation damage
- If protected: Innocent becomes ally/witness, reputation boost

## Lethal vs Non-Lethal

**Lethal Combat:**
- Standard damage, faster kills
- Groups scatter sooner (morale drops faster)
- Innocents are scared and flee faster
- **Consequence:** Reputation damage, witness testimony

**Non-Lethal Combat:**
- Use Brawling skill (grappling, subduing)
- Deals less damage but doesn't kill
- Takes longer—more rounds needed
- Inciters and groups exploit this (grab innocents, target weak PCs)
- **Consequence:** Harder fight, better reputation

## Lyra's Hidden Intervention

**Lyra watches from rooftops. She will NOT stop the fight.**

**If Lia or Lily reaches 0 Condition (imminent death):**
- Lyra shoots an attacker (kills or wounds them)
- **Once per combat only**
- **After Lyra acts:**
  - Protestor/attacker is out of fight (dead or fleeing)
  - Lyra disappears immediately
  - Lia/Lily doesn't see who saved them
  - Party might guess, but never know for sure

**Narrative Purpose:** Shows maternal instinct despite Lyra's darker mission.

## GM Notes: Running This

**How to Track:**
1. Initiative board showing all entities
2. Separate HP/Condition tracker for each Inciter and each Group
3. d6 dice for each Group to roll actions
4. Morale counter (starts at 2-3, ticks down)

**Pacing:**
- Each group's turn is quick: roll d6, describe action (10-15 sec)
- Don't get bogged down in minion math
- Focus on Inciters and Groups; minions are threats in numbers only

**Tone:**
- Chaotic, multiple threats at once
- Groups can be doing different things (one swarms, one grabs hostage, one rallies)
- Pressure is dynamic—no "safe" turns

**Difficulty:**
- 3 Inciters + 2 Groups = challenging for a 6-person party
- Groups break morale quickly if party is effective
- Minions are weak individually but dangerous in numbers
- Adjust by:
  - Reducing Group Condition if party is losing
  - Adding Group C if party is winning too easily
  - Changing morale thresholds
