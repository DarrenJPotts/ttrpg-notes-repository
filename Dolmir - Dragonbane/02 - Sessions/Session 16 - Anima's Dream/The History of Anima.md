#### History 
During the Second Long War, in the Age of Betrayal, the first Umbarian king had fallen and the Umbarian throne lay empty. King Alric I was crowned as the new king behind the walls of the Valehall, the city that rivalled the Umbar itself, and soon after had declared for the reclamation of Umbar turning their backs on the Eldar in pursuit of his fathers kingdom. 

In those desperate days, a band of would-be heroes led by Alexios Valon cut their way across the heartlands. Their dream was audacious: save both Man and Eldar from the Jotun-born, those savage creatures born of Titans, and reunite the fractured kingdoms. Most of the God Trees were lost or unreachable. But Altes Lesym of the Beldam Tribes had, through ancient magic found a way to gain the power needed to succeed in their plan. If they could reach Sehan'nain and perform the rite, they might harness enough of that power to turn the tide of war.

They had made it there with the Jotun-born close behind, creatures born of Titans, savage and dark with great Wyverns. 

Now they stood at the gates of Sehan'nain, Uron in the common tongue. The God Tree of Blessings. The very tree that had granted humanity the gift of magic in an age long forgotten. Alexios's party had one goal: harness the power of a god. To the Eldar who guarded this place, they were here as petitioners, asking for aid. What they actually came to do was something else entirely.
