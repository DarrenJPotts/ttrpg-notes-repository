### Saving the Refugees 
There are a total of four sections within this camp, each might offer a benefit while leaving others may offer downsides. 
Two people can potentially save 2 groups but you cannot do both the Exaltation Army and the Camps. 

#### The Western Wall Section
This section is filled with Mixed Kin, mostly adults who are healthy and untouched by the plague running through the camp. 
**Benefit:** This section has the most organised support, the residents help each other and are their own community. They are currently the safest option. Average skills with a slight buff in HEALING. 
**Downside:** These people are organised, but few of them are capable of offering much in skill as they are in the dusk years.

#### The Fringe Section
This section contains the Blood Cloak Mercenaries, led by Arok of the Stormlands. They are well known mercenaries from Karvosti, of [[House Enoai]]. They are skilled fighters 
**Benefit:** Experienced fighters accustomed to hardship. They offer protection for the Winehouse, escorts for deliveries, and training for its defenders. Strong **combat skills** and **BUSHCRAFT**.
**Downside:** They remain mercenaries with their own leadership and loyalties, not household retainers. Keeping their services requires ongoing pay and independence; bringing them into the Winehouse also risks involving it in their contracts and rivalries.

#### The Centre Section - Chosen 
The most crowded and downtrodden section, filled with displaced families, labourers, and tradespeople who have lost almost everything.
**Benefit:** The widest range of useful skills and the greatest potential for growth. Craftspeople and labourers could help rebuild the Winehouse, expand its operations, and establish new trades. A slight buff in **CRAFTING**.
**Downside:** They bring the largest number of dependants and the greatest immediate needs. Food, housing, tools, and recovery must come before reliable work. Their potential is considerable, but supporting them could strain the Winehouse’s finances for months.

#### The Human Section
This section shelters mostly human refugees around the Exaltation Army’s organised relief efforts.
**Benefit:** Literate organisers, provisioners, and disciplined volunteers. They could improve the Winehouse’s accounts, supply network, and dealings with human institutions. A slight buff in **BARTERING** and **CONNECTIONS**. This is a group that saw [[Rewyn Fosley]] will [[Elenessa Vaeloriane]] and could lead to a path out of the consequence.
**Downside:** Some retain strong loyalties to the Exaltation Army and its pro-human beliefs. Integrating them into a mixed-kin household requires sustained work; tolerating preferential treatment could alienate the Winehouse’s existing allies, while challenging it may cause members to leave.

### The Exaltation Army 
Standing within the Human Section of the camp, the members prepare to hand out more Twilight Milk. Rain drums against the serving awning as refugees queue with empty bowls.

Several refugees have already drunk the milk, and the next cups are being filled.

Stopping further distribution is still possible.

#### If Elenessa arrives
One of the soldiers stops what he is doing. His gaze moves from Elenessa to the leader.
> “That’s her. The woman Rewyn detained.... before he died”

The leader turns towards her.
> “Then perhaps you can explain why you are here and he isn't. Found with the very same 'poison' you warned us of.”

- **If she accuses Rewyn of treachery:** He asks what Rewyn did and whether anyone can corroborate her account.
- **If she refuses to answer or tries to leave:** A soldier moves to block her path. He does not immediately attack.
- **If the party threatens violence:** The soldiers ready weapons, while volunteers usher refugees away.

**Possible compromise:**
> “We set the provisions aside. You remain here while we establish what happened. Nobody needs to draw a weapon.”
