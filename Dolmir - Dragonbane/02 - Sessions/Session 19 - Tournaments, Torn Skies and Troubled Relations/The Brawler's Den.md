### Running the Fight Card
- Each bout starts at 0-0. First to 3 scoring hits, knockout, or surrender wins. No round limit.
- Secretly commit the opponent's move before the player reveals theirs. Describe a tell, not the exact move.
- After every unfinished round, resolve the Crowd Influence Round from the rules. Rotate the spotlight between spectators.
- Ring position, tackles, and flurries are narration, not extra actions. No HP damage or automatic ring-out wins.
- Allow a stretch rest between bouts. All temporary bout effects end when the bout ends.
- Stats below are suggested starting values, not established character stats. Adjust after comparing them with the challenger.

**Tricks and upper hands (house additions):** Each trick below is usable once per bout, declared before rolls and after moves are revealed; nobody changes their committed move. It grants no extra action or scoring hit. Apply normal BOON/BANE handling. Telegraph the tell before move selection and let a fighter or spectator describe a countermeasure; use an appropriate skill only if the outcome is uncertain. Preventing a trick spends its use. An exposed cheat can earn a warning or disqualification from the referee; legitimate edges are not fouls. Surrender always ends the bout immediately.

#### If follows the man: 
Roll me a STEALTH as you attempt to follow this strange man. The crowd funnelling in obscures your vision and movement but eventually you make it outside and see him in the distance.

- This wont have immediate effect but will cause them to glimpse who it is and start the raid on the Brawler's Den
##### Success
