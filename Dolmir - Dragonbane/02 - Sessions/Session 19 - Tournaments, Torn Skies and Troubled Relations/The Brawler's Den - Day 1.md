## GM Notes
Brawler's Den Rules: 

Rounds: There are 5 rounds in total but first to score 3 hits wins or a knockout. 

Stats: 
Attack: Agile Brawling / Brawling 
Defense: Evade / Parry
Saving throw: Con

Actions: 
Evade: Roll evade to dodge incoming attack 

Counter: Roll evade with BANE , if the opponents next move is to attack, then you get a free attack on successful evade.

Attack: Roll attack (Brawling or Agile Brawling)  to hit, on success lower roll wins

Special Move: 
Stun: Roll attack with BANE, on success loser skips next round 
Trip: Roll attack, forgo damage, cancels enemy attack even if attack result is lower. Grants BOON on next round attack
Liver shot: Roll attack with BANE, on success causes Exhausted attacks for the next round (all attacks with BANE) 

Knockouts: Knockouts occur when rolling Dragons on attacks, the loser rolls CON with BANE. 

Victory: First to 3 round wins, or first to knockout opponent

Crowd Influence Round: 
Upon round win, the winner can attempt to influence the crowd or this can be done by spectating party members.

## Fights
### 1. Pella “Anklebiter” Reed

**Halfling — one opponent**  
_A cheerful former drover with taped fists and an enormous grudge against tall people._

> “You can laugh now. Harder with the wind knocked out of you.”

**Fighting style:** Low tackles, body blows and using the ring posts to change direction.

- **Low tackle:** Deals no damage; attempts to knock the challenger prone.
- **Crowd the edge:** When the challenger stands near the boundary, Pella prioritises shoving over punching.
- **Small opening:** After a failed tackle, she is off-balance: give the challenger a **boon on their next attack against her**.

**How to beat her:** Hold the centre and punish failed tackles rather than chasing her.

**Personality twist:** She congratulates a clever winner—and mocks anyone who needed brute force.

**Difficulty:** Forgiving opener. Low HP and modest damage.

## 2. Borun “The Anvil” Kegborn

**Dwarf — one opponent**  
_A retired quarry worker whose beard is tucked into his belt. He barely moves until someone comes within reach._

> “Take your time. I’m paid by the fight.”

**Fighting style:** Guard, absorb, counterpunch.

At the start of each round, clearly describe his stance:

- **Closed guard:** He braces behind his forearms. He focuses on defence and counters an unsuccessful melee attack with a short body blow.
- **Raised hammer:** He draws back for a huge overhead punch. It hits hard, but **a miss leaves his next defence at a bane**.
- **Planted feet:** He is difficult to shove while braced, but cannot pursue someone who backs away.

**How to beat him:** Bait the heavy swing, then attack. Don’t reward him by repeatedly punching into his guard.

**Personality twist:** He respects restraint. If the challenger lets him recover from a dangerous fall, he later returns the favour.

**Difficulty:** Durable, but slow. His big attack should be frightening, not a potential one-hit knockout.

### Round moves ? choose one per round

Announce Borun?s stance before resolving the round.

| Move | Mechanical effect |
|---|---|
| **Hammer Punch ? Attack** | Roll Brawling. A successful hit scores 1; lower successful attack roll wins. |
| **Closed Guard ? Counter** | Roll Evade with **BANE**. If the opponent attacks and Borun successfully evades, he gets a free Brawling attack. |
| **Brace ? Evade** | Roll Evade, describing it as absorbing or deflecting the blow. |
| **Anvil Blow ? Stun** | Roll Brawling with **BANE**. On success, opponent **skips next round**. |

**Special weakness:** A missed Hammer Punch gives Borun?s **next defence BANE**.

## 3. Neris & Dorrik — “Silk and Stone”

**Elf and dwarf — two opponents**  
_An elegant, disgraced fencing tutor and her stocky former debt collector. Their affectionate arguments are almost as rehearsed as their attacks._

> **Neris:** “Try not to embarrass us.”  
> **Dorrik:** “Which of us?”

**Fighting style:** One distracts; the other closes the trap.

- **Neris, the silk:** Quick jabs and retreats. Low damage, low HP.
- **Dorrik, the stone:** Grabs, blocks routes and tries to pin the challenger against a post.
- **The combination:** If Dorrik successfully holds the challenger, Neris gains a **boon on her next strike**. His hold itself deals no damage.
- **Crossed wires:** If either misses while they have the challenger surrounded, the challenger can immediately move out of the trap without an additional roll.

**How to beat them:** Keep both opponents on the same side, break holds and isolate Neris.

**Personality twist:** Neither abandons the other. An injured partner makes the survivor protective, not magically stronger.

**Difficulty:** The action-economy spike. Keep their individual damage and HP noticeably below those of a solo fighter.

## 4. Saelith “The Last Bell”

**Elf — one opponent**  
_A former household bodyguard who carefully removes her employer’s old signet before entering the ring._

> “You’ve proved you can hurt people. Now show me you can fight.”

**Fighting style:** Observes habits, then exploits them.

- **First exchange:** She fights cautiously, studying whether the challenger favours charging, grappling or defensive play.
- **Read the habit:** If the challenger repeats the **same approach on consecutive turns**, she gains a boon to defend against the repeated attempt. Announce the read: _“Her eyes drop to your shoulder before you throw the punch.”_
- **Change the rhythm:** Switching approach removes that advantage.
- **Overcommitted counter:** Once during the fight, she attempts a decisive counter. If it misses, she stumbles toward the edge—an obvious ring-out opportunity.

**How to beat her:** Mix attacks, shoves, feints and movement. Clever descriptions should matter more than guessing a hidden mechanic.

**Personality twist:** She stops instantly at surrender. Whatever the Den has become, she insists that fighting still has rules.

**Difficulty:** Hardest qualifier through skill, not a huge health pool.

| Move | Mechanical effect |

| **Read and Retreat ? Evade** | Roll Evade to dodge the incoming attack. |
| **Decisive Counter ? Counter** | Roll Evade with **BANE**. If the opponent attacks and Saelith successfully evades, she gets a free Agile Brawling attack. **Once per fight.** |
| **Break Their Rhythm ? Trip** | Roll Agile Brawling. On success, score no hit, cancel the enemy attack even if its result is lower, and gain **BOON on next round?s attack**. |
| **Temple Strike ? Stun** | Roll Agile Brawling with **BANE**. On success, opponent **skips next round**. |

**Special trait:** Repeating the same approach on consecutive rounds gives Saelith **BOON to defend against it**. Switching approach removes that advantage.
