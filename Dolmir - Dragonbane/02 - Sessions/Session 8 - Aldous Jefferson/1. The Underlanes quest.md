## The Situation

[[Aldous Jefferson]] went into the Underlanes with the assistance and hiring of The Shamblers criminal group, led by [[Cilius Val]], in an attempt to find a connection between the creatures called [[Crinwin]] and [[Anima Vossel]]. He did not come out. 

[[Pip Giles]] found a box at his door containing two severed fingers after receiving a collection of books promised to [[Anima Vossel]], one of which contains a Primrose flower,  the Primrose Court's signature calling card. 
	The two are unrelated, it would appear [[Aldous Jefferson]] knew someone in [[The Primrose Court]] 

Beneath Valehall runs a network of sewers, catacombs, and underground waterways known as the Underlanes. One connection point is in the lower quarter but there are many throughout [[Valehall]] and contains many secrets within from the days of the [[Second Long War]]. [[Aldous Jefferson]] found this out.

But then he was betrayed by those who the order paid more.

Due to Lia and Anima's killing, a Beast woke.

**The Beast of the Underlanes** is made from the blighted blood of the 5 protestors who died in the ambush with [[Lyra Lovecraft]], and fused together by blight and men of the Shamblers. As long as it lives, the water below Valehall corrodes. Not quickly, but steadily. Another week or two and the wells will fail. 

## Notes
- The Beast was born from hate amplified by blight
- Restraint, stealth, and problem-solving weaken it
- This is not a dungeon that rewards combat prowess
- When a creature is killed, it will be pulled under the water

## Strong Start

**The Entry:**

The party descends into the Underlanes from the Ordo Magi safehouse (via the hidden access point in the merchant's shop where Pip found Jefferson's note). The air shifts immediately.

It is cold. Not the cold of stone—the cold of something wrong.

Water drips from the ceiling, but the drops fall slowly, as if moving through thick air. Where they touch stone, they leave trails of faint corruption: not quite visible, but *felt*. The walls glisten with something that isn't quite wet. The smell is old: decomposition, stagnant water, and something sharper—like burnt metal and spoiled blood.

**Read aloud:**
> *The Underlanes stretch before you in three directions. To the left, a natural cavern system—old, water-carved stone worn smooth by centuries. Straight ahead, carved passages, older still: Eldar work, the stonework precise and angular. To the right, recent masonry—sewer channels, pragmatic, less elegant.*
>
> *In all three directions, the water is wrong. It doesn't flow naturally. It pools and eddies as if something beneath is breathing.*
>
> *And in the distance—from the Eldar passages, you think—something moves. Not quickly. Deliberately. Like a predator that knows you're there but doesn't need to hurry.*

**Prompt:** What do you do?

## Situations (Non-Linear)

The party will encounter these threads. They don't happen in order. The party's choices determine sequence and emphasis.

### Situation 1: The Shambler Congregations

**What's happening:**
- Shamblers move through the Underlanes in loose groups
- They are not aggressive unless directly threatened or the party gets between them and the Beast
- They are *drawn* to sound, violence, and emotional outbursts
- Killing them is *possible* but every kill feeds the Beast (water corrupts faster, Beast grows stronger)

**The choice:**
- **Stealth/avoidance** — harder but starves the Beast
- **Combat** — easier immediate survival, but long-term cost
- **Distraction** — lead them away, create false noise sources, let them scatter

**Signs they're near:**
- Wet, wrong smell intensifies
- Water begins to move unnaturally (eddies, small whirlpools)
- Faint clicking/gurgling sounds
- Cold intensifies
- The shambler's "breath" — a sound like drowning, distant but everywhere

**If combat happens:**
- Shamblers are slow but resilient (reduced damage from weapons)
- They don't die cleanly — when killed, they collapse into a pool of corrupted water that spreads across the stone
- Each shambler killed: **the water corruption visibly worsens** (party can see it spreading, corrupting new stone)
- Each shambler killed: **the Beast moves closer** (next turn it will be encountered sooner)

**Fail-forward:**
- If the party is overwhelmed: shambler herds part around them (Beast called them off) or force them to flee to a higher chamber
- Party is not TPKed by shamblers

### Situation 2: Jefferson's Trail

**What the party can find:**
- His pack, abandoned in a side chamber (Healing herbs, coin, a journal)
- A blood trail leading deeper (toward where the Beast dwells)
- Scraps of Eldar cloth caught on stone (the family was here)
- A final note, written in Jefferson's hand: *"They're ahead. I'm buying time."*

**In the journal:**
- Notes on Eldar movements, safe houses, sympathizer networks
- Growing fear: *"Something wrong in the water. Smells like rot and rage."*
- Final entry (recent): *"The protestors. They came down looking for Eldar. We ran. But they're... changing. The water is changing them. I can hear them screaming but they're still moving."*

**GM note:** Jefferson is already dead. His body is part of the Beast. The party will not find him alive. But the journal grounds the tragedy and explains how the Beast formed.

**Clue value:**
- Confirms Jefferson's purpose (protecting Eldar, not helping the Court)
- Hints at what the Beast is
- Warns the party about the corruption

---

### Situation 3: The Eldar Sanctuary Chamber

**What's there:**
- A large cavern, old Eldar stonework, deliberately hidden
- Supplies: water (not corrupted), food stores, blankets
- **The Eldar family:** 3-4 people, frightened, trapped
- Evidence: recent habitation, barricaded entrance (they locked themselves in)
- **A problem:** The water is starting to seep through cracks in the chamber walls. It moves slowly, but it is *coming*

**The family's state:**
- They speak Eldar primarily, some Common
- Terrified of the Beast (they can hear it in the deep passages)
- Grateful to see non-hostile faces, but cautious
- One of them was wounded by a shambler—healing but infected with early corruption
  - **This is the ticking clock**: if they don't get to the surface soon, the corruption will spread to them
  - Possible to slow/halt with Anima's arts (Field Brew, The Study)
  - Not possible to cure in the Underlanes

**What they know:**
- Jefferson brought them down three days ago
- "A man came down to speak with Jefferson. Hooded. Left something glowing with the protestors' bodies."
- (This was someone deliberately feeding the Beast—likely Lyra or a Primrose agent)
- The water started to change after that
- They've been trapped here, too afraid to move

**The choice:**
- Stay and protect the chamber until corruption passes? (It won't — the Beast keeps spreading)
- Make a run for the surface now? (The family is slow, one is wounded)
- Send the family out while the party stays? (To do what? Fight the Beast?)

**Fail-forward:**
- If the party delays too long: the water breaches the chamber, forcing them to leave quickly
- The family cannot be perfectly saved — but they can be *gotten out* if the party moves

### Situation 4: The Beast Hunts

**When it appears:**
- After significant noise/violence (killing shamblers)
- After a certain amount of time has passed (GM discretion, 4-5 "turns" of activity)
- When the party gets close to its lair (the deepest chamber where the protestors' bodies fused)

**What it is:**
- Not a single creature: five bodies (the protestors) fused into a shambling mass
- No clear "front" — it moves in all directions, joints bending wrong
- Eyes from all five protestors, moving independently, all focused on the party
- The sound it makes: five voices screaming at once, but the screams are synchronized, deliberate
- It is **intelligent** — it was a mob, and the mob's collective hate gave it direction and purpose

**Mechanics:**
- It is slower than the party but relentless
- It does not stop. It does not tire.
- It can fit through smaller passages (it's made of bodies, not solid) but cannot fit through very tight spaces
- It knows the Underlanes intimately (it was born there; it is part of them)

**What it wants:**
- To end all life it perceives (the hate is indiscriminate now)
- Specifically, to kill the Eldar (the original target of the protestors' rage)
- It will pursue the party if they're between it and prey, but it is not *obsessed* with the party

**How to survive:**
- **Chase sequence**: If encountered, this becomes a dynamic chase, not a static fight
- Party can lead it into traps (cave-in, water flow, shambler congregation)
- Party can split up (it can't hunt everything at once)
- Party can use the family as motivation to *keep moving* rather than stand and fight
- **Combat is possible but costly**: every hit causes it to spread corruption further, makes the water worse

**Fail-forward:**
- Party doesn't have to "defeat" the Beast
- If they're caught: they escape (breathless, maybe injured, but alive)
- The Beast moves on to hunt elsewhere in the Underlanes (doesn't immediately re-engage unless threatened again)

## Environmental Complications

### Water Corruption Spread

**Track it with a simple clock (4 segments for now):**
1. Water is wrong but manageable (where party starts)
2. Water begins seeping into upper chambers (party feels time pressure)
3. Water rises in some sections, cutting off routes (forces re-routing)
4. Corruption reaches critical mass (water makes entire sections impassable)

**Triggers:**
- Each shambler killed: +1 segment
- Each round spent in close proximity to the Beast: +1 segment
- Leaving the Underlanes: stops at current level, doesn't advance

**Player-facing effect:**
- Visual: stone begins to crumble where water touches it, color changes (gray-blue corruption)
- Physical: certain passages become flooded, forcing detours
- Mechanical: anyone in corrupted water takes 1 damage per turn (slow poisoning, not an instant threat)
- Narrative: **The party is running out of time. Valehall's water supply depends on them leaving.**

### The Water Itself

**It has properties:**
- Moves slowly but deliberately (as if guided)
- Corrodes stone, metal, and flesh (but slowly, not explosively)
- Does not affect undead/shamblers (they move through it freely)
- Can be temporarily held back with barriers or redirected with clever use of the stone (Engineering/Wits check)

**Possible use:**
- Party could try to use the water as a *weapon* against the Beast
- Channeling water toward it might slow it or force it toward a particular area
- But this accelerates corruption spread (double-edged)

## NPC Threads

### Pip Giles

**If Pip is with the party:**
- He's terrified but stubborn (he brought them here partly out of guilt over Jefferson)
- He can provide context about the city above (what Valehall knows about the Underlanes)
- He knows some Ordo Magi contacts who might be waiting topside (for extraction)
- His fear might spike shambler-detection (loud panic = Beast knows where they are)

### The Eldar Family

**Names & personalities:**
- **Seline** (mother, mid-40s) — speaks halting Common, protective, grateful
- **Theron** (father, early 50s) — mostly silent, watches the party intently
- **Mira** (daughter, ~20) — wounded by shambler bite, feverish from early corruption
- **Kel** (grandfather, ~70) — speaks Common well, remembers the old Underlanes, has knowledge of secret passages

**Their knowledge:**
- Kel knows an old passage to the surface (bypasses some shambler areas) — but it goes deeper first before going up
- They know the Eldar settlements in the Mitland are being targeted (pattern of persecution)
- Jefferson told them the Primrose Court is orchestrating some of the persecution (hired the protestors as muscle)

**Their role:**
- Plot motivation: rescue them, protect them
- Mechanical clock: Mira's corruption worsens each turn (if not treated, she becomes a shambler)
- Narrative weight: their survival feels *important* — not just quest objective but moral anchor

## Secrets & Clues

### Secret 1: Who Fed the Beast?

**The fact:** Someone deliberately brought the protestors' bodies to the Beast and left something with them (described by the Eldar family as "glowing").

**Possibilities:**
- [[Lyra Lovecraft]] (spreading blight, poisoning Valehall's water)
- An agent of the Primrose Court (removing evidence of their hired thugs, destabilizing Valehall)
- Someone working for the Faith (trying to discredit the Eldar/water poisoning)

**How the party learns:**
- The Eldar describe the hooded figure
- Deep in the Underlanes, the party finds remnants of a ritual circle (blood, symbols, the source of the "glow")
- Anima or a Lore-skilled character recognizes it (blight-work, intentional amplification)

**Consequence:** This is not an accident. Someone wants the water poisoned. The party needs to report this.

### Secret 2: Jefferson's Final Act

**The fact:** Jefferson knew he couldn't make it. He deliberately stayed behind to buy the family time.

**Where they learn it:**
- The journal's final entries show his realization
- The Eldar can confirm: "He told us to run. He said he would slow it down."
- The party might find evidence he tried to collapse a passage (cave-in marks, broken stone)

**Emotional weight:** Jefferson is a sympathizer, a good man. His death was a sacrifice, not a failure.

### Secret 3: The Underlanes Connect Deeper

**Hinted at:**
- Kel mentions "the old roads" and "places deeper where even the Eldar don't go"
- There are **old carvings** deeper down (not Eldar, something older)
- The corruption seems to flow from *below*, not from the Beast itself

**Far-off implication:** The tomb connection. But not for this session.

## Fail-Forward Notes

**If the party kills the Beast somehow:**
- The water doesn't stop corrupting immediately (it takes 1d4 days to clear)
- But the active spread halts (no new corruption after the kill)
- The city is saved, but Jefferson is still dead, and the mystery of who fed the Beast remains

**If the party can't save the Eldar family:**
- One or more escape with the party (possible, but difficult without good choices)
- One or more are corrupted/lost (tragic, but not campaign-ending)
- The party brings *testimony* to the surface (the family's story, their knowledge of persecution)
- This feeds into larger threads about the Primrose Court and Faith persecution

**If the party is forced to flee without major victory:**
- They escape with some of the Eldar
- Jefferson is confirmed dead (they find his remains or get confirmation from survivors)
- The water corruption is still active threat (escalates city-wide crisis)
- Party must rally forces to deal with it *from above* (quests, allies, investigation)

**If the party gets trapped:**
- Chase sequence leading to hidden passage (Kel knows it)
- Or: the Beast moves on, party finds temporary shelter, must wait for water to recede
- Or: shambler congregation becomes an unexpected ally (they turn on the Beast if it gets too aggressive)
- Party is never unrecoverable

## Map Notes (Rough Structure)

The Underlanes have three main branches from the entry point:

**LEFT BRANCH: Natural Caverns**
- Largest space, open water (not yet corrupted)
- Shambler congregation lives here (3-4 scattered groups)
- Dead end, but offers space for maneuvering if needed

**CENTER BRANCH: Eldar Passages**
- Carved stone, precise Eldar work
- Tight in places (Beast can't fit through very narrow sections)
- Leads to the Sanctuary Chamber (where Eldar family is hiding)
- Beyond: leads deeper (to the corrupted zone and Beast's lair)

**RIGHT BRANCH: Sewer Passages**
- Recent masonry, water channels
- Designed for water flow (can be manipulated with effort)
- Connects to Valehall's lower quarter (the exit most party members will use)
- Connects to City Guard routes (if they're called in)

**Deep Zone (reached via Center or deliberate descent):**
- Where the protestors fused into the Beast
- Ritual circle (where it was "fed")
- Most corrupted water
- Tomb access (far deeper, not reachable this session)

## Session Pacing

**Assuming 4-5 hours of play:**

- **Hour 1:** Descent, first shambler encounter, establish tone & corruption threat
- **Hour 2:** Navigate to Eldar Sanctuary, meet family, learn Jefferson is dead
- **Hour 3:** The Beast is encountered/close. Dynamic pressure increases. Corruption clock ticks.
- **Hour 4:** Chase sequence or boss encounter OR skillful navigation + escape
- **Hour 5:** Escape sequence, party emerges with or without some of the family

**Adjustable:** If party is moving slowly, compress. If they're exploring deeply, expand—but remind them corruption is spreading.
