## Pip Giles' Investigation

**Day 1 — After the Riot**

[[Pip Giles]] is frantic. His master [[Aldous Jefferson]] has been missing for days. The riot in the streets doesn't help—news spreads quickly that Valeguard is arresting people, and Pip fears the worst.

A contact approaches him quietly—someone from the Ordo Magi, or someone who knows them. The message is simple and urgent:

**"Jefferson went into the Underlight. Near the Shambles. He hasn't come back."**

Pip has a choice: wait for the party to return from arrest, or investigate alone.

---

## If Pip Investigates Alone (Likely)

**The Underlight Shop**

The shop is tucked into the Lower Quarter, not far from the Shambles Tavern. It's a cramped, cluttered place:
- Shelves packed with curiosities: strange herbs, bones, old books, oddities
- The air smells of dampness, incense, and something *wrong* — like spoiled fruit
- The proprietor is [[Cilius Val]]c , a thin man with sharp eyes and sharper teeth

**Cilus' Demeanor**

When Pip enters, Cilus is immediately hostile:

"Looking for something specific, boy? Or are you just browsing?"

If Pip asks about Jefferson:
- Cilus goes very still. His expression hardens.
- "I don't know any Jefferson."
- If Pip presses: "Get out. Now. And don't come back asking questions about people who don't exist."

If Pip doesn't back down:
- Cilus' hand drifts under the counter. A weapon? A bell to summon guards?
- "You seem like a smart boy. So I'm going to tell you once: some questions get people killed. Jefferson asked too many. You should be smarter."
- A beat of silence.
- "But if you want to know what really happened to him... there's work. Honest work. I could use someone reliable in the Underlanes. Moving packages. No questions. Good coin."

**What Cilus Knows:**
- Jefferson went into the Underlanes three days ago
- He was helping move Eldar refugees to safe passages
- Something went wrong down there
- Cilus doesn't know what, or he's not saying
- Cilus is now offering Pip a job—either because he respects Pip, or because he wants to keep him quiet/contained

---

## The Underlanes (The Descent)

The cellar beneath the Underlight is small, unremarkable. A trapdoor in the floor. Stone steps leading down into darkness.

**If Pip goes alone:**
He takes a lantern. He descends.

**What he finds on the way down:**

1. **The first chamber** — An old storage room, converted into a safe passage. Signs of recent habitation: blankets, food scraps, Eldar inscriptions on the walls in old script.

2. **The main passage** — The Underlanes proper. Catacombs, carved centuries ago. Waterways running through them. It's vast and labyrinthine.

3. **Signs of disturbance** — Scattered belongings. A torn cloak. Blood on the stone (fresh? Three days old? Hard to tell).

4. **A side passage blocked** — Rocks and rubble, as if intentionally collapsed. Deliberately? Or did something collapse it?

5. **Deeper paths** — The Underlanes fork into multiple directions. Without a map or guide, it's easy to get lost.

---

## What Happens to Pip

**Option 1: He finds Jefferson (barely)**

Pip discovers [[Aldous Jefferson]] trapped in a collapsed section. Jefferson is:
- Injured (broken leg, bleeding from a head wound)
- Weakly conscious
- Calling out for help
- Has been there for days without food or water

But there's a problem: the passage is unstable. Bringing him out would require:
- Multiple people
- Careful work to avoid another collapse
- Possibly divine or magical intervention

Pip *cannot* save Jefferson alone. But he can get him water, assess the situation, and escape to get help.

**Option 2: He encounters something in the Underlanes**

Pip hears something moving. Echoes that don't match his footsteps. A shape in the darkness.

He doesn't see it clearly, but he *feels* it. Something large. Something that shouldn't be there.

He runs.

He escapes back to the surface, badly shaken. No sign of Jefferson.

**Option 3: He finds a dead end (or dead bodies)**

Pip reaches a chamber deep in the Underlanes. Inside:
- The bodies of Eldar refugees (killed? Or died of hunger/exposure?)
- Signs of violence or struggle
- A journal or ledger (Anima's? Someone's records?)
- No Jefferson

## Pip's Disappearance / The Note

**Whether Pip goes alone or investigates further, he vanishes.**

The party returns from arrest to find:

**At Pip's home (wherever he's staying):**
- The door is ajar
- A note on the table, written in Pip's hurried hand:

> "Gone to find Master Jefferson. The Underlight, near the Shambles. If I don't return in two days, tell the Valeguard. Tell them to search the Underlanes below. The entrance is in the cellar.
>
> I'm sorry. He's out there somewhere.
>
> — Pip"

**A box is left beside the note:**
- Small, wooden, sealed with wax
- Inside:
  - **Two severed fingers** (fresh, or recently preserved)
  - **A primrose flower** (dried, but still intact)
  - **A ledger or journal** (old, from the Second Age, with handwriting Anima will recognize as hers or someone she knew — a record from a conclave that no longer exists)
  - **A note in a different hand:**

> "For the green-eyed girl. These belonged to someone who asked too many questions. The Primrose doesn't forget. Tell her: the conclave is remembered, and the price is still being paid."

---

## Implications

**What the party learns from the box:**

- Someone knows about Anima's past
- Someone knows about her connections to an old conclave
- The Primrose Court is involved or aware of Anima
- Pip is likely still alive (why send the box otherwise?)
- The Underlanes are dangerous

**What they can investigate:**

- Go to the Underlight themselves and confront Cilus Val
- Enter the Underlanes to search for Pip and Jefferson
- Track down who sent the box (the finger-cutter)
- Pursue leads on the conclave mentioned in Anima's journal

**Timing:**

Pip left his note immediately after discovering something. If the party returns from arrest the next morning, Pip has been missing for ~18-24 hours. The "two days" timeline means they have maybe a day or so before Pip either escapes on his own or something worse happens.

---

## [[Cilus Val]] (NPC Notes)

- Operates the Underlight as a front for something deeper
- Connected to the Underlanes and possibly the Primrose Court
- Knows more than he's saying about Jefferson's disappearance
- May have sent Pip down intentionally, or warned him deliberately to see what he'd do
- Offers work (which could be a trap, or could be legitimate)
- His "job offer" to Pip might be the party's way to get access to the Underlanes

**What he wants:**
- To stay in control of the Underlanes entrance
- To keep the party from digging too deep
- Possibly to recruit them

**What he won't say:**
- What exactly happened to Jefferson
- What he's actually moving through the Underlanes
- Why the Primrose Court is interested in Pip/Anima
- What he knows about the conclave
